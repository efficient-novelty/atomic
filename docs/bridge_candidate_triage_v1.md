verdict rests on paraphrase.

**G1 — no value quantity legislates at a guarded stage.** From the Selective

> No bar, efficiency comparison, parsimony order, or deterministic presentation

proposition is withdrawn as *false under the adopted law*: "guarded acceptance
never consults ρ", and the audited worth column is non-monotone with accepted

standing warning that it "must not be confused with the false stronger claim
that PEN always selects the initial completion of a weaker requirement fixed in
advance."

**G5 — verdict-blindness and the register fence.** F-XF2 (no premise defined
with the target in view), F-XF6 (realization-quotient criterion), the hazard
fence (no structural-ledger quantity enters a physical prediction without a
separate bridge certificate), and the XF-0 consequence that no item may lean on
a register ↔ degrees-of-freedom correspondence.

## 2. The dominant finding: one error, five times

Items 2.3, 3.2, 5.2, 5.3 — and the *motivation* offered for 3.2 — are the same
move wearing four costumes: **select the candidate that minimizes (or maximizes)
a quantity.** "Minimizes the total sealed descriptive cost." "Public-record
capacity is minimized by a single doublet." "Maximizes the observer-indexed
entropy capacity." "The same ρ = ν/κ continues to price material seals."

G1 names three of these forms explicitly — bar, efficiency comparison, parsimony
order — and rules them out of guarded acceptance. G3 records that the theory
already tried the efficiency-gradient picture and *retired it as false*, not as
unproven. So this is not a technicality to be argued around; it is the one move
the Selective Law was revised to forbid.

The brainstorm's framing device — "philosophically continuous with the
framework's own commitments" — is what makes this hard to see. Efficiency
pressure *was* one of those commitments. It is not one any more.

**The repair is uniform, and the theory already contains the worked example.**
`docs/tier_c_free_sealing_2_law_conjecture.md` §5 states it exactly:

> What P1 buys is therefore not the value `C⁵` but the *right to assert it*: it
> converts a forbidden minimization into a universal property.

Every one of the four illegal items has a candidate universal-property
reformulation. Whether the reformulation is *true* is then an ordinary
mathematical question, and the answer may be no — but at least it is a question
the law permits asking.

| illegal form | lawful reformulation to attempt |
|---|---|
| minimal-root hierarchy | a **sealing-order** condition: each generation seals outward from the already-sealed pair, so a root interior to `(√m_e, √m_μ)` is not a lawful extension. Order-theoretic, not a magnitude comparison. See §4. |
| minimal Higgs carrier | the electroweak API's carriers have an initial exact realization; a second doublet is an **unforced generator**, not an expensive one |
| observer maximality | drop entirely — it also violates the two-role ontology |
| ρ across the ignition seam | drop as a *law-level* claim; it may survive as engine-register **diagnostic testimony**, which is where the two-register discipline already puts it |

## 3. Group 1 — the operational-quantum package

**The four candidates are not new bridges. They are the four theorem targets
already stated in the tex** under *Genesis operational-completion package*:
Genesis-to-Jordan, natural monoidal phase, no fundamental mixing, public
descent. The brainstorm's 1.1/1.4/1.3/1.2 are these four, in a different order.

That is not a criticism of the brainstorm — it is a *status correction*, and it
changes the recommendation. The brainstorm proposes adopting them **as bridges**.
The tex commits to **deriving** them, and closes the section with:

> Failure to derive any target from the Genesis candidate competition
> identifies that target as genuine bridge content rather than permitting it to
> be silently inserted into Step 14.

Adopting all four as premises is precisely the silent insertion that sentence
forbids. It would convert the open reconstruction conjecture into a definition.
That move is *available* — the theory permits declared bridges — but it must be
counted as four premises, and it would mean the reconstruction theorem is not
proved but assumed. The brainstorm's own recommendation to "begin with the four
operational-quantum bridges" therefore inverts the theory's stated priority.

**A structural observation worth recording.** The tex names exactly four
surviving countermodels and, on inspection, each of the four targets kills
exactly one:

| countermodel | why it survives now | which target kills it |
|---|---|---|
| finite stochastic / classical simplices | purification fails | 3 — free environment completion |
| real quantum | local tomography fails (two-rebit invisible direction) | 4 — public descent |
| quaternionic fragments | noncentral phase | 2 — natural monoidal phase |
| rational polyhedral GPT | cone not homogeneous self-dual | 1 — Jordan |

The bijection cuts **against** treating the package as a discovery. A premise
set calibrated exactly to the known countermodels has no margin against an
unknown fifth, and the sufficiency direction of the conjecture is precisely the
claim that no fifth exists. Killing four named models is the weakest possible
evidence for that claim.

**Minimality (criterion 2) — the four are not independent.** Real quantum theory
is already excluded by local tomography alone, per the tex's own countermodel
paragraph. So if public descent is derived, the central-phase bridge is not
needed *for the real case* and its remaining job is quaternionic exclusion
alone. Any honest statement of the package should say which target is doing
which work, rather than presenting four coordinate premises.

**Verdict-blindness (criterion 1).** 1.1 as worded — "complex rather than real
or quaternionic" — states the measured answer in the premise. The tex's version
of the same target does not: it demands "a faithful primitive central `U(1)`
action … natural under all processes and balanced under composition", and lets
complexity fall out. Keep the tex's wording. This distinction is exactly what
F-XF2 protects.

**Depth-2 consistency (criterion 3).** A derivation of central phase from the
width-two structure and `d_obl = 2` is permitted — that is the same allowance
XF-A already carries — but the registered caution applies verbatim: it may rest
only on width-2 and `d_obl = 2`, **never** on the engine-register d²/r² counting
laws, and never on a register ↔ degrees-of-freedom correspondence (XF-0
consequence). Any argument of the shape "phase is complex because the ledger is
width two" must show which of those two it is using.

**The one genuinely strong item is 1.2.** "Every mixed public state admits a
free (initial) pure dilation" is not a new primitive — it is free sealing
(relative initiality, G4) applied to the environment side of the public record.
It has the same logical shape the tier-C derivation just executed successfully:
identify the API, define the category of exact realizations *under* it, exhibit
the initial object. That is a transferable proof template, and purification is
the natural next target for it. **This is the recommendation §6 makes.**

## 4. Group 2 — Koide

**2.1 is already an axiom in the tex** (*Rank-blind central actualization*), and
is registered as XF-C1. It is not a candidate. Deriving it — the brainstorm's
actual proposal, extending the central-phase privilege to the radial sector —
would be an upgrade from axiom to theorem, which is worth doing, but must be
stated as such. Note the axiom's own closing line: "This is new bridge content.
It is not supplied by `S_N` or univalence, because `U` and `W` have unequal
dimensions and are not equivalent representations." Any derivation must defeat
that sentence, which is the whole content of the underdetermination no-go.

**2.2 = XF-C3.** Admissible and correctly aimed. Two constraints bind hard:

1. **F-XF2, sharpened by amendment D3**: "No mass-seal functional or
   jurisdiction theorem may be defined with the tau target in view." A
   uniqueness-up-to-free-sealing claim is legal (universal property, not value
   quantity), but the *construction* must be blind.
2. **The physics is genuinely hard, and the tex says so**: a charged stable
   field in zero-temperature QED has an infraparticle branch point, not a simple
   pole, and unstable fermions need a gauge-independent complex-pole treatment.
   "QED-dressed" has to become an actual dressing prescription
   (Faddeev–Kulish-class, or an equivalent infrared-safe observable) before
   uniqueness is even a well-posed question. This is not a paragraph of work.

**2.3 is illegal as stated** — see §2. It is also the item the amendment most
directly warned about: "until they exist blind, Koide is bridge research, not a
precision prediction."

**A lawful line of attack on XF-C4 worth trying.** The two roots are
`m₃⁺ = 1776.969` MeV and `m₃⁻ = 3.3174` MeV; the second lies strictly between
`m_e` and `m_μ`. Sequential sealing is already load-bearing elsewhere in the
theory — it is exactly what forces `Y_{ν^c} = 0` and hence `q_ν = 0`, and the
tex is explicit that the standard traces are "exact relative to sequential
sealing, not consequences of the simultaneous system." So ask: **does the
sealing order that produced `q_ν = 0` also forbid a third-generation seal
interior to the already-sealed pair?** If yes, XF-C4 becomes an order-theoretic
condition — structural, target-blind, and native to the theory — rather than a
magnitude preference.

Three cautions on that line, all disqualifying if unmet:

- It must be argued from the sealing order *as already frozen*, not from a new
  order chosen because it gives the high root. That is the F-XF2 trap in its
  purest form.
- The Koide no-go's proofsketch treats the spectrum as the unordered quotient
  `ℝ^N/S_N`. An outward-sealing condition reintroduces an order, so it must be
  shown to be an order on *seals* (acts) and not on *masses* (values) — or it
  is G1 all over again in disguise.
- If it fails, the honest outcome is **not** a fallback to minimality. Per G2 it
  is a certified two-branch cone in the charged-lepton sector, exactly parallel
  to the Step-4 four-branch cone. A published cone is a Two-Law result; a
  smuggled minimum is a campaign void.

## 5. Groups 3–5, briefly

### 3.1 — this is closed; the tex has since been brought up to date

> **Status update, same day.** The three edits listed at the end of this
> subsection were applied to `the_answer_is_two_theory_short.tex` after this
> triage was written. The section below is retained as the finding that
> prompted them. `docs/tier_c_free_sealing_2_law_conjecture.md` still records
> P1 as open and is now stale on that point; per repo convention it has not
> been edited in place.


The brainstorm calls this "the single highest-leverage formal item, because it
would convert the Hopf census from 'declared uniqueness' into a theorem." That
was true when the audit was written. It is no longer the state of the tree:

- `docs/tier_c_free_sealing_2_law_conjecture.md` §4.3 proves
  `(V₃ ⊕ V₂, can₃, can₂)` is initial in the category of exact realizations,
  hence `C⁵`, hence `n★ = 4` — **given premise P1** (the API is carrier-bearing).
- P1 was the note's one open item. **P1 is now closed in the tex**: the
  uncommitted working copy contains *Lemma (Carrier retention in the local
  alphabet API)* with a proof, in the exact form §5 of the note asked for
  ("the carrier travels with the group into the API").

So the chain is complete in the tree and incomplete only on the page. Three
edits finish it:

1. Add the initiality theorem (note §4.3) to the gauge section, immediately
   after the carrier-retention lemma it depends on.
2. Rewrite census clause (iii) from a declared clause to a corollary of that
   theorem.
3. **Correct the fallback sentence.** The *Open items* remark currently reads:
   "if it cannot [be derived], the census returns an `argmin` and a monotone
   cost `κ(H_n)` must be justified independently." Per §1 of the note that
   fallback is forbidden by G1 — a monotone cost is a value quantity
   legislating at a guarded stage. The lawful trichotomy is: derived ⇒ theorem;
   not derived ⇒ certified cone; cost ⇒ forbidden. The abstract bullet calling
   this "the sharpest open item this synthesis adds" needs the same treatment.

Until (3) is done the tex asserts, in its own voice, a fallback its own law
forbids. That is the highest-value correction on this list and it is editorial,
not research.

### 3.2 — illegal as stated; reformulate as an unforced generator

See §2. Note the reformulation is not obviously true: a second doublet is not
*forced*, but neither is it forbidden by initiality unless the electroweak API's
carrier set is pinned as tightly as the alphabet's was. That pinning is the
work. XF-B2 is already registered as "conditional; bridge premise open", so
nothing downstream moves until it lands.

### 3.3 — already a build, and the stated form is probably too strong

This is XF-B5 in the amendment: a finite census over the four candidate
quotients `Γ ⊆ ℤ₆` by line/bundle probes, frozen before comparison. The
brainstorm's form — "the *unique* quotient such that every line operator local
with respect to the sealed point-field action remains local" — asserts
uniqueness that the standard theory does not supply: choosing a global form is
choosing a maximal mutually-local set of line operators, and in general several
maximal isotropic choices exist. That non-uniqueness is *why* there are four
candidates. Expect the census to return a cone over `Γ` unless a further
structural condition intervenes; the tex's own remark is appropriately hedged
("geometrically distinguished", not "selected"). Do not upgrade that hedge
without the probe policy.

### 4.1 — admissible, but de-circularize it

The reparameterization no-go stands: models agreeing on the Constitutive Law,
PEN, and the structural trace can disagree on `Q_p` and on clock calibration.
The candidate as worded — unique "up to monotone reparameterization fixed by the
observer's thermal or atomic standards" — fixes the clock by assuming a clock.

The non-circular version is already half-built. The seven-cell thermal
repetition observer is fully explicit, with `n_p = 4.887399`, `n_g = 3.894753`,
`K_cap = 3.307335` bits and a derived Hamming-weight sufficient statistic. Ask
whether *that* observer's sealed thermal record fixes a metric time up to affine
reparameterization. If it does, the bridge is a model-level theorem of the same
grade as the existing observer construction — which the tex is careful to say is
not universal. If it does not, the no-go is sharper than currently stated, which
is also publishable.

### 4.2 — hazard, hold

Amendment D2 moved the φ-cosmology items to historical/exploratory and requires
"a registered theorem-grade clock-bridge certificate" for reactivation. This
candidate would reach Λ *through* a clock bridge that does not yet exist, and it
picks one of the two cosmological readings that ledger event 008 says "remain
competing alternatives and must not be combined." Blocked behind 4.1 on both
counts.

### 5.1 — no-op

"No unforced generators or equations" is already the verbatim text of free
sealing. Promoting it to an explicit axiom removes no freedom, because the
freedom was never in that clause — it is in **which API is selected**. The
tier-C derivation is the demonstration: the entire content was P1, the
identification of the API as carrier-bearing, and the no-unforced-generator
clause did no work until that identification was in hand. Restating the clause
would not have produced `n★ = 4`; identifying the API did.

### 5.2 — illegal twice over

A maximality principle is value legislation (G1). Worse, it is a *third
selective role*: a criterion that picks among theories all of which discharge
their obligations. The theory's title claim is that two roles suffice. And the
stated motivation — "would favor complex Hilbert-space structure over
lower-capacity alternatives" — reads the verdict back into the premise, which is
F-XF2.

### 5.3 — illegal four times over

G1 (efficiency comparison as selector), G3 (the ratchet is retired *as false*),
G5 (a ledger quantity entering physical claims without a bridge certificate —
the automated fence in amendment F would reject it), and the XF-0 consequence
(no item may lean on a register ↔ degrees-of-freedom correspondence, which is
what "one operator across the ignition seam" asserts). The engine-register ρ
trajectory remains sealed testimony; that is its whole permitted role.

## 6. Recommendation

The brainstorm's stated strategy — start with the four operational-quantum
bridges, treat geometric free sealing as the highest-leverage formal item — is
inverted relative to the current state of the record. Suggested order:

1. **Finish 3.1 on the page** (editorial, hours). The mathematics is done; the
   tex still calls it open and still offers a forbidden fallback.
2. **Attempt 1.2, purification, by the tier-C template** (research, tractable).
   It is the only group-1 item that is a specialization of an existing principle
   rather than a new premise, and the template is now proven to work.
3. **Attempt the sealing-order route to XF-C4** (research, verdict-blind, may
   fail into a publishable cone).
4. **Repair the three κ-comparison sites in the tex** (§2a — editorial, and it
   strengthens the claims).
5. **Hold 4.2 and 5.2/5.3.** Drop 5.1 as a no-op.

One meta-point, offered because the record earned it. The XF-0b2 finding —
that the certified register turned out to be a hand-authored literal that no
search in the repository ever found — is the reason this triage quotes the law
verbatim instead of paraphrasing it. Four of these fourteen candidates read as
plausible continuations of the framework and are forbidden by a single sentence
of its own Selective Law. Plausibility at the level of philosophical continuity
is not evidence of admissibility, and this list is a good demonstration of the
gap.

## 7. What would overturn this triage

- **§2 is wrong** if guarded-stage acceptance can be shown not to govern the
  stages at which gauge, Higgs, and hierarchy selection occur — i.e. if those
  are open-band acts. Then G1 does not bind. (Note the open band is foreclosed
  by ρ > 4, so this rescues nothing, but it would change the reason.)
- **§3's bijection claim is wrong** if any listed countermodel is excluded by a
  target other than the one paired with it, or by more than one.
- **§5's 3.1 status is wrong** if the carrier-retention lemma in the working
  copy does not in fact discharge premise P1 as the tier-C note states it — the
  lemma must supply the carriers *as API content*, not merely assert that
  carriers exist.
- **§4's sealing-order suggestion is wrong** if the frozen sealing order is
  silent on interior seals, or if making it speak requires choosing the order
  after seeing the roots.
