## 2. Ordinary carrier/action substrate: common notation

The substrate reserved for `ordinary-dependent-envelope-v1` is universally

`pen-kernel` signature `Sigma`. Every Level-II instance pins and retains one
exact normalized signature, the kernel/normalizer/configuration identities,
and the exact JG2b2b0, JG2b2b1, and A1 parents. The substrate definition itself
contains no history-specific signature. For a future factual pair `(H,o)`,

born strictly before `Birth(o)` and retains every current-birth declaration
byte-exactly. The full historical successor remains separate provenance
evidence and is never the evaluator through which an older opaque global can
unfold.

Write:

- `Tel_Sigma(Gamma)` for an arbitrary finite oldest-first dependent telescope
  over `Gamma`;
- `Gamma.A` for dependent-context concatenation;
- `Sec_Sigma(Gamma,A)` for a complete oldest-first dependent section of `A`
  under `Gamma`;
- `theta : Delta -> Gamma` for a context morphism in the frozen JG2b2b1
  orientation, whose images live in `Delta` and replace the declarations of
  `Gamma`;
- `theta^A : Delta.A[theta] -> Gamma.A` for the iterated, internally derived
  binder lift; and
- `nf` for the current kernel's beta, permitted-global, and projection
  normalization.

Context and telescope formation are checked field by field. Telescope
equality is equal length followed by byte equality of the separately
formation-checked normalized field types in their dependent prefixes; it does
not require a `DefinitionallyEqual` judgment between types at a
caller-supplied universe. Equality between terms is a kernel
`DefinitionallyEqual` judgment at an internally derived type. There is
no eta rule, UIP, proof irrelevance, path equality, univalence, or extra
equation.

Structural identity is canonical encoding of the normalized Level-I value
and all of its indices. It is not interchangeable with typed equality.

## 3. The nine ordinary carriers

### 3.1 `PublicContext[Sigma,Gamma]`

The base category has every finite dependent context `Gamma` with
`Sigma |- Gamma ctx` as an object and the section-3.4 substitutions as its
morphisms. Over each base object, `PublicContext[Sigma,Gamma]` is the singleton
displayed fibre `{Here(Gamma)}`. Normalization is sequential kernel
normalization; typed equality is equality of verified normalized contexts. A
morphism `theta : Delta -> Gamma` has the total displayed action
`theta*(Here(Gamma)) = Here(Delta)`. Identity and composition subjects are the
literal endpoint equations.

Level II retains the raw context, a derived context-formation sentinel, the
normalized context, the full signature/configuration binding, and replay
evidence.

### 3.2 `PublicInterface[Sigma,Gamma]`

A value is an arbitrary finite telescope `A in Tel_Sigma(Gamma)`. The empty
telescope is allowed; any constructor-specific nonemptiness premise belongs
to A3-O. Formation, normalization, and equality are the prefix-wise judgments
defined above. The sort is contravariant in `Gamma`:

```text
theta*: PublicInterface[Gamma] -> PublicInterface[Delta]
theta*(A) = A[theta].
```

Level II retains `Gamma`, raw and normalized `A`, every internally derived
prefix `TypeFormation` judgment, and its replay.

### 3.3 `InterfaceFamily[Sigma,Gamma,A]`

For `A in PublicInterface[Gamma]`, a value is an arbitrary finite result
telescope `B in Tel_Sigma(Gamma.A)`. The complete value is `(Gamma,A,B)`; it
is not a `Pi`, a `Sigma`, or a caller classification tag. Formation,
normalization, and equality are prefix-wise. Its joint dependent action is

```text
theta*(Gamma,A,B) = (Delta, A[theta], B[theta^A]).
```

Level II retains the complete `Gamma/A/B` prefix judgments and all particular
lift and reindexing receipts. A3-O may recognize a declaration footprint as one
such family but may not change this carrier.

### 3.4 `Substitution[Sigma,Delta,Gamma]`

A value is exactly a JG2b2b1-oriented typed simultaneous substitution: one
image for every declaration of `Gamma`, in oldest-codomain-first order, with
the image type derived after the preceding images. Equality fixes both
endpoints and is pointwise kernel definitional equality at those internally
derived image types. Normalization is the independently replayed normalized
proposal.

The carrier is two-sided:

```text
rho : Xi -> Delta       gives precomposition  theta . rho : Xi -> Gamma
sigma : Gamma -> Omega  gives postcomposition sigma . theta : Delta -> Omega.
```

It is contravariant in the domain endpoint and covariant in the codomain
endpoint. Level II is the complete two-batch particular-substitution object,
not a digest. A2-O states no generic identity, associativity, or whiskering fact.

### 3.5 `ComparisonWitness[Sigma,Gamma,A,B]`

For `A,B in PublicInterface[Gamma]`, the only route tag is the internally
fixed `KernelNormalizedTypeEquality` route. A value retains both exact
endpoints, every prefix `TypeFormation` replay for both telescopes, and the
fieldwise comparison of the resulting normalized type bytes. It exists
exactly when the two telescopes have equal length and those separately checked
dependent normal forms agree. It does not synthesize a universe level or ask
the kernel to check a type-level `DefinitionallyEqual` judgment. Normalization
replaces the record by its replayed canonical endpoints and evidence. Equality
is canonical witness identity after endpoint normalization.

Reindexing along `theta` independently replays the witness between `A[theta]`
and `B[theta]`. This value is not a path, equivalence, transport square, or
cubical binary comparison.

### 3.6 `SealedPublicGrammar[Sigma,Gamma]`

A value is `G=(E,O)`:

- `E in Tel_Sigma(Gamma)` is the finite normalized opaque-export telescope;
  and
- `O` is a finite dependency-preserving ordered list of public operation
  signatures. Operation `k` is exactly a pair `(P_k,R_k)` with
  `P_k in Tel_Sigma(Gamma.E)` and
  `R_k in Tel_Sigma(Gamma.E.P_k)`.

The operation anchor is the derived ordinal `k`. There are no operation
bodies, complete histories, stage labels, held-out facts, arbitrary names,
fresh globals, or authority bits. Opacity is the absence of private bodies,
not replacement of the public type by a digest.

Normalization preserves all dependent field and operation order. Equality is
componentwise normalized dependent equality with the same ordinals. For
`theta : Delta -> Gamma`, the action uses the lift through `E` and then each
`P_k` to reindex `E`, `P_k`, and `R_k`. Level II retains the full record and
all derived formation judgments.

### 3.7 `DemandScheme[Sigma,Gamma,G]`

For `G=(E,O)`, a value is `S=(X,ports,contracts)`:

1. `X in Tel_Sigma(Gamma.E)` is the complete positive trigger and
   trigger-evidence telescope, including any external parameters. A section of
   `X` therefore proves activation rather than merely choosing parameters.
2. `ports` is a finite dependency-ordered list. Before port `j`, let
   `C_j = Gamma.E.X.Q_0...Q_(j-1)`. The port retains an internally bounded
   operation anchor `k` and a complete typed section of `P_k` weakened into
   `C_j`. Its demanded output `Q_j` is derived by substituting that section
   into `R_k`; it is never caller supplied.
3. `contracts` is a finite canonical set of triples `(left,right,type)` in
   the final context `Gamma.E.X.Q_0...Q_m`. Scheme formation checks both
   endpoints at the stated internally checked type but does not require them
   to be equal.

Every demanded output and dependency edge is recomputed. Normalization keeps
the port order and anchors, normalizes all telescopes, arguments, and terms,
and sorts/deduplicates only the independent contract set. Reindexing proceeds
through `E`, `X`, and the already derived outputs by iterated lift. Level II
retains the entire scheme and every formation/typing receipt. No negative
demand or underivability bit occurs in this carrier.

### 3.8 `LiveDemand[Sigma,Gamma,G,S,a]`

The activation index `a` is a complete typed section of `X` in `Gamma.E`,
where `X` includes the scheme's complete positive trigger and trigger-evidence
telescope. This is positive activation data, not an absence test. The carrier contains
the unique specialization `L` obtained by substituting `a` through every port
in dependency order: its demanded telescope over `Gamma.E`, inherited exact
operation anchors, and specialized contracts.

Equality and normalization retain the exact activation and compare the
deterministically rebuilt normal form. Reindexing acts on `G`, `S`, and `a`
and then rebuilds and replays `L`. Level II retains `a`, every substitution
and lift receipt, and all specialized judgments.

`Derivable(witness)`, `Underivable(complete certificate)`, and `Unknown` are
separate factual dispositions. No `Underivable` or `Unknown` disposition is a
field of `LiveDemand` or is required to reindex.

### 3.9 `Discharge[Sigma,Gamma,G,S,L]`

A value is one complete dependent section `d` of `L`'s demanded telescope in
`Gamma.E`. It covers every port exactly once; shared dependencies are shared
by that one section. Formation additionally substitutes `d` into every
specialized contract and derives the resulting kernel
`DefinitionallyEqual` judgment.

Equality is pointwise typed definitional equality between sections with
identical indices. Normalization retains the normalized section and equality
receipts. Reindexing sends `d` along the lift through `E`, rebuilds the
reindexed demand, and independently rechecks all typing and equality
obligations. Level II retains all indices, the raw section, internally derived
types and contracts, normalized output, and aggregate replay evidence. A
Level-I value is only proposed data. Only a fully checked Level-II discharge
may later be carried by `Derivable(witness)`; A2-O mints no such factual
disposition. Nonexistence is never certified by failure to construct a value.
