## Rule: `future-hole-hypothesis-definition-v1`

1. **The definition.** A future-directed demand scheme is an *open
   judgment* Γ ⊢ scheme, where each hole is an ambient hypothesis with a
   **declared, Bₙ-formable motive** — the world knows the type of what it
   owes before the thing exists. Motives are declared at scheme
   registration (wrapper discipline: enumerated, never inferred, never
   selected by verdict).
2. **Actions on holes.** The unary and structural actions of sealed
   operators on holes are the adopted transparent-former closure and
   contextual-internality rules acting on hypotheses — the proven
   17-obligation matrix, applied in the demand register. No new action
   semantics is introduced; a hole action outside the adopted closure
   fails closed as a named gap.
3. **Discharge.** A scheme is discharged by a sealed filler exactly when
   the hypothetical derivation specializes at that filler and replays
   under the closed rules — clause 4′ instantiation coherence, verbatim.
   D-membership of a scheme is hypothetical derivability, which D has
   been operationally since the contextual rule was adopted.
4. **Charging.** Holes are hypotheses: zero κ, zero ν, no anchors (the
   endpoint-API law, unchanged). Fillers are sealed clauses and are
   charged as such. Nothing about a hole or its filling mints credit.
5. **Stage successors (R-T2's instrument).** The typed successor of a
   stage under a chosen winner is the next window's scheme set with
   holes typed against that winner's sealed content. Confluence of tied
   winners is equivalence of their successor scheme sets under the
   certified family quotient and transport — comparable by machinery
   that now exists.
