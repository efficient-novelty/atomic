# The Schema Calculus: Deriving the Evaluator's Class Formulas from First Principles

**Status:** derivation audit, first complete pass (2026-07-02).
**Purpose:** close, or precisely bound, the evaluator's degrees-of-freedom problem — the
gap between the philosophical definitions of ν and κ and the class-dependent closed-form
formulas in `crates/pen-eval/src/nu.rs`.
**Method:** treat the philosophical definition as primary, reconstruct each formula from
it, and label every component **FORCED** (a theorem given the definitions),
**DERIVABLE** (a theorem modulo a stated lemma, with proof strategy),
**GAUGE** (a convention that provably cannot affect selection), or
**OPEN** (a genuine degree of freedom requiring the robustness program of §6).

**Machine-check clarification (2026-07-18):** the proposed L1/L2 finite bases
are now mechanically checked, but their identification with actual schema
classes is not. Here, "finite layer checked" means arithmetic and finite
partitioning conditional on a semantic realization; it does not mean a
cubical or fibration proof.

---

## 0. The Problem, Stated Fairly

The philosophical definition is one sentence: ν(X|B) = |L(B∪{X})| − |L(B)|, the number
of new derivation schemas, measured at coherence depth 2; κ = the number of
constructively irreducible public clauses. The implementation is a family of
class-dependent closed forms (Hit: `pre_path + 3 + parametric`, `path_count + d²`;
Map: `2κ + r²`; Modal: `m/2`, `(κ−m/2) + |B| + C(m,2)`; Axiomatic:
`ν(L_max) + κ + (k−1)`; Synthesis: distributive-law and polymorphism bonuses;
plus the `(post+1)/2` chaining term). The worry: these forms have enough free choices
that the 15-step trace could be a fixed point of tuning rather than a consequence of
the definitions — especially given a development history of many AI-driven iterations
in which inadvertent target-fitting is possible even with honest intent.

Two preliminary observations bound the problem before any derivation:

**Observation A (class taxonomy is forced).** The classes themselves
(Foundation / Former / Hit / Map / Modal / Axiomatic / Synthesis) are not free: they are
the constructor-theoretic *kind* of the extension, read off the AST head forms —
object-like, arrow-like, modality-like, record-like, temporal. Distinguishing them is
not tuning; a single closed form could not be correct across kinds any more than one
formula could count the elements of every algebraic structure. The single definition
ν = |L(B∪X)| − |L(B)| *evaluates differently* on different constructor kinds, exactly
as "order of the automorphism group" is one definition with family-dependent closed
forms. The right question is therefore not "why are there classes?" but "is each
class's closed form the correct evaluation of the one definition on that class?"

**Observation B (the gauge theorem).** The selection rule is invariant under global
rescaling of the schema-counting unit. If every ν is multiplied by a constant c (and
histories with it), then ρ = ν/κ scales by c, Ω = Σν/Σκ scales by c, and
Bar = Φ·Ω scales by c: every comparison ρ ≥ Bar and every minimal-overshoot ordering
is unchanged. **Only relative granularity across classes can matter.** All conventions
that apply uniformly (e.g. "one schema per generator" vs "two per generator,
everywhere") are pure gauge. This shrinks the audit to cross-class *ratios*, and it is
why several apparent choices below are labeled GAUGE.

## 1. Strengthened Definitions

The philosophical definitions support the derivations below once three terms are made
precise. These sharpenings are proposed as the canonical statement (for the paper's
§4 and Appendix audits):

**D1 (Schema).** A *derivation schema* over library B is a natural family of derivable
judgments generated by (i) a single new generator (formation, introduction, path
constructor, or operator clause), or (ii) a single adjoint mate of such a generator,
or (iii) a single depth-≤2 interaction of new generators with each other or with
sealed interfaces. Schemas are counted up to definitional equality and up to
adjoint mateship (mates are one schema, not two — see P2).

**D2 (Depth-2 counting).** Interactions are counted through *ordered pairs* at most:
a schema may be indexed by one generator, or by an ordered pair (generator, probe),
never by triples. This is the counting-level face of the d=2 coherence window: the
library's obligation surface is pairwise, so its derivational surface is pairwise.
(Triples and beyond are composites, definitionally complete, hence free.)

**D3 (Kernel clause).** Unchanged, but stated with the derivability contrast made
primary: a clause is charged iff realizing it requires a new neutral head or stuck
eliminator relative to the prior library's operational semantics (`def:derivability`
in the paper). Definitional completion is free *in κ and paid in ν* — this asymmetry
(free to specify, counted as capability) is deliberate and is what the Adjoint
Completion Principle formalizes.

## 2. The Six Principles

Every class formula in `nu.rs` decomposes, exactly, into the following six counting
principles. This is the central finding of the audit: the evaluator is not twenty
free choices; it is six principles applied per constructor kind, of which four are
forced or already proven and two need lemmas.

**P1 — Generator counting (FORCED).** Each irreducible clause contributes one
formation/introduction schema. Trivial from D1(i).

**P2 — Adjoint completion (FORCED; proven).** Each introduction forces its
eliminative mates: one eliminator per independent premise of the introduction
(λ-intro: one premise → application; pairing: two premises → two projections).
For structures organized as adjoint pairs/strings, units are the intro-side
generators (m modalities in an adjoint string have m/2 units) and counits/mates
are eliminative; of an ordered pair of interaction laws related by mateship, one is
definitionally free. This is the paper's Adjoint Completion lemma (Lawvere
adjointness); it *derives*, rather than stipulates: Witness ν=2; Π/Σ ν_G=2, ν_C=3;
Modal ν_G = m/2; Modal pairwise term = C(m,2) *un*ordered (mates halve the ordered
m(m−1) matrix).

**P3 — Universal property of inductive generators (FORCED).** A higher inductive
type with a path constructor opens exactly: the path introduction, the recursor,
and the inductor — the three components of its universal property. This is the
opaque-looking "+3" in `hit_nu_g = pre_path + 3 + parametric`: pre-path clauses
count by P1; the +3 *is* the HIT's defining data (nothing more is available,
nothing less exists). The `+1 parametric` (implemented as `Trunc(Var)`) is the
functoriality schema: a parametric former is a functor and its action on maps is
one schema; concrete types (S¹, S²) have no such action. FORCED, including the +1.

**P4 — Depth-2 self-coherence: the d² law (DERIVABLE; Lemma L1 required).**
A path constructor of dimension d contributes 1 β-schema plus d² second-order
computation schemas: `nu_h = path_count + d²`. Proposed derivation from D2: in the
depth-2 window, the computation payload of a d-cell is indexed by ordered pairs
(direction of travel, direction of coherence probe) — the full d×d matrix of the
cell's pairwise self-interactions; the β-rule is the unpaired base case. **Lemma L1
(to prove):** in the CCHM-style operational semantics, the independent composition/
filling schemas exposed by a single d-dimensional generator, counted per D1/D2, number
exactly d². Candidate proof route: composition in each of d dimensions × coherence
constraint in each of d dimensions; show the (i,j) fillers are jointly independent and
exhaustive at depth 2, with higher fillers definitionally complete. Rival counts that a
referee would propose — 2d (faces) and C(d,2) (unordered planes) — are *not* refuted by
principle yet; they are refuted only by the audited trace, which is circular if the
trace is in question. L1 is therefore the single most important open item.
Mitigating evidence of non-tuning: the d² rule is applied identically in three
independent contexts (HIT payload at Steps 5/7/8; the temporal inheritance bonus at
Step 15, where ν_H = Σd² = 1+1+4+9 = 15 over the loop-bearing library; and the
suspension pathway) — one rule, three exposures, no per-step freedom.

**P5 — Connectivity dichotomy (half proven, half DERIVABLE; Lemma L2 required).**
Extensions that *import* interfaces as a record/telescope (Axiomatic class) open:
the deepest import's inherited surface + one internal eliminative face per clause +
a spanning tree of bridges: ν_C = ν(L_max) + κ + (k−1). **This is already a proven
theorem** (Telescopic Elimination, paper §4: functorial subsumption forbids
double-counting shared ancestry; adjoint completion gives the κ faces; minimal
strong connection of k components is k−1 edges). Extensions that *construct
morphisms between* interfaces (Map class, the Hopf row) open: two composition
surfaces per arrow-clause (pre- and post-composition: 2κ) plus the full ordered
transport matrix over the r referenced strata (r²). The dichotomy itself is forced —
arrows compose (generating transport), record fields do not (generating access) —
and 2κ is forced (an arrow's two composition surfaces are its adjoint mates under
D1/P2). **Lemma L2 (to prove):** a weave that strongly connects r sealed strata
opens exactly the r² ordered pairs (i,j), including diagonal round-trips, as
independent depth-2 transport schemas. The diagonal (self-transport through the
weave = induced endomorphism) and the ordering (transport is directed) are both
principled inclusions; the lemma must show length-≤2 composites through the weave
neither undercount (< r²) nor overcount (> r², via definitional completion of
longer composites). Rival counts: r(r−1) (no diagonal), C(r,2) (undirected).

**P6 — Uniform action (FORCED given polymorphism).** An operator family polymorphic
over the universe acts on every sealed library entry, contributing |B| schemas —
one per entry, by D1(iii) with the entry as probe. Applied in exactly two places:
the modal shell's aspect action (|B| term in `modal_nu_c`) and the temporal
polymorphic eliminator (`detect_universe_polymorphism` = poly_elims × |B|). Same
principle, same granularity, both instances. The Synthesis-class distributive-law
bonus is the P5-record principle transposed: an exchange law λ : ST → TS is
precisely the naturality that lifts the referenced modal stratum's *entire* inherited
surface through the new operator (`law_count × ν(modal reference)`) — structurally
identical to the ν(L_max) inheritance term, hence inherits its proof.

## 4. The Three Residual Items

**L1 (the d² law).** RESOLVED at proof-sketch level — see
`LEMMA_L1_D_SQUARED.md` (2026-07-02). The count decomposes as d monodromy
schemas + d(d−1) variation schemas = d², plus the β-rule; rivals 2d and C(d,2)
are refuted at d = 1 by inspection of the circle, independently of the trace.
Two steps flagged for mechanization (the transpose obstruction; cofibration
union-staging), and one new off-trace engine discrepancy found
(max_dim² vs. Σ dᵢ² for multi-constructor telescopes → V2b). P4's status
upgrades to PROVEN (sketch), mechanization pending.

**L2 (the r² matrix).** RESOLVED at proof-sketch level — see
`LEMMA_L2_R_SQUARED.md` (2026-07-02). Decomposition: 2κ = per-clause
(forward face, lifting face) pairs, with lifting supplied by the charged
fibration structure — which *explains* the engine's κ = 1 carve-out (bare
arrows have no lifting face); r² = r monodromy families (diagonal, guaranteed
live by admissibility itself: trivial self-transport ⇒ trivializable ⇒
inadmissible) + r(r−1) directed couplings (ordered by the
propositional-inverse obstruction). P5 restated in unified closure form:
cross-interface surface = depth-2 closure of the reference structure under the
extension's own operations (access ⇒ spanning fusion, k−1; transport ⇒ full
matrix, r²). Delicate step flagged for mechanization: clause-face vs.
interface-transport disjointness, localized in a Möbius-bundle anchor.
P5-morphism upgrades to PROVEN (sketch), mechanization pending.

**The `(post+1)/2` term (Hit operative chaining).** RESOLVED by hand enumeration —
see `HSPACE_ENUMERATION.md` (2026-07-02). The principled count is
ν_C = post + ops (each operative clause's forward face, plus one cell-action
schema per *operation*), and ⌈post/2⌉ = ops under the canonical
(operation, coherence)-pairing convention. At Step 8 the three schemas are
apply-μ, unit-rewrite, and translate-the-cell — the last being exactly the
capability Step 9's Hopf construction consumes. The code now routes all three
sites through a documented `canonical_operation_count`; the convention's
enforcement for deviant packings is V1's second named target. Additionally, the
Lemma L1 correction (Σ dᵢ² for max(d)²) is applied engine-wide, including a
soundness fix to the Hit upper-bound estimator; V2b rerun required before
trace claims are re-asserted.

## 6. The Verification Program (pre-registered)

**V1 — Direct-counter ground truth (the decisive fix).** Implement a slow,
definition-faithful schema enumerator: given B and X, enumerate judgments per
D1/D2 explicitly (no class dispatch, no closed forms) and count. Then prove — or
empirically confirm across the full candidate corpus, including *rejected*
candidates from the retained-evidence artifacts — that the class formulas equal the
direct count on their domains. This converts every formula from definition to
theorem-about-a-definition. The class formulas become what they should have been
all along: optimizations. (Estimated scope: a new `pen-eval` module; the depth-2
window bounds the enumeration, so it is finite per candidate.)

**V2 — Rival-rule reruns.** Within the principled family, implement the rival
counts a referee would propose — d² → {2d, C(d,2)}, r² → {r(r−1), C(r,2)},
(post+1)/2 → {post pairs by derivation} — and rerun the strict lane end-to-end for
each. Pre-registered expectations: the Bootstrap and late-API steps (1–4, 10–15)
are insensitive (their formulas are in the FORCED set); Steps 5–9 orderings and the
Step-6/8 margins are exposed. Report which rivals preserve the 15-shell path and
which break it, and *where*. If the path survives all principled rivals, the
lane-conditionality caveat weakens dramatically. If it breaks under a rival that L1/L2
then prove correct, the trace revises and the framework updates — publicly.

**V3 — Evaluator archaeology.** The git history of `nu.rs` and the trace fixtures
records every formula change and every trace change (e.g., the draft-era totals
ν₁₁..ν₁₅ = 27/35/47/63/88 versus the current 26/34/46/62/103, with the *same
fifteen shells* selected). Reconstruct the timeline: for each formula change, was it
(a) a principled refactor with trace stability as an outcome, or (b) motivated by
trace preservation? The author's stated intent is (a) with no deliberate tuning;
the archaeology makes the claim auditable by a third party, which is worth more
than the claim.

**V4 — Independent re-derivation.** Hand D1–D3 and the constructor taxonomy (not
the formulas, not the trace) to an independent party — human or a fresh AI context
with no access to this repo — and ask them to derive the counts for each class.
Score agreement per formula. Disagreements localize exactly the conventions that
remain unforced.

## 7. Honest Bottom Line

The audit's headline is better than feared and sharper than hoped: the evaluator
decomposes into six principles, of which four are forced or proven, and the entire
degrees-of-freedom problem concentrates into two provable lemmas and one
enumerable coherence count — all three localized in the Geometric Ascent (Steps
5–9), none touching Bootstrap or the Framework/Terminal steps whose formulas are
theorem-backed. The 15-step trace is currently "principled modulo L1, L2, and one
H-space count." That is a radically smaller exposure than "nineteen hand-tuned
formulas," and it is now stated precisely enough to be closed — or to break the
trace, which would be equally valuable to know.

## 9. Machine-Check Boundary and T1 Consequence (2026-07-18)

`agda/CountingLemmas.agda` proves both displayed cardinalities from explicit
schema/basis isomorphisms. The Rust checker independently enumerates the
proposed index sets, validates their finite partitions, checks a submitted L2
graph, and tests numerical parity with the evaluator. Neither layer derives a
schema classifier from the current AST, which has no `coe`, `hcom`, fibration
semantics, or quotient by definitional interderivability.

These checks therefore do not close T1. After the Step-16 probe was corrected
to require symbolic raw-catalog membership and the engine's semantic-minimality
screen, the high-dimensional L1 and high-reference-count L2 maximizers ceased
to be lane candidates. A role-corrected Axiomatic candidate with `kappa = 3`
remains raw-generable and engine-minimal, with `nu = 108` and `rho = 36`. Its
score comes from P5-record inheritance, not L1 or L2, and it clears Bar_16.
Exhaustive Step-16 search also remains unavailable. Thus validating L1 and L2
can support the first fifteen scores, but cannot prove that no sixteenth step
exists.

## 10. P5-record Domain Audit (2026-07-18)

The remaining Axiomatic candidate does not satisfy the unique-maximal-import
hypothesis of the stated Telescopic Elimination theorem.  Its imports are
`{L14,L15}`; the accepted reference DAG has
`reach(14)={14,13,12,11,10}` and `reach(15)={15,10}`, so neither import
subsumes the other.  The evaluator nevertheless uses the historical-nu maximum
`L15`, a different operation from reachability maximum.

`crates/pen-eval/src/p5_record.rs` now computes this premise from the accepted
telescope history, and `agda/P5RecordBoundary.agda` proves the fixed finite
case independently.  Both also preserve the intended reference cases: Step 13
has dominant import `L12`, and Step 14 has dominant import `L13`.

This invalidates the candidate's *P5 theorem justification* for `nu=108`; it
does not prove internality.  The principled internal theorem is conditional on
a transparent elaboration/erasure equivalence, which the current untyped AST
cannot supply.  See `docs/P5_RECORD_INTERNALITY.md` for the exact boundary and
its consequence for T1.
