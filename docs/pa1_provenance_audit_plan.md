# PA-1 — The Provenance Audit: what the engine actually did

**Date:** 2026-07-25. **Status:** brief frozen before any audit output
exists. Companion to `provenance_disclosure_v1.md`. Purpose: settle,
stage by stage and run by run, the causal history of the certified
record — authored versus found — and re-grade every affected claim.
The audit's own discipline: no grade assigned before its evidence; no
narrative consideration (book schedule, milestone optics, sunk
narrative) admissible anywhere; sealed artifacts are never edited;
the finding that prompted this audit is the model for its honesty.

## PA-1a — The gate-and-literal census (mechanical + git)

For each of the fifteen stages and every clause position: (i) the
sealed literal's first-commit date and context; (ii) every
recognizer/gate referencing that shape (pen-core, pen-type,
pen-search), with commit dates and whether it sits on a live
enumeration path or a post-hoc replay path; (iii) the gate's
selectivity, established mechanically by perturbation probes in the
XF-0b2 pattern (singleton / narrow / genuinely open), with the probe
code shipped and replayable. Output: a 15-stage provenance matrix,
each cell graded literal-first / gate-first / derived, with digests.

## PA-1b — The open-competition question (the audit's center)

For every certified run in the record that reports a candidate
surface, determine what the enumeration could actually have accepted:

- the claim-lane surfaces (Step 1's 2,144; Step 15's 5,000-in-7,211);
- the reselection cones (v2/v3) and their discharger censuses;
- the T-BF1 Stage-4 cone (four candidates "re-enumerated directly");
- the BI-1/BI-1b branch cones, including the three never-enacted
  branches' stage cones 5–15;
- the Step-16 halting probes and the adversarial probe's survivors.

For each: were competitors real expressions lawfully generated and
lawfully rejected, or shapes that could never have been admitted
because the winning position was pinned? The question is answered by
instrumented reruns where feasible (widened probes on non-sealed
copies; never touching sealed artifacts) and by code-path analysis
where not. **If even one certified run contains a genuinely open
competition with a lawful winner, that run becomes the program's
strongest autonomy evidence and is named as such. If none does, that
is the finding, verbatim.**

## PA-1c — The claim re-grade

Every claim in the ch07 evidence ladder, the MS-1 headline set, and
the Genesis chapter's load-bearing sentences receives exactly one of
three grades, each cited to PA-1a/b evidence:

- **G-DISC** (earned as discovery): survives with autonomy content.
- **G-VER** (earned as verification): true of the sealed library,
  with the engine as auditor of a hand-derived sequence; the claim is
  re-stated in that form, which is weaker and still substantial.
- **G-UN** (unearned): neither; retired verbatim into testimony.

Expected pressure points, named now so the grades cannot dodge them:
the uniqueness census (law versus authorship echo); the Stage-4
four-cone (real law-silence versus authored multiplicity); the
recreation headline (convergence versus transcription); the halt
(law-property versus library-property); the BI cone invariances
(theorems about four authored branches remain theorems — about what,
exactly, is the re-grade's question).

## PA-1d — The forward criterion

One constructive deliverable: the specification of what a genuinely
open run would require in this codebase — enumeration without
position-pinned recognizers, acceptance by the adopted law only
(admissibility + total discharge, two-register), sealed-history
regression as the only use of the authored table — so that the
program has, at the end of the audit, not only a graded past but a
buildable path to the discovery claim it wanted: a **degenerate-gate
rerun**. Whether to build it is a separate decision; PA-1 prices it.

## Falsifiers

- **F-PA1.** Any grade assigned without its census/probe evidence →
  void.
- **F-PA2.** Any narrative ground (book, milestone, momentum) in any
  disposition → void on its face.
- **F-PA3.** Any perturbation probe or instrumented rerun touching a
  sealed artifact → void; copies only.
- **F-PA4.** Suppression or summary of an unflattering cell →
  the audit is void (the constitution's own clause, applied inward).
- **F-PA5.** The disclosure notice may not be weakened while PA-1
  runs; it is superseded only by PA-1's report.

## Relation to the standing program

Everything else pauses in place: the XF front items (XF-A artifacts,
XF-B5) may proceed — their grounds are content-facts and bridges, not
autonomy — but no book chapter touching the suspended claims is
revised until PA-1c lands, and no external communication cites them
without the disclosure. The program's own precedent governs the
mood: every prior audit of this kind ended with a theory that claimed
less and stood harder. This one is bigger, and the same is possible:
at minimum, a certified, hand-derived, machine-audited Genesis with a
buildable path to the open-run experiment its authors originally
wanted. What it can no longer end with is the unexamined version of
the story.
