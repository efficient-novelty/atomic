## The bounding principle, stated first

**Every operational bound of the domain must be an identification of an
already-adopted, dated, sealed quantity --- cited by provenance in the
definition itself.** A bound with no dated source is not a lawful
bound; it is a free parameter, and free parameters are exactly what
this program does not accept at law level. Three sources of bounds are
recognized, and only these three: the sealed library's closed content;
the adopted depth discipline; and the adopted declaration disciplines.
Explicitly inadmissible: raw AST-height caps (syntactic convenience
with no semantic ground --- the executor's own assessment, adopted
here as law); any bound justified by its effect on a regression,
enumeration size, or desired theorem (the 103-temptation in domain
form); any bound imported from the retired search-surface caps of the
enacted engine (those were exploration budgets, sealed as testimony,
never semantics).

## Rule: `schema2-operational-domain-v1`

The depth-two semantic domain \(\mathsf{NfSch}_2(H)\) over a sealed
history \(H\) is the set of typed normal schema judgments satisfying
all of the following, quotiented as in the parent rule:

1. **Alphabet bound (source: the sealed public boundary).** Every
   head symbol and former occurring in a domain judgment belongs to
   the closed public-symbol inventory exported by \(H\)'s sealed
   boundary, as fixed by the adopted former-closure rule --- the
   transparent formers and the sealed trace signatures, a finite,
   dated list. No symbol outside the sealed boundary's closure
   participates in the fragment; the fragment is \emph{about} the
   world that exists.
2. **Depth bound (source: the constitutive depth discipline).**
   Former application above library-resident content nests to depth
   at most **two** --- the same depth-two that bounds obligation
   discharge (the constitutive law's exact bound) and fixes the
   demand window's width. The identification is the definition's
   core: the depth-two \emph{fragment} is the fragment whose
   coherence burdens are dischargeable inside the depth-two
   \emph{window}. A tower `Susp^3(Univ)` is excluded not because it
   is tall but because its coherence debt outruns the window that
   would have to service it; the infinite towers of the stop are
   cut by the same knife that cuts the demand horizon, and by
   nothing else.
3. **Context and binder bound (source: the declaration disciplines).**
   Parameter contexts are dependent contexts under the adopted rules
   --- declared, finite, sequentially substituted --- with arity and
   binder depth bounded by the maximal declared arity and binder
   depth occurring in \(H\)'s sealed interface (a finite, computable,
   dated quantity of the sealed record, cited per history).
4. **Dimension bound (source: the sealed cubical content).** Path and
   cell dimensions are bounded by the maximal cubical dimension
   exported by \(H\)'s sealed boundary --- again a quantity read off
   the sealed record, not chosen.
5. **Provenance citations are part of the definition.** The domain
   implementation carries, for each bound, the digest of the adopted
   document or sealed artifact it is read from. A bound whose
   citation fails to resolve is a named gap; the domain does not
   fall back to any default.

**Theorem obligations (unchanged in spirit from clause 4 of the
parent, now discharged against the bounded fragment):** termination of
the typed normalizer on the bounded fragment; decidability of the
adopted quotient on it; finiteness of the quotiented enumeration.
Only with all three does T-D2-1 rerun.

**Regression semantics (the fitting ban, stated exactly).** The 61
proved families and 89 membership rows are the regression corpus: upon
a successful rerun they must be recognized as domain inhabitants. The
corpus \emph{tests} the identification; it may not \emph{shape} it ---
the bounds above are fixed by their citations before the regression
runs, and if a certified family then falls outside the bounded domain,
the result is a published divergence and a versioned successor, never
an adjustment of a bound. An identification that fails its regression
is wrong; a bound tuned to pass is worse than wrong.

**Grounds (all dated, none numeric-by-choice).** (i) The constitutive
depth bound \(d_{\mathrm{obl}} = 2\) --- adopted, proven exact, and
already load-bearing as the demand window's width; clause 2 identifies
rather than stipulates. (ii) The former-closure adoption: the
transparent-former list and sealed trace signatures are a closed,
dated alphabet. (iii) The dependent-context and declaration-independence
disciplines: declared, finite, enumerated interpretation data.
(iv) The sealed library itself as the source of its own interface
maxima --- reading bounds off the sealed record is the certified-field
dereference pattern at the level of the domain. (v) The T-D2-1 stop as
occasion, never ground.

## Falsifiers

- **F-OD1 (generator independence, inherited).** Any generator
  enumeration reaching the membership decision → void (F-D2-1
  verbatim).
- **F-OD2 (cited bounds only).** Any bound in the implementation
  without a resolving provenance citation to a dated adoption or
  sealed artifact → invalid on its face.
- **F-OD3 (the fitting ban).** Any bound chosen, revised, or defended
  by reference to regression outcomes, enumeration cardinality, or
  any desired theorem → invalid; the recorded order (bounds frozen,
  then regression) is part of the artifact and replays.
- **F-OD4 (no caps of convenience).** Raw AST-height caps, node
  budgets, or any bound whose only ground is tractability → invalid;
  a fragment the adopted semantics cannot bound is a named gap, not
  an engineering knob.
- **F-OD5 (scope).** This adjudication opens no gate: the bridge
  remains unauthorized, M-3 v1 authoritative, `m4_authorized` false;
  T-D2-3's frozen key schema is untouched; only a completed T-D2-1
  rerun (all three theorem obligations + regression) reopens T-D2-2,
  and only the full bridge chain reopens M-4.

## Consequences upon adoption

The infinite towers are excluded by the window's own knife; the domain
becomes bounded by what the world is rather than by what an engineer
caps; T-D2-1 reruns with the three theorem obligations against a
fragment every bound of which carries its pedigree; T-D2-2's
surjectivity question becomes well-posed for the first time. The
program's last term of art has an operational definition, and it was
--- fittingly --- the same "two" as everywhere else.
