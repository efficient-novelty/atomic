## Mission

Build the unified theorem layer that lets certified semantic content
replay \emph{exactly}: canonical dependent contexts, one typed
judgment form across all four certified surfaces, naturality under
every legal substitution, and full-content projections for the entire
historical corpus — so that T-D2-1 can rerun against evidence rather
than proxies.

## Tasks

- **DNF-1 (canonical dependent contexts).** A finite canonical form
  for dependent contexts, assembled from the adopted rules only
  (dependent-ambient-context: declared arity, sequential substitution,
  totality; support comprehension: canonical dependency analysis,
  acyclicity). Canonicity must be *checkable*: two presentations of
  one context normalize to one canonical form, with the normalization
  replayable. No new context semantics; a context the adopted rules
  cannot canonicalize is a named gap.
- **DNF-2 (unified judgment form).** One typed judgment presentation
  covering the core, ordinary, cubical, and R1 surfaces, built by
  identification: each surface's sealed presentation maps into the
  unified form by a cited, structure-preserving translation, and the
  translations compose with the frozen equality. If any surface's
  content resists structure-preserving translation, that is a result
  (a genuine format heterogeneity), published — not smoothed.
- **DNF-3 (naturality closure).** Naturality of family membership
  under every legal substitution, proved once at the layer level:
  reindexing by any well-typed substitution commutes with
  normalization and the family quotient. The exported transport maps
  (M-1) are the toolbox; count agreement is never evidence — the
  squares themselves are constructed and replay.
- **DNF-4 (full-content projections — the corpus rebuilt as evidence).**
  For each of the 61 proved families and 89 A3 rows: a create-new
  projection theorem supplying the full telescope, presentation,
  substitution set, and naturality witnesses, *re-derived* from the
  sealed artifacts and adopted machinery — never backfilled from
  expectation. Per-row disposition is exactly one of
  {projected, named-gap}; a named gap leaves the historical claim
  standing at its original granularity and honestly blocks that row's
  domain recognition. No partial credit, no representative sampling.
- **DNF-5 (rerun).** With DNF-1..4 sealed: T-D2-1 reruns unchanged —
  same bounds, same citations, same three theorem obligations — and
  its regression now tests exact recognition against full-content
  evidence. Only a T-D2-1 pass reopens T-D2-2; only the completed
  chain (T-D2-2 → BC1 → 431-gap bridge rerun → M-3 v2 at 9/9)
  reopens M-4.

## Falsifiers

- **F-DNF1 (no proxies, permanent).** Any hash, count, label, digest
  match, or archived verdict standing in for constructed content
  anywhere in this layer → the containing artifact is void. The
  retracted route stays retracted.
- **F-DNF2 (no backfill).** Any projection witness not derivable from
  sealed artifacts plus adopted machinery → named gap; inventing the
  witness the archive "must have had" is the provenance-circularity
  disease in evidence form.
- **F-DNF3 (bounds untouched).** Any modification, reinterpretation,
  or parameterization of the domain bounds from inside this layer →
  void (F-OD2..4 lineage).
- **F-DNF4 (translation honesty).** Any DNF-2 translation that drops,
  merges, or repairs surface content to fit the unified form → void;
  heterogeneity is published, not dissolved.
- **F-DNF5 (scope).** No gate moves: M-3 v1 authoritative,
  `m4_authorized` false, BC1 unopened until DNF-5's chain runs.
  Parked items stay parked.
