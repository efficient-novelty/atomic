## What this project is

This is a project of **explicit rational reconstruction**: reverse
engineering known physics down to its canonical philosophical
principles, to understand — for the author first, potentially for
others — *why* the world's formal structure is what it is. It belongs
to the lineage of Kolmogorov's axioms for probability, von Neumann's
for quantum mechanics, Carathéodory's for thermodynamics, and the
modern quantum reconstruction programs: start from the known answer,
hunt for the fewest principles that make it inevitable.

It is **not** a discovery-science project. The Genesis sequence was
derived by hand, from known mathematics and physics, and transcribed
into the engine; the machinery exists to *audit* the reconstruction —
to verify its bookkeeping, catch its double counting, refuse its
circularities, and stress-test its claims — not to pretend to have
found it. The machine-audited reconstruction is itself this project's
methodological novelty. Claims are made in reconstruction voice:
"the two laws *host / compress / recreate* known structure," never
"the engine discovered it blind."

## The two goals, in order

1. **Now (writing phase): retrodictive closure.** The theory should
   fit the relations and parameters of known physics — the Standard
   Model's discrete structure, the kinematic frameworks of GR and QM
   — from as few principles as possible, with every fit's cost
   honestly priced.
2. **Before publication: a thin forward layer.** A small set of
   genuinely falsifiable predictions, registered under full blindness
   discipline, so the reconstruction can be tested against future
   experiments. This layer is a bonus and a bet, not the project's
   justification.

## The quality metric: lossless abstraction, scored by compression

The goal is **lossless abstraction** — not compression alone. The
distinction is load-bearing. Compression tolerates summaries: fitting
most of physics approximately. Losslessness is the constraint that
every covered relation and parameter is recovered *exactly*, and that
no covered fact is quietly dropped, approximated, or broken by a later
fit (any genuine loss is disclosed as a loss note, never absorbed).
Subject to that constraint, the **compression ratio** is the score:
how few principles, how much physics, how little per-fact tuning.
Additional bits are acceptable — the author will pay for more axioms
where the physics demands them — provided the abstraction stays
lossless and every bit's price is on the tag. One axiom per fact is a
paraphrase; few axioms, many facts, everything recovered exactly, is
insight.

This standard is the method mirroring its object: each Genesis payment
is certified as a lossless abstraction of the library beneath it, and
the reconstruction as a whole aims to be a lossless abstraction of
known physics. The book's methodology is its own subject's law applied
one level up. (See `ch_parsimoniousness.tex` for the three kinds of
parsimony and the Kolmogorov framing; the framework's honest weakness
— apparatus cost — is priced there too.)

## What does not relax

The internal honesty discipline is not discovery-science theater; it
is how a reconstruction avoids fooling *itself*, and it stays in full
force: versioned adjudications with pre-registered falsifiers; the
register discipline (every counted unit carries a receipt);
fix-the-machinery-never-the-history; published divergences; burned
hypotheses stay burned. The audits that corrected this project's own
ledgers (358 to 32), exposed its own circular certificate, and caught
its own narrative drift are the reason the reconstruction can be
trusted — and they apply with equal force to everything below.

## Calibration notes for AI collaborators

- Do not apply blind-discovery standards to R-layer (reconstruction)
  work; apply the compression ledger and the disclosure rules.
- Do apply full blindness (registration before comparison, no
  target-aware premises) to F-layer (forward prediction) work.
- The distinction between "fits known physics" (the goal) and
  "secretly tuned while claiming derivation" (the sin) is the
  disclosure, and it is absolute.
- When in doubt about which voice a claim should wear, ask: could
  this sentence survive a referee who has read the engine's git
  history? Write that sentence.
