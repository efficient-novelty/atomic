## 1. Overarching research objective (registered restatement)

The two-law research program aims to reverse engineer the constitutive
and selective principles that could generate a rich, self-propagating
foundational world from minimal registered structure, while treating the
historical Genesis sequence and recognizable physical structures as
held-out evidence rather than encoded targets. Philosophical motivation
(the book's two-law architecture) is reference material; it must never
enter the production dependency graph.

Division of labor (registered 2026-07-30, plan §1.1a): the fixed
philosophical input is only the two-law commitment itself — one law for

reverse-engineering objective. Every specific formulation question in

## 2. The H3/H4 result, rediagnosed

The frozen `gsc-inductive-completion-core-v1` profile produced exactly
one Act-4 quotient class — the direct unit eliminator with its
computation equation — and the H4 continuation then halted debt-free.
The plan records this as profile-relativity (lesson 4.2). The present
frontier sharpens the diagnosis:

- The H3 profile interpreted the unit type as an ordinary finite
  inductive datatype. Under that interpretation the constitutive debt of
  Acts 1–3 is owner-specific elimination, and the direct eliminator is
  exactly the correct next structure. The profile did not fail; it
  faithfully discharged the debt its own reading compiled.
- The alternative reading: the first acts are arena-founding. They do
  not merely add three declarations to an already complete metalanguage;
  they establish the field in which later formation, substitution, and
  comparison become possible. Under that reading their principal debt
  can be larger than owner-specific elimination.

The direct-eliminator halt is therefore evidence about the level of
abstraction at which the first acts were read, not evidence against the
two-law architecture.

## 3. The candidate principle (target-neutral formulation)

Constitutive Self-Containment:

> No operation that is essential to the formation, substitution, or
> comparison of public possibilities may remain permanently external to
> the world once it acts nontrivially on sealed public structure.

Compressed: the grammar must eventually become part of the world it
grammars.

Derivation sketch from the Constitutive Law: the law uses contexts,
context extension, substitution, and reindexing to state what is well
formed and what follows from what. If those operations remain forever in
an external metalanguage, the supposedly fundamental world is not
explanatorily closed: its order of possibility depends on operations
that never enter its own order of actuality. Once a public type `A` and
a public witness `a : A` have been sealed, context extension is no
longer an empty formal possibility — there is an actual projection
`π_A : Γ.A → Γ` and an actual reindexing `π_A* : T(Γ) → T(Γ.A)` acting
on sealed public structure. Constitutive self-containment then writes a
promissory note: internalize this operation by its least universal
public interface.

The demand must be stated without naming its expected dischargers:

- the obligation is the internalization of reindexing along a sealed
  extension, characterized by universal properties (the adjoint
  characterizations of the reindexing operation — its universal left
  and right companions — stated as introduction/elimination/computation
  contracts against the unit/counit of the extension, in whatever exact
  finite form the demand compiler adopts);
- the obligation attaches under a trigger condition to be formalized:
  the reindexing operation "acts nontrivially on sealed public
  structure" (the formal trigger — first nontrivial use versus at the
  sealing act itself — is an open design question below); and
- the profile must compile positive activations from the current target-
  neutral sealed public grammar and their exact opaque-export support, without
  a chronological cutoff or a held-out answer. Present derivability is a
  separate complete disposition, not part of activation.

This is constitutive work in the two-law sense: the architecture claims
two irreducible jobs, not exactly two isolated clauses, and additional
foundational principles are species of constitutive or selective work.
Contextual internalization determines what a self-contained field of
coherent possibility must include; it selects nothing.

## 4. Registered held-out prediction

In categorical semantics, the canonical universal internalizations of
reindexing along an extension are its adjoints
`Σ_A ⊣ π_A* ⊣ Π_A`: dependent sum packaging a base object with
dependent witness-bearing content, and dependent product abstracting
context-dependent constructions into uniform operations.

Prediction (registered now, before any profile is formulated or run):
if a contextual-internalization profile is lawfully executed, the
external decoder will identify the surviving quotient classes of its
dischargers with dependent sum and dependent product structure.

This prediction is held-out evidence in the XF-campaign sense. It must
not inform demand compilation, generation, acceptance, quotienting, or
halting. If the blind run produces different dischargers, or halts, or
branches, that outcome is a mathematical result about the principle,
not a defect to be repaired by tuning the demand until the prediction
comes true. A demand schema reverse-engineered from the shape of Pi or
Sigma would be target fitting and void.

## 6. Open research questions (owned by the program)

Each question below is to be resolved by argument from the registered
principles — never by which answer produces the anticipated
dischargers — with the rationale documented and adversarially reviewed
before the freeze, and with genuine underdeterminations registered as
such (carried as branching profiles where executable).

1. Trigger condition: which exact positive structural event activates the
   internalization scheme--sealing the extension itself, or the first public
   nontrivial use of reindexing on sealed structure? Activation must be
   derived from the current grammar. A separate complete disposition decides
   whether the activated scheme is presently derivable, certified
   underivable, or unknown.
2. Least universal interface: the exact finite statement of the
   universal properties inside the demand language, including how
   uniqueness (the universal property's mediating-map clause) is
   expressed in the GF2 fragment without importing unbounded
   quantification.
3. Checkable-fragment boundary (verified 2026-07-30): the trusted
   kernel itself ALREADY supports Sigma — `Term::Sigma` with pairs and
   projections is fully handled by the kernel checker
   (`pen-kernel/src/syntax.rs`, `checker.rs`). What excludes
   Sigma-shaped structure is the certificate surface: the synthesis
   protocol V2 (`Term::Sigma → OutsideLambdaUnitFragment`) and the
   lambda/unit production-wire grammar with its Agda mirrors. If the
   blind run's dischargers require Sigma-shaped carriers, the work is
   therefore a versioned extension of the wire grammar, the safe Agda
   decoders/checkers/correspondence, and the synthesis protocol — not
   of the trusted kernel — under the §12.1 wire-change rules. The
   honest alternatives remain (a) extending that certificate fragment
   first, under review and freeze, or (b) recording that the
   discharger lies outside the checkable fragment (plan §5.8) rather
   than silently weakening the check. Which route to prepare is a
   design decision that must precede the freeze.
4. Support factorization: when the extension and trigger are chronologically
   distant, does the normalized obligation factor through one opaque export
   and one typed substitution (unary support), or irreducibly require a
   compatibility square between two exports (binary support)? Chronological
   distance itself must not expire a public obligation.
5. Relation to the H3 profile: whether
   `csc-arena-internalization-v1` replaces the inductive-completion
   reading for Acts 1–3 or composes with it (the direct eliminator was
   a genuine discharge of a genuine debt under its reading; the
   relationship between the two readings' ledgers must be stated, not
   assumed).
