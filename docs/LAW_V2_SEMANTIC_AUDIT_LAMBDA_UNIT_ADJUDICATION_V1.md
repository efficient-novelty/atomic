The issued H3/H4 results, the Profile A registry, and
[`LAW_V2_WINDOW_REGISTER_AUDIT_V1.md`](LAW_V2_WINDOW_REGISTER_AUDIT_V1.md)

## Decision

The first rewrite-admissibility theorem should be proved for an independently
meaningful projection-free fragment:

> finite dependent lambda calculus with universe levels 0 and 1, unit,
> transparent definitions and aliases, opaque operations, and fresh
> nonrecursive public equations.

The profile is manifest-indexed and finite. It makes no normalization,
confluence, or completeness claim about arbitrary GF2 syntax. Records,
record descriptors, and projections are excluded from this first profile
rather than admitted without their missing reduction theorem.

## Exact supported surface

The term surface contains exactly:

```text
Sort(0)
Sort(1)
UnitType
Unit
Variable(index)
Global(id)
Pi(parameter_type, body_type)
Lambda(parameter_type, body)
Apply(function, argument)
```

The public surface may contain:

- verified transparent public definitions and aliases;
- verified opaque bodyless public operation heads;
- separately sealed public equations;
- fresh equations only when they are typed, left-linear, nonrecursive,
  nonoverlapping, and conservative over predecessor-supported terms;
- finite declaration groups and equation-free operation-role metadata that
  contains no record field or projection payload;
- exact conservative public extensions;
- the finite context, context-amalgamation, substitution, and one-hole-context
  carriers fixed by the manifest;
- rank-0, rank-1, and rank-2 schema derivations; and
- a positively verified-empty origin-cutoff Q3 registry.

The profile contains no `Sigma`, record, record constructor, record
descriptor, record field, projection term, projection clause, or
projection-reduction witness. A metadata label cannot move any such object
inside the fragment.

## Projection boundary

`DescriptorForcedProjection` is absent from the lambda/unit Q0 manifest. The
verified public inventory supplied to this profile must have an exhaustively
empty forced-projection census.

Any of the following returns exactly:

```text
OutsideFragment(DescriptorProjection)
```

- a nonempty forced-projection inventory;
- a raw forced-projection public clause;
- a descriptor that introduces a record field or projection;
- a projection term or projection reduction; or
- a claimed descriptor-forced-projection reconstruction.

This disposition aborts the complete audit. It is not `Unknown`, a certified
absence, a zero-cost classification, or permission to omit the object from a
census.

Other unsupported syntax receives its own frozen `OutsideFragment` reason.
Resource exhaustion and an undecided in-fragment obligation remain distinct
from `DescriptorProjection`.

## Verified public inventory

The profile consumes only a private verifier-minted public-inventory
capability. Its unchecked wire is replayed against:

- the exact predecessor history and public boundary;
- the exact successor boundary and conservative append;
- every public group, declaration, and equation;
- every source identity and source-to-normal judgment;
- declaration and equation origins;
- predecessor-visible demand contracts with exact family/output `PortKey`s;
- the canonical dependency DAG and verifier-derived availability;
- the exhaustive empty forced-projection census; and
- the positively verified-empty origin-cutoff Q3 registry.

Private verified fields and replayed contents, rather than caller labels or
`complete = true` flags, carry authority. Internal inventory closure is only
relative to the supplied generic ledger. It does not prove that the ledger is
the issued Profile A history.

## Pre-Q0 raw carrier

The dependency order is:

```text
verified inventory
-> syntactic raw carrier
-> finite rewrite universe
-> rewrite-admissibility theorem
-> Q0 normalization
-> Q1/Q2/Q3 quotient
```

The raw rank-0/1/2 carrier is generated syntactically before Q0
deduplication. The manifest enumerates every finite context, context
amalgamation, substitution, source tuple, public application, equation action,
and registered one-hole witness. Every enumerated tuple receives exactly one
disposition:

```text
Applicable(canonical_derivation)
CertifiedInapplicable(typed_reason)
OutsideFragment(reason)
Unknown(reason)
```

Only `CertifiedInapplicable` excludes a tuple. Neither normalization nor
serialized order may suppress a raw derivation before rewrite authority
exists. One `OutsideFragment` or `Unknown` disposition leaves the complete
audit undefined.

The finite substitution carrier has two generators and their finite
composites:

1. an **embedding lift** maps every source variable along a
   dependency-respecting context embedding; and
2. a **forced-newest-argument substitution** maps
   \(\Gamma,A\) to \(\Gamma\) by the identity on the old variables and maps
   the newest variable to an enumerated term \(a:A\) in \(\Gamma\).

Every substitution stores the complete source-variable image vector in
oldest-first declaration order. The verifier checks those images
left-to-right against the successively substituted dependent source
telescope, closes the finite set under composition, and binds every image and
typing judgment into the substitution identity. Context renamings alone are
not a complete substitution carrier. Omission of a forced-newest instance or
composite returns `Unknown(IncompleteEnumeration)`.

## Q0 rule manifest

The lambda/unit Q0 grammar contains only:

- de Bruijn binder normalization;
- left-to-right sequential substitution;
- typed beta reduction for lambda/application;
- provenance-preserving delta unfolding of verified transparent public
  definitions;
- the `UnitType` and `Unit` rules;
- canonical telescope flattening; and
- separately admitted fresh, nonrecursive, nonoverlapping public equations.

It contains no descriptor-generated projection rule. Eta, arbitrary iota,
zeta, theorem search, recursive equations, overlapping equations, and
unregistered rewrite rules are outside the profile.

An inventoried fresh equation is candidate reduction data, not Q0 authority.
Only an inventory-bound rewrite-system theorem may authorize it.

## Ambient public export

`Sort(0)`, `Sort(1)`, `UnitType`, and `Unit` remain ambiently formable but are
not automatically public. A primitive is publicly exported only when the
complete normalized body of a verified public declaration is that primitive.
Occurrence in a type, proper subterm, verifier implementation, or unexported
intermediate reduction is not a public export.

Before this profile can classify such a seed, a verifier-produced census must:

1. replay all predecessor public declaration bodies;
2. replay all successor-new public declaration bodies;
3. retain normalization provenance;
4. form complete Q0/Q2 export classes; and
5. distinguish predecessor re-export from the first successor export class.

Simultaneous equivalent first exports form one Q2 class. No declaration
order, hash, or caller `first_export` label selects a representative. Missing
authority for this census returns `Unknown(MissingAmbientFirstExportInventory)`.

## Ordinary beta

Ordinary beta implicit in a verified bodyful definition is structural
behavior, not an additional public equation or raw cost clause. When a
separately represented equation is claimed to be that ordinary behavior, the
verifier must reconstruct:

- the definition body and binder telescope;
- the complete typed argument spine;
- the sequential substitution;
- the substituted body and result type; and
- the exact kernel-replayed delta/beta/congruence reduction path.

A tag asserting that beta occurred is not a proof. Until a private,
inventory-bound derivation capability supplies this path, the in-fragment
case returns `Unknown(MissingOrdinaryBetaDerivation)`.

## Finite rewrite-admissibility theorem

The theorem is finite and manifest-relative. A verifier first constructs the
complete typed term universe from:

- inventory declaration, equation, and demand judgments;
- every pre-Q0 raw-family judgment;
- every finite context and substitution witness; and
- every subterm of those objects.

It closes that universe under every candidate one-step beta,
provenance-preserving delta, unit, and inventoried fresh-equation reduction,
and under every substitution in the frozen finite substitution carrier. If
closure exceeds a frozen resource bound, it returns `ResourceExhausted`; it
does not truncate.

For every term/rule pair, the one-step graph records exactly one of:

```text
Applicable(typed_edge)
CertifiedInapplicable(reason)
OutsideFragment(reason)
Unknown(reason)
```

Only applicable edges enter the graph. A private
`VerifiedRewriteSystemV1`-style capability may be minted only after replay
proves:

1. type preservation for every edge;
2. substitution stability, with an actual path for every edge and admitted
   substitution;
3. termination by absence of nontrivial strongly connected components and
   self-loops, together with an explicit decreasing topological rank;
4. confluence by exactly one reachable normal form for every node;
5. exhaustive critical-pair and overlap disposition; and
6. conservativity by equality of the predecessor graph and the successor graph
   restricted to predecessor-supported terms.

One omitted edge, untyped edge, missing substitution square, cycle, multiple
normal forms, unresolved overlap, or conservativity mismatch returns
`Unknown` and grants no Q0 authority. These are theorems about the complete
finite audit universe, not arbitrary ambient GF2.

## Q1, Q2, and Q3

After authorized Q0 normalization:

- Q1 preserves originating-family identity; specialization does not create a
  new family.
- Q2 exhaustively decides the frozen finite presentation witnesses on the
  fixed normalized vertex set, including binder renaming, independent
  declaration exchange, typed telescope presentation, predecessor/dependency-
  prior transparent aliases, and duplicate public presentations.
- Q3 accepts only the positively verified-empty origin-cutoff registry.

Every ordered vertex/witness pair receives `Applicable`,
`CertifiedInapplicable`, `OutsideFragment`, or `Unknown`. The quotient is the
exact connected-component partition of all applicable Q1/Q2 edges; Q3 adds no
edge.

## Downstream theorem gates

This scope decision does not itself complete the semantic audit. Before any
value could be considered for adoption, a future implementation must still
prove:

- raw-carrier completeness;
- finite rewrite admissibility and Q0 soundness/completeness;
- exhaustive Q1/Q2 and verified-empty Q3 quotient completeness;
- structural family weakening and old-support restriction with
  \(\mathrm{res}_x\circ\mathrm{wk}_x=\mathrm{id}\);
- the exact quotient-class marginal set difference and support-touching
  theorem;
- total typed SR2 anchor assignment, transport, and injectivity; and
- independent Rust/safe-Agda transcript agreement over the full generic and
  adversarial vector suite.

The Agda side must compute from the same canonical input data. Typechecking a
fixed reference module or embedding a Rust-produced answer is not transcript
agreement.

## Adoption and freeze boundary

This document neither adopts nor freezes
`gf2-semantic-audit-lambda-unit-v1`. A later adoption packet must expose the
exact manifest, candidate digests, theorem surface, transcripts, supported

freeze may a separately isolated Profile A adapter be proposed. That adapter

registry. This document does not create such an adapter.
