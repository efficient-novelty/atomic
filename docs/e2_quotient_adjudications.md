# E-2 quotient adjudications: two count-blind rules

**Date:** 2026-07-19. **Status:** **ADOPTED — both rules** (2026-07-19;
see ADOPTION blocks). Prepared from `docs/SCHEMA2_RESULT.md`
(`ADJUDICATION_REQUIRED_STAGE1_UNIVERSE_PACKAGE_BOUNDARY`,
`ADJUDICATION_REQUIRED_STEP8_POST_PATH_TYPED_SEMANTICS`).

**Design constraint (binding).** Per F-E4, the sealed counts (`1` at
Stage 1; the Step-8 cardinality) are inadmissible as grounds. This
proposal therefore adopts *rules*, not stage answers. Each rule is
count-blind and kernel-decidable; its application to Stage 1 and Step 8
is engine output. If that output disagrees with a sealed count, the
disagreement is recorded as Phase-5b fork data — never absorbed, never
retuned.

## Rule R1: the formation–completion package rule
### `formation-completion-package-family-rule-v1`

1. A formation clause together with its uniform completion/action over
   declared parameters constitutes **one** natural family — the completed
   package family. The carrier/formation clause exports no separate
   family.
2. Exception, kernel-decided: the carrier exports a second family only if
   it carries an independent typed role (per the `LocalRole` inventory)
   whose derivation is *not generated* by the package's own
   formation/completion derivations. Independence is a generator-
   membership fact, not a label.
3. The package family's canonical normal form is the completed action;
   the formation clause is its recorded carrier (provenance, not credit).
   Coverage direction: completion covers carrier, typed and replayed.
   Predecessor disposition is decided on the package, once.

**Grounds (all frozen, none numeric).** T4's family-versus-instance law
(uniform specializations are members of one family); the Phase-2
definitional rule already registered in
`SEMANTIC_NORMALIZATION_PROGRAM.md` (§2, item 4); D1's mates-count-as-one
pedigree; and the charging asymmetry: clauses are κ-side objects, and a
carrier's *presence* is already priced there — exporting it again as a
ν-side family would charge the same content on both sides of the ledger.

**Relation to the certificate's options.** R1 is option B in rule form,
with option A's operational content recovered by clause 3 (the action is
the canonical form) and the A/B difference reduced to recorded provenance.
Option C is *not* foreclosed by fiat: if the kernel finds the Stage-1
carrier satisfies the clause-2 exception, R1 itself outputs two families,
and the sealed `1` becomes a certified divergence routed to Phase 5b.

## Rule R2: the derived-action rule + Step-8 registered signatures
### `derived-action-generator-membership-rule-v1`

1. An operation-on-cell (or coherence) row is an **independent** natural
   family iff its typed derivation is not generated by the parent
   operation's naturality generators (the E-4 basis) over the frozen
   equality. Independence is decided by generator membership — never by
   position, label, or archived cardinality.
2. Registered typed interpretation of the three Step-8 positional rows
   (interpretation, revisable under the F-B1 pattern if term typing
   fails; never by loosening the checker):
   - clause-3 operation: `mu : El(S3) × El(S3) → El(S3)`, a natural
     family in both arguments over the sealed signature;
   - clause-4 unit/coherence: the typed equality `mu(base, x) = x`,
     orientation left-unit, as a coherence-role family (the right-unit
     variant, if demanded by elaboration, is a mate under D1, not a
     second family);
   - cell action: `mu`'s action on the Step-8 3-cell (the `PathCon(3)`
     stratum), typed against the registered constant-boundary bundle.
3. Whether the cell action is independent is then R2-clause-1 output:
   if the E-4 generators for `mu` already generate it, it is an instance;
   if not, it is an independent family with a `SupportAction` anchor.

**Grounds.** The E-4 generator basis is the already-registered arbiter of
"generated versus new" (spec §1.4); the `LocalRole` inventory is frozen
in the EGP program; `HSPACE_ENUMERATION.md` supplies the hand reading as
*interpretation input* exactly as the sphere diagrams did for boundary
v2 — registered, typed, and revisable on typing failure only.

## Falsifiers

- **F-Q1.** A registered Step-8 signature fails term-level elaboration →
  the interpretation is revised in a versioned successor; the checker is
  never weakened (F-B1 pattern).
- **F-Q2.** R1/R2 kernel output at any historical stage diverges from the
  sealed count → certified divergence, recorded verbatim, routed to the
  Phase-5b fork; the rules are not adjusted to fit, and the history is
  not edited (both directions of the standing law).
- **F-Q3.** Either rule found to require the archived count or the bar in
  its decision procedure → rule invalid as adopted; successor required.
- **F-Q4.** The clause-2 / clause-1 independence tests prove undecidable
  in the fragment → named obstruction; the affected rows stay pending, as
  `PathConstructorIntro` correctly does now.
