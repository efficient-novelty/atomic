\section{Reversible Determination and the Jordan--Quantum Closure}
\label{sec:reversible-determination}

Primitive-Parameter Provenance removes unexplained continuous law dials.
Primitive Public Irredundancy removes primitive distinctions that perform no
independently required public work.  Isometric Ledger Invariance closes the
pure-to-mixed process interface once a positive dagger exists.  The remaining
upstream gap is the geometry of the state cone itself: why preparations and tests
should be reciprocal, why every state should admit a spectral decomposition, and
why the Hilbert-functional shell should realize a Jordan rather than a generic
convex operational theory.

The wider philosophy points to a symmetry principle rather than a numerical one.
Within one irreducible system type, a pure alternative may be a different actuality,
but it should not be a different \emph{kind} of actuality carrying an unexplained
primitive label.  Likewise, two full-support states contain the same live
alternatives and differ only in their public weighting; an unbridgeable difference
between them would be a brute distinction in the manner of weighting rather than in
what the system can be.  The physical statement is:
\begin{center}
\emph{Pure alternatives have no brute names, faithful weightings have no brute
shapes, and a determinate alternative must be recognizable as well as preparable.
Pure alternatives are related by reversible motions; full-support weightings by
reversible filters; preparation and sharp recognition are reciprocal.}
\end{center}

\begin{definition}[Finite operational state cone]
For a finite physical system $A$, let $V_A$ be the real vector space spanned by its
unnormalized states, let $C_A\subset V_A$ be the closed pointed generating cone of
positive states, and let $u_A\in C_A^*$ be the deterministic effect.  The normalized
state space is
\[
 \Omega_A:=\{\omega\in C_A:u_A(\omega)=1\}.
\]
A normalized state is \emph{pure} when it is an extreme point of $\Omega_A$.  A
state is \emph{faithful} when it lies in $\operatorname{int}C_A$, equivalently when
no nonzero positive effect vanishes on it.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Reversible motions and reversible filters]
A \emph{reversible motion} of $A$ is an order automorphism
$U:V_A\to V_A$ with $u_AU=u_A$ whose inverse is an admissible deterministic
process.  A \emph{reversible filter} is an order automorphism
$F:C_A\to C_A$ for which both $F$ and $F^{-1}$ are physically implementable after
multiplication by nonzero success factors.  Thus a reversible filter need not be
deterministic: it is reversible on its successful branch and changes weights without
removing any state direction.
\end{definition}

\begin{axiom}[Reversible Determination --- no brute state labels]
\label{ax:reversible-determination}
For every finite irreducible physical system $A$:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{D\arabic*},leftmargin=2.4em]
\item \emph{Pure accessibility.}  For every pair of normalized pure states
      $\alpha,\beta\in\Omega_A$, there is a reversible motion $U$ with
      $U\alpha=\beta$.
\item \emph{Faithful accessibility.}  For every pair of faithful states
      $\rho,\sigma\in\operatorname{int}C_A$, there is a reversible filter $F$ with
      $F\rho=\sigma$.
\item \emph{Sharp reciprocal witnessability.}  There is at least one normalized
      pure state $\alpha$ and one pure effect $a$ with $a(\alpha)=1$.  Reversible
      motions transport admissible effects contragrediently, and the physical effect
      interface is the least convex, coarse-graining-complete family generated by
      that transported sharp orbit.
\end{enumerate}
Equivalently, the reversible dynamics is transitive on pure normalized states, the
reversible-filter group is transitive on the interior of the positive cone, and
every pure orbit carries a sharp recognizing test whose mixtures and compatible
coarse-grainings exhaust the freely sealed public effect interface.
\end{axiom}

\begin{remark}[Placement, irreducibility, and conservation laws]
The axiom belongs to the actualization/integration face of the Selective Law.  It
compares no candidates by value and fixes no parameter.  It states the internal
mobility of a system type after that type has been selected.

The irreducibility qualification is essential.  A charge or superselection label
carried by an independently required public record may split a system into sectors;
Axiom~\ref{ax:reversible-determination} applies within each irreducible sector.
Primitive Public Irredundancy removes an idle sector label, but it does not erase a
load-bearing one.  Likewise D2 does not say that arbitrary state conversion is free
under a fixed laboratory Hamiltonian.  The filter may consume a resource and succeed
probabilistically; it says only that no full-support weighting is a new primitive
kind of state.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Independence from the previous closure axioms]
Primitive Public Irredundancy does not imply transitivity: several symmetry orbits
may all be publicly distinguishable and therefore non-idle.  Primitive-Parameter
Provenance does not imply it either, because a finite or isolated orbit label is not
a continuum.  Isometric Ledger Invariance presupposes a dagger and therefore cannot
supply the positive state--effect geometry derived below.  Reversible Determination
is new bridge content, and its strength is exposed rather than hidden.
\end{remark}

\subsection{From reversible accessibility to Jordan structure}

\begin{theorem}[Homogeneity and pure transitivity]
\label{thm:rd-homogeneous-pure-transitive}
For every finite irreducible system satisfying Reversible Determination, the cone
$C_A$ is homogeneous and its normalized pure-state space is pure-transitive.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proofsketch}

\end{proofsketch}

\begin{theorem}[Self-duality and Euclidean Jordan closure]
\label{thm:rd-jordan-closure}
Assume the standard finite operational hypotheses: $C_A$ is closed, pointed,
generating, and finite-dimensional, and the declared reversible filters are genuine
cone automorphisms.  Then every irreducible system satisfying Reversible
Determination admits a positive-definite inner product $\langle-,-\rangle_A$ for
which
\[
 C_A
 =
 C_A^*
 :=
 \{x\in V_A:\langle x,y\rangle_A\ge0\text{ for all }y\in C_A\}.
\]
Consequently $V_A$ carries the structure of a simple Euclidean Jordan algebra
$J_A$ whose cone of squares is $C_A$.  In the reducible case the only additional
possibility supplied by the theorem is a direct sum of isomorphic simple Jordan
blocks, with any surviving block label subject to Primitive Public Irredundancy.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proofsketch}
Theorem~\ref{thm:rd-homogeneous-pure-transitive} supplies homogeneity and pure
transitivity.  Barnum, Ududec, and van de Wetering prove that, under these
finite-dimensional cone hypotheses, self-duality follows from those two physically
transparent properties \cite{barnum-ududec-wetering}.  The Koecher--Vinberg theorem
then identifies homogeneous self-dual cones with cones of squares of Euclidean
Jordan algebras.  Irreducibility of the operational system gives a simple Jordan
factor.  The direct-sum residue in the non-irreducible theorem is precisely a
classical sector label; it survives only when it belongs to the frozen public-record
profile.
\end{proofsketch}

\begin{corollary}[Positive state--effect dagger and reciprocal evidence]
\label{cor:rd-positive-dagger}
Normalize the Jordan trace $\operatorname{tr}_A$ so that every primitive idempotent
has trace one and the deterministic effect is
\[

\[

 p^\dagger(p)=1.
\]
For pure states $p,q$, the transition probability is symmetric:
\[
 p^\dagger(q)
 =
 \langle p,q\rangle_A
 =
 q^\dagger(p).
\]
Thus preparation and recognition are the two orientations of one positive

\end{corollary}

\begin{proofsketch}
The canonical trace form of a Euclidean Jordan algebra is positive definite and

\end{proofsketch}

\begin{corollary}[Positive adjoints and the pure-process dagger]
\label{cor:rd-process-adjoint}
Let $T:V_A\to V_B$ be a positive linear map.  Its adjoint, defined by
\[
 \langle T^\dagger y,x\rangle_A
 =
 \langle y,Tx\rangle_B,
\]
is positive.  Reversible motions have their inverse as adjoint, and the Jordan
quadratic filters $U_a$ are self-adjoint.  Hence the freely sealed pure-process
category generated by reversible motions, sharp compressions, and reversible
filters is dagger closed and supplies the positive dagger presupposed by Isometric
Ledger Invariance.  The statement does not assert that every mathematically positive
map is a primitive physical process.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proofsketch}
If $y\in C_B$ and $x\in C_A$, positivity of $T$ gives
$\langle y,Tx\rangle_B\ge0$.  Self-duality of $C_A$ therefore implies
$T^\dagger y\in C_A$.  Unit-preserving Jordan automorphisms preserve the canonical
trace form and have inverse adjoints, while quadratic representations are
self-adjoint.  Thus the generated pure API is dagger closed without adding an
unlicensed process family.
\end{proofsketch}

\subsection{Spectra, sharp records, and filters}

\begin{theorem}[Jordan spectral theorem for physical states]
\label{thm:rd-spectral}
Every element $a\in J_A$ has a spectral decomposition
\[
 a=\sum_{i=1}^{r_A}\lambda_i p_i,
\]
where $\{p_i\}$ is a Jordan frame of pairwise orthogonal primitive idempotents,
\[
 p_i\circ p_j=0\quad(i\ne j),
 \qquad
 \sum_i p_i=e_A,
\]
and the multiset of eigenvalues $\{\lambda_i\}$ is presentation-independent.  For a
normalized state $\rho$,
\[
 \rho=\sum_i p_i^{(\rho)}\,p_i,
 \qquad
 p_i^{(\rho)}\ge0,
 \qquad
 \sum_i p_i^{(\rho)}=1.
\]
Hence every state is a mixture of perfectly distinguishable pure states.  Effects
are exactly the elements $a$ with $0\le a\le e_A$, and an observable is a spectral
assignment
\[
 X=\sum_i x_i p_i.
\]
The probability and expectation rules are
\[
 \Pr_\rho(i)=\langle p_i,\rho\rangle_A,
 \qquad
 \mathbb E_\rho[X]=\langle X,\rho\rangle_A.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proofsketch}
This is the finite-dimensional spectral theorem for Euclidean Jordan algebras.  The
operational interpretation follows from Corollary~\ref{cor:rd-positive-dagger}:
primitive idempotents are both pure preparations and sharp effects, while
orthogonality makes the effects jointly distinguishable.  Related operational
axiomatics derive the same diagonalization and the associated majorization structure
from purification and pure sharpness \cite{chiribella-scandolo-diagonal}.
\end{proofsketch}

\begin{theorem}[Constructive filter completeness]
\label{thm:rd-filter-completeness}
Let $L_a(x):=a\circ x$ and let
\[
 U_a:=2L_a^2-L_{a^2}
\]
be the Jordan quadratic representation.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)},leftmargin=2.2em]
\item If $a$ is positive and invertible, $U_a$ is a positive cone automorphism with
      inverse $U_{a^{-1}}$.
\item If $p$ is an idempotent, $U_p$ is a positive self-adjoint compression onto the
      face supported by $p$.
\item If $r_A:=\operatorname{tr}_A(e_A)$ and
      $\chi_A=e_A/r_A$ is the invariant neutral state, then every faithful normalized
      state $\rho$ is prepared by the canonical reversible filter
      \[
       \rho
       =
       U_{(r_A\rho)^{1/2}}(\chi_A).
      \]
\end{enumerate}
Thus the abstract accessibility axiom returns a canonical, spectrally constructed
filter calculus.  The spectral dagger/filter-completeness target is closed.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proofsketch}
The quadratic representations of an EJA preserve its cone of squares; invertibility
of $a$ makes $U_a$ an order automorphism.  For an idempotent, $U_p^2=U_p$ and its
image is the Peirce face supported by $p$.  Finally $U_b(e_A)=b^2$, so with
$b=(r_A\rho)^{1/2}$ one obtains
$U_b(e_A/r_A)=\rho$.
\end{proofsketch}

\subsection{Born probabilities, state counts, and interference}

\begin{corollary}[Born trace rule]
\label{cor:rd-born-trace}
For a reconstructed $d$-level system, normalized states are density operators
$\rho\ge0$, $\Tr\rho=1$, and effects satisfy $0\le E\le I$.  The reciprocal
state--effect pairing is
\[
 \boxed{\Pr(E\mid\rho)=\Tr(\rho E).}
\]
For pure rays $\psi,\phi$ and the sharp effect
$E_\phi=|\phi\rangle\!\langle\phi|$,
\[
 \boxed{
 \Pr(\phi\mid\psi)
 =
 \Tr(E_\psi E_\phi)
 =
 |\langle\phi|\psi\rangle|^2.
 }
\]
This gives an operational/Jordan route to the same Born rule as the proof-space
cardinality construction.
\end{corollary}

\begin{corollary}[Exact parameter counts]
\label{cor:rd-parameter-counts}
A $d$-level irreducible system has:

 \end{array}

unnormalized state parameters, then

For $d=2$, this yields a three-parameter mixed-state ball, a two-parameter pure-state

\begin{theorem}[No irreducible interference above second order]
\label{thm:rd-no-higher-interference}
For one fixed additive event algebra in the reconstructed complex quantum theory,
all Sorkin/Moebius interference coefficients of order three or higher vanish:
\[
 \boxed{I_T=0\qquad(|T|\ge3).}
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proofsketch}
For path amplitudes $a_i$ and a subset $S$, the event probability is
\[
 P(S)=\left|\sum_{i\in S}a_i\right|^2
 =\sum_{i,j\in S}a_i\overline{a_j}.
\]
Every monomial depends on at most two path labels.  In the Moebius coefficient
\[
 I_T=\sum_{S\subseteq T}(-1)^{|T|-|S|}P(S),
\]
inclusion--exclusion annihilates every term once $|T|\ge3$.  Jordan-algebraic
operational theories likewise admit no genuine higher-than-second-order
interference under their projective filter calculus
\cite{barnum-hilgert-jordan,barnum-muller-ududec}.
\end{proofsketch}
