\section{Primitive Public Irredundancy and Finite Physical Closure}
\label{sec:primitive-public-irredundancy}

Primitive-Parameter Provenance closes positive-dimensional realization moduli, but
it deliberately leaves finite multiplicities, finite quotients, and isolated hidden
state directions untouched.  The wider philosophy suggests a distinct and more
structural completion.  The Constitutive Law requires witness-bearing structure and
identifies equivalent presentations; the Selective Law says ``nothing without
demand,'' refuses self-issued receipts, and freely realizes a selected API with no
hidden surplus.  At the physical face, actuality is carried by stable public records.
The common content is not numerical parsimony.  It is that a primitive distinction
must be answerable to an independently fixed role in determinate history.

\begin{definition}[Frozen required-record profile]
Let \(\mathcal S\) be a candidate primitive physical API over public history \(H\).
Its \emph{frozen required-record profile}
\[
  \mathsf{Req}_H(\mathcal S)
\]
is generated, before any proposed surplus primitive is inspected, by:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{R\arabic*},leftmargin=2.4em]
\item records already locked into \(H\);
\item records explicitly demanded by the selected carrier-bearing API and its
      typed interaction obligations;
\item comparison, decoding, and nondemolition-refinement data required by Internal
      Record Actualization and Public Confluence; and
\item records forced by composition, duality, transport, and public descent from
      R1--R3.
\end{enumerate}
A record whose only justification is that an additional primitive would make that
record possible is not in the frozen profile.  A primitive may not pose the only
question it answers.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Record-conservative quotient]
A map of physical realizations
\[
  q:\mathcal S\longrightarrow\mathcal S'
\]
is a \emph{record-conservative quotient} when it removes or identifies primitive
data and every \(R\in\mathsf{Req}_H(\mathcal S)\) factors through \(q\), uniquely up
to public operational equivalence, while preserving the declared decoder,
tolerance, lock-in, and confluence maps.  Thus the quotient changes no inherited or
independently demanded public fact.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Idle primitive distinction]
A primitive state direction, generator, carrier copy, sector label, symmetry
element, or equation is \emph{idle} when it is removed or identified by a
record-conservative quotient that is not an equivalence.
\end{definition}

\begin{axiom}[Primitive Public Irredundancy --- no idle primitive distinction]
\label{ax:primitive-public-irredundancy}
Every record-conservative quotient of a completed primitive physical seal is an
equivalence:
\[
 \boxed{
 q:\mathcal S\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal S',
 \quad
 q\text{ preserves }\mathsf{Req}_H(\mathcal S)
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 q\text{ is an equivalence}.}
\]
Equivalently, every primitive distinction in a completed physical API is necessary
for at least one inherited or independently demanded public record.  Every primitive
distinction must do indispensable public work.
\end{axiom}

\begin{remark}[Placement and independence]
The axiom belongs to the actualization/integration face of the Selective Law.  It
does not compare two complete payments by a score, cardinality, or simplicity
order.  It states a universal irreducibility property relative to a profile fixed
before candidate inspection.  Free sealing is initiality among realizations of an
already selected API; Primitive Public Irredundancy additionally constrains whether
the API itself contains an unforced primitive distinction.  Neither principle
implies the other.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Not operational positivism]
``Public'' means internally recordable in principle by an admissible context, not
observed by present human instruments.  A dark carrier that changes a gravitational,
gauge, or other admissible record is not idle.  Topological, edge, and nonlocal
records count when they are forced by the frozen API.  The axiom excludes only a
primitive difference whose removal preserves every independently licensed record.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Historical alternatives are protected]
A lawfully actualized branch index is inherited history and therefore belongs to R1,
even if bounded later observers forget it.  The axiom does not collapse the
Stage-4 cone or replace the Selective Law's certified silence.  It constrains surplus
primitive content of a completed physical API, not finite indexical facts already
sealed into history.
\end{remark}

\begin{proposition}[Joint public separation]
\label{prop:joint-public-separation}
The required record family jointly separates primitive states, processes, and
symmetry actions.  In particular, for primitive states \(\omega,\omega'\) of a system
\(A\),
\[
 \bigl( R(\omega)=R(\omega')\text{ for every }R\in\mathsf{Req}_H(A)\bigr)
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \omega=\omega'
\]
up to the public equivalence already quotiented in the API.  The analogous statement
holds for processes after quantifying over required preparations and effects.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proofsketch}
If two primitive states agree on every required record, quotient the state object by
the equivalence relation identifying them.  Every required record factors through
the quotient.  If the states were genuinely distinct, this would be a proper
record-conservative quotient, contradicting
Axiom~\ref{ax:primitive-public-irredundancy}.  The process statement uses the same
argument on the process object.
\end{proofsketch}

\begin{corollary}[Projective pure states]
\label{cor:projective-pure-states}
Once the Born event interface is sealed, a common phase of a nonzero pure vector does
no public work:
\[
  \psi\longmapsto e^{i\theta}\psi
\]
leaves every event weight and every later unitary interference record invariant.
Primitive Public Irredundancy therefore quotients the common phase, while retaining
relative phase.  The physical pure-state space is
\[
  \mathbb P(\mathcal H)
  = (\mathcal H\setminus\{0\})/\C^\times,
\]
and normalized pure states form complex projective space
\(\C P^{d-1}\), not the unit sphere itself.  Connected reversible dynamics acts
projectively, through \(PU(d)=U(d)/U(1)\).
\end{corollary}

\begin{proofsketch}
Born records depend on projectors
\(\lvert\psi\rangle\!\langle\psi\rvert\), which are invariant under common phase.
The quotient by common phase is therefore record-conservative and must already be
built into the completed primitive state API.  Relative phases affect interference
records and cannot be quotiented.
\end{proofsketch}

\subsection{Public descent and the complex quantum field}

\begin{definition}[Record-complete ordinary composite]
A composite \(AB\) is \emph{record-complete ordinary} when its frozen required-record
profile is generated by compatible local records and their product refinements,
and no independent gauge, gravitational, topological, or edge record lives on the
overlap.  This definition states the jurisdiction of ordinary local tomography; it
does not impose that jurisdiction on sectors carrying genuine overlap data.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Local tomography from Primitive Public Irredundancy]
\label{thm:ppi-local-tomography}
Every finite-dimensional record-complete ordinary composite is locally tomographic:
\[
 \left[
 (e_A\otimes e_B)(\omega)
 =
 (e_A\otimes e_B)(\omega')
 \quad\text{for all required }e_A,e_B
 \right]
 \Longrightarrow
 \omega=\omega'.
\]
Under the standard nondegenerate composition hypotheses, if \(K_A\) is the real
dimension of the unnormalized state space, then
\[
  K_{AB}=K_AK_B.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proofsketch}
Let \(N\) be the common kernel of all product effects on the composite state space.
The quotient \(V_{AB}\to V_{AB}/N\) preserves every record in the frozen profile by
the definition of a record-complete ordinary composite.  Primitive Public
Irredundancy forces \(N=0\), proving separation.  Product preparations and effects
supply the usual tensor lower bound; separation supplies the upper bound, giving
dimension multiplicativity.
\end{proofsketch}

\begin{proposition}[Real, complex, and quaternionic dimension diagnostic]
\label{prop:scalar-field-diagnostic}
For a \(d\)-level matrix system, the real dimensions of the self-adjoint state spaces
are
\[
 K_{\R}(d)=\frac{d(d+1)}2,
 \qquad
 K_{\C}(d)=d^2,
 \qquad
 K_{\HH}(d)=d(2d-1).
\]
Consequently:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
 & K(2)^2 & K(4)\\
\hline
\R & 9 & 10\\
\C & 16 & 16\\
\HH & 36 & 28
\end{array}
\]
The standard complex composite satisfies local tomography.  Standard real and
quaternionic matrix composites do not.  In the two-rebit case the one extra real
global direction can be represented by the
\(\sigma_y\otimes\sigma_y\) correlation, although neither local rebit admits
\(\sigma_y\) as a real-symmetric observable.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proofsketch}
Real self-adjoint matrices are real symmetric, giving
\(d+d(d-1)/2\) parameters.  Complex Hermitian matrices have \(d^2\) real parameters.
Quaternionic self-adjoint matrices have \(d\) real diagonal entries and four real
parameters for each off-diagonal pair, giving
\(d+4\binom d2=d(2d-1)\).  Compare the composite dimension at \(d=4\) with the
product of the two local \(d=2\) dimensions.  The real-theory mismatch is the
standard limited-holism obstruction \cite{hardy-wootters-real}.
\end{proofsketch}

\begin{theorem}[Conditional complex-quantum reconstruction]
\label{thm:conditional-complex-reconstruction-ppi}
Assume:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{Q\arabic*},leftmargin=2.4em]
\item the Genesis-to-Jordan target: finite irreducible systems have homogeneous
      self-dual cones and Euclidean Jordan state spaces;
\item the selected \(SU(2)\)-carrier, after the Hilbert-functional seal, is identified
      operationally with a qubit; and
\item composites satisfy the ordinary record-completeness hypotheses of
      Theorem~\ref{thm:ppi-local-tomography}.
\end{enumerate}
Then every finite system is the self-adjoint part of a finite-dimensional complex
matrix algebra, with possible classical superselection between irreducible blocks.
For irreducible systems the state algebra is
\[
  H_n(\C)=M_n(\C)_{\mathrm{sa}}.
\]
Thus, conditional on Q1 and the small carrier-to-qubit lemma Q2, Primitive Public
Irredundancy closes the public-descent target and removes the real and quaternionic
quantum alternatives in ordinary sectors.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proofsketch}
Theorem~\ref{thm:ppi-local-tomography} supplies local tomography.  Under Q1, the
systems are Jordan-algebraic, and Q2 supplies a qubit.  The Hanche--Olsen theorem in
the operational form established by Barnum and Wilce then identifies the finite
locally tomographic theory with complex quantum theory, up to superselection
\cite{barnum-wilce-local}.  Primitive Public Irredundancy supplies one of the three
load-bearing hypotheses; it does not prove Q1 or Q2.  Isometric Ledger Invariance
is adopted in Section~\ref{sec:isometric-ledger-invariance}; once the positive
dagger required by Q1 is available, free environment completion yields the usual
completely positive mixed-process category.
\end{proofsketch}

\begin{remark}[Why gravity and gauge sectors are not over-quotiented]
The theorem does not assert ordinary local tomography across a cut carrying Gauss-law
flux, edge modes, topology, or gravitational dressing.  Such data are independently
required overlap records and therefore belong to the frozen profile.  The conclusion
there is public descent with the overlap record included, not naive factorization.
\end{remark}

\begin{theorem}[Carrier-generated effective gauge presentation]
\label{thm:ppi-z6-global-form}
On the carrier-generated probe jurisdiction, Primitive Public Irredundancy selects
\[
 \boxed{
 G_{\mathrm{phys}}^{\mathrm{gen}}
 =
 \frac{SU(3)\times SU(2)\times U(1)_y}{\mathbb Z_6}
 \cong S(U(3)\times U(2)).}
\]
Here
\[
 \mathbb Z_6
 =
 \left\{(z^2I_3,z^3I_2,z):z^6=1\right\}

is the exact kernel already computed from the sealed matter and Higgs carriers.
Every admitted irrep therefore satisfies
\[
 2t+3d+y\equiv0\pmod 6,

where \(t\) is color triality, \(d\) is weak parity, and \(y=6Y\) is primitive

\end{theorem}

\begin{proofsketch}
Every kernel element acts trivially on each selected carrier.  It therefore acts
trivially on every probe generated functorially from those carriers by the listed
operations.  For any
\(\Gamma\subsetneq\mathbb Z_6\), the further quotient

 G_0/\Gamma\longrightarrow G_0/\mathbb Z_6

\end{proofsketch}

\end{remark}

\begin{corollary}[No spectator gauge generator]
A primitive gauge factor or generator acting trivially on every selected carrier and

\subsection{Flavor multiplicity and the exact remaining premise}

\begin{proposition}[Conditional three-generation theorem]
\label{prop:ppi-three-generations}
Suppose the frozen flavor profile requires exactly one independent rephasing-invariant
CP-odd orientation record and no further primitive flavor-orientation record.  For
\(N\) Dirac generations the number of physical mixing phases is
\[
 n_{\mathrm{CP}}(N)=\frac{(N-1)(N-2)}2.

Primitive Public Irredundancy then gives
\[
 n_{\mathrm{CP}}(N)=1
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \boxed{N=3}.

\end{proposition}

\begin{proofsketch}
For \(N\le2\), no CP-odd phase exists and the required orientation record cannot be

\begin{remark}[The orientation premise is not yet derived]
The temporal orientation of a history does not by itself force microscopic CP or
T violation: a time-oriented macroscopic record can arise in a microscopically
T-symmetric theory from boundary data and coarse-graining.  The Two Laws therefore
do not yet prove that the flavor interface owes exactly one CP-odd record.  The new
axiom converts the generation problem into this single jurisdiction question; it
does not conceal it.
\end{remark}

\section{Isometric Ledger Invariance and the Quantum Process Completion}
\label{sec:isometric-ledger-invariance}

Primitive-Parameter Provenance removes unexplained continuous law dials, and
Primitive Public Irredundancy removes primitive distinctions that perform no
independently required public work.  One gap remains between the pure
Hilbert-functional layer and the ordinary process theory of physics.  A carrier may
be enlarged or recoded without losing any of its encoded state, while the public
discarding account nevertheless changes.  The nonnegative-matrix countermodel in
the reconstruction audit shows that this mismatch is not repaired by Internal
Record Actualization, Public Confluence, monoidal discarding, free sealing, or the
two preceding closure axioms.

The wider philosophy points to one further clause.  Equivalence-invariance says
that redescription does not create a second reality.  Lossless abstraction says
that a sealed interior may be moved behind another interface without being
renegotiated.  Record lock-in says that an admissible comparison may not silently
alter an already established fact.  Applied to physical carriers, the common
content is:
\begin{center}
\emph{A lossless recoding may change where information is carried.  It may not
change whether the information counts, nor create or destroy total public weight.}
\end{center}

\begin{definition}[Dagger-lossless recoding]
Let \(P\) be the sealed pure-process category after a positive dagger has been
constructed.  A pure map
\[
 u:E\longrightarrow E'
\]
is a \emph{dagger-lossless recoding}, or isometry, when
\[
 u^\dagger u=\mathrm{id}_E.
\]
It may enlarge the carrier, since \(uu^\dagger\) need not equal
\(\mathrm{id}_{E'}\), but every encoded state is recovered on the image by
\(u^\dagger\).
\end{definition}

\begin{axiom}[Isometric Ledger Invariance]
\label{ax:isometric-ledger-invariance}
In every completed physical realization with public discard effects
\(\varepsilon_E:E\to I\), each dagger-lossless recoding is causal:
\[
 \boxed{
 u^\dagger u=\mathrm{id}_E
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \varepsilon_{E'}\circ u=\varepsilon_E.}
\]
Thus enlarging or re-encoding a carrier without losing any encoded distinction
cannot change its total public account.
\end{axiom}

\begin{remark}[Blank-extension form]
In finite-dimensional complex quantum theory, unitary invariance together with
Axiom~\ref{ax:isometric-ledger-invariance} is equivalently expressed by blank-ancilla
neutrality.  For every normalized blank state \(\eta:I\to F\),
\[
 \varepsilon_{E\otimes F}\circ
 (\mathrm{id}_E\otimes\eta)
 =\varepsilon_E.
\]
A larger unused carrier is not an additional event.  The isometric form is stronger
and presentation-independent: it does not depend on choosing a particular
factorization of the enlarged carrier into ``old system plus blank.''
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Placement, independence, and jurisdiction]
The axiom belongs to the actualization/integration face of the Selective Law.  It
does not rank candidate APIs or choose a numerical normalization.  It states which
pure recodings count as the same lossless public process once a dagger and discard
interface are present.  It presupposes the positive dagger targeted by the
Genesis-to-Jordan programme and therefore does not derive that dagger.

The separation countermodel remains load-bearing.  In nonnegative real matrices
with transpose dagger and \(\varepsilon_n=(1,\ldots,1)\), the column
\(u=2^{-1/2}(1,1)^{\mathsf T}:1\to2\) obeys \(u^\dagger u=1\), while
\(\varepsilon_2u=\sqrt2\ne1=\varepsilon_1\).  Hence dagger-losslessness and
ledger-losslessness are genuinely different notions.  The present axiom identifies
them as a physical requirement rather than pretending that the earlier clauses
already did so.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Closing the environment-completion target}

\begin{theorem}[The isometric environment class is lawful]
\label{thm:isometric-environment-class}
Let \(P\) be an essentially small dagger symmetric monoidal pure category.  Write
\[
 \operatorname{Iso}(P)
 =\{u:E\to E':u^\dagger u=\mathrm{id}_E\}.
\]
Then \(\operatorname{Iso}(P)\) is a wide symmetric monoidal class, and under
Axiom~\ref{ax:isometric-ledger-invariance} every member is causal in the physical
discarding realization.  Consequently the selected environment completion is
\[
 \boxed{\operatorname{Env}_{\operatorname{Iso}}(P),}
\]
not the collapsing completion obtained by identifying environments along arbitrary
maps.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proofsketch}
Identities are isometries.  If \(u\) and \(v\) are isometries, then
\((vu)^\dagger(vu)=u^\dagger v^\dagger vu=\mathrm{id}\), and
\((u\otimes v)^\dagger(u\otimes v)=\mathrm{id}\); hence the class is wide and
monoidal.  Ledger invariance makes every such embedding causal, exactly the
selection condition needed by the already proved free-environment universal
property.  Identifying along all maps is still forbidden by the zero-object
collapse theorem; the adopted class retains precisely lossless enlargements.
\end{proofsketch}

\begin{theorem}[Free environment completion and no fundamental mixing]
\label{thm:free-environment-completion-closed}
Under Theorem~\ref{thm:isometric-environment-class}, the mixed operational category
is the free isometric environment completion of the sealed pure category.  Every
process \(A\to B\) has a representative
\[
 [E,f],
 \qquad
 f:A\longrightarrow B\otimes E
\]
with \(f\) pure, followed by discarding \(E\).  In particular every normalized
mixed state of \(A\) has a pure dilation
\[
 \psi:I\longrightarrow A\otimes E.
\]
Two dilations represent the same mixed process exactly when they are related by the
equivalence generated by isometric environment embeddings.  Hence mixing is not a
second primitive state-former: it is pure correlation viewed through a discarded
interface.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proofsketch}
The relative free-sealing theorem already proves that
\(\operatorname{Env}_J(P)\) is initial among exact \(J\)-realizations.  Theorem
\ref{thm:isometric-environment-class} now supplies the previously missing lawful
choice \(J=\operatorname{Iso}(P)\).  The representation and the general zigzag
uniqueness are the defining universal property of that completion.  No separate
mixed generator is present in the free seal.
\end{proofsketch}

\begin{remark}[General versus Hilbert-space uniqueness]
In an arbitrary pure category, uniqueness is only the generated zigzag of
isometric embeddings.  A statement that two purifications are connected by one
reversible map needs an additional common-cocone or minimality theorem.  In
finite-dimensional Hilbert spaces, minimal Stinespring dilations supply that
theorem: minimal environments are unitarily equivalent, and arbitrary dilations
factor isometrically through a minimal one.
\end{remark}

\subsection{The complex realization is the completely positive completion}

\begin{theorem}[Environment completion equals the CPM construction]
\label{thm:env-cpm-equivalence}
Assume the conditional complex-kinematic closure obtained from
Genesis-to-Jordan, the carrier-to-qubit lemma, and Primitive Public Irredundancy,
so that the irreducible pure category is finite-dimensional complex Hilbert space
with linear maps.  Define
\[
 \mathfrak D([E,f])(X)
 :=
 \operatorname{Tr}_E\!\bigl(fXf^\dagger\bigr).
\]
Then \(\mathfrak D\) is a symmetric monoidal equivalence
\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname{Env}_{\operatorname{Iso}}(\mathbf{FHilb})
 \simeq
 \mathbf{CPM}(\mathbf{FHilb}),}
\]
where the right-hand side has finite-dimensional Hilbert spaces as objects and
completely positive maps as morphisms \cite{selinger-cpm,coecke-channels}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proofsketch}
If \(f'=(\mathrm{id}_B\otimes u)f\) for an isometry \(u:E\to E'\), then
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}_{E'}
 \left[(\mathrm{id}\otimes u)fXf^\dagger
       (\mathrm{id}\otimes u^\dagger)\right]
 =\operatorname{Tr}_E(fXf^\dagger),
\]
so the map is well defined on environment classes.  It is completely positive by
construction.  Conversely, if
\(\Phi(X)=\sum_iK_iXK_i^\dagger\), choose an environment basis \(\{|i\rangle\}\)
and set
\[
 f=\sum_i K_i\otimes|i\rangle;
\]
then \(\Phi=\mathfrak D([E,f])\).  Stinespring uniqueness identifies any two
dilations of the same completely positive map through their common minimal
dilation, exactly the equivalence generated by environment isometries
\cite{stinespring,selinger-cpm}.
\end{proofsketch}

\begin{corollary}[Causal processes are quantum channels]
\label{cor:cptp-processes}
A process represented by \(f:A\to B\otimes E\) is causal precisely when
\[
 f^\dagger f=\mathrm{id}_A.
\]
Choosing an orthonormal basis of \(E\) and defining
\[
 K_i=(\mathrm{id}_B\otimes\langle i|)f
\]
gives the Kraus representation
\[
 \boxed{
 \Phi(\rho)=\sum_iK_i\rho K_i^\dagger,
 \qquad
 \sum_iK_i^\dagger K_i=\mathrm{id}_A.}
\]
Thus deterministic finite ordinary processes are completely positive and
trace-preserving.  Probabilistic branches are completely positive and
trace-nonincreasing, and an instrument is a family of such branches whose sum is a
channel.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proofsketch}
For every trace-class \(X\),
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}\mathfrak D([E,f])(X)
 =\operatorname{Tr}(Xf^\dagger f).
\]
Equality with \(\operatorname{Tr}X\) for all \(X\) is therefore equivalent to
\(f^\dagger f=\mathrm{id}_A\).  Expanding \(f\) in an environment basis gives the
Kraus equations.
\end{proofsketch}

\begin{corollary}[Finite environmental bound]
For \(d_A=\dim A\) and \(d_B=\dim B\), every process has a dilation with
\[
 \dim E\le d_Ad_B.
\]
The least possible environment dimension is the rank of the Choi matrix
\(J_\Phi\), equivalently the least Kraus number.  Minimal dilations are unique up to
a unitary on \(E\) \cite{stinespring,choi-cp}.
\end{corollary}

\begin{corollary}[No primitive deterministic nonlinear update]
On density operators, every deterministic finite ordinary update is affine:
\[
 \Phi(p\rho+(1-p)\sigma)
 =p\Phi(\rho)+(1-p)\Phi(\sigma).
\]
Nonlinear normalization may occur only after conditioning on a particular
non-deterministic record branch.  It is not an additional deterministic law of the
unconditioned state.
\end{corollary}

\subsection{Closed dynamics, measurements, and locality}

\begin{theorem}[Closed finite-system dynamics is unitary]
\label{thm:closed-unitary}
A pure causal process with no discarded environment is an isometry
\[
 U^\dagger U=\mathrm{id}.
\]
For evolution of a fixed finite-dimensional system type, \(U\) is unitary.  If the
closed reversible updates form a continuous one-parameter group, then
\[
 U_t=e^{-itH},
 \qquad H=H^\dagger,
\]
and the state equations are
\[
 i\frac{d}{dt}|\psi_t\rangle=H|\psi_t\rangle,
 \qquad
 \frac{d\rho_t}{dt}=-i[H,\rho_t].
\]
Thus nonunitary deterministic evolution is an open-system description arising from
coupling to, and then discarding, an environment; it is not primitive closed-system
dynamics.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proofsketch}
Corollary~\ref{cor:cptp-processes} with \(E=I\) gives
\(U^\dagger U=\mathrm{id}\).  In equal finite dimensions an isometry is unitary.
The continuous-group statement is the finite-dimensional form of Stone's theorem.
\end{proofsketch}


\begin{corollary}[Scattering unitarity and the optical theorem]
\label{cor:optical-theorem-ledger}
Assume asymptotic completeness, so the scattering map is a reversible process on
one complete asymptotic Hilbert space.  Then
\[
 S^\dagger S=\mathrm{id}.
\]
Writing \(S=\mathrm{id}+iT\) gives
\[
 \boxed{-i(T-T^\dagger)=T^\dagger T.}
\]
For an initial channel \(\lvert i\rangle\),
\[
 \boxed{
 2\,\operatorname{Im}T_{ii}
 =\sum_f\lvert T_{fi}\rvert^2,}
\]
where the sum includes the appropriate continuum phase-space measure.  The
absorptive part of the forward amplitude and total transition probability are thus
two presentations of one conserved process ledger.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proofsketch}
Expand \((\mathrm{id}-iT^\dagger)(\mathrm{id}+iT)=\mathrm{id}\) and take the
\((i,i)\) matrix element.  The assumption doing physical work is asymptotic
completeness: omitted stable channels must be included before the displayed sum is a
complete ledger.
\end{proofsketch}

\begin{theorem}[Public measurement dilation and the general Born rule]
\label{thm:instrument-dilation}
Every finite quantum instrument \(\{\Phi_x\}_x\) has an isometric realization by a
pure system--pointer--environment coupling.  If
\[
 \Phi_x(\rho)=\sum_\alpha M_{x\alpha}\rho M_{x\alpha}^\dagger,
 \qquad
 \sum_{x,\alpha}M_{x\alpha}^\dagger M_{x\alpha}=\mathrm{id},
\]
then
\[
 V
 =
 \sum_{x,\alpha}
 M_{x\alpha}\otimes|x\rangle_C\otimes|\alpha\rangle_E
\]
is an isometry.  Reading the stable pointer \(x\) and discarding \(E\) gives the
instrument, with
\[
 p(x\mid\rho)
 =\operatorname{Tr}(\rho E_x),
 \qquad
 E_x=\sum_\alpha M_{x\alpha}^\dagger M_{x\alpha},
 \qquad
 \sum_xE_x=\mathrm{id}.
\]
Hence the projective Born rule extends to every finite POVM, and a public
measurement is pure coupling plus pointer actualization plus permitted discard.  A
particular conditioned update is not fixed by the POVM alone.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proofsketch}
The displayed completeness relation gives \(V^\dagger V=\mathrm{id}\).  Projecting
onto the pointer value \(x\), discarding the unresolved index \(\alpha\), and using
the Born pairing yields the stated probability and branch map.  This is the finite
Naimark--Stinespring realization of a quantum instrument.
\end{proofsketch}

\begin{theorem}[No signalling by local deterministic operations]
\label{thm:no-local-signalling}
For a bipartite ordinary sector and a local channel \(\Phi_A\),
\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname{Tr}_A
 \bigl[(\Phi_A\otimes\mathrm{id}_B)(\rho_{AB})\bigr]
 =\operatorname{Tr}_A\rho_{AB}.}
\]
Therefore choosing and applying a local deterministic operation cannot by itself
change the remote public state.  Conditioning on a communicated outcome may steer
the remote conditional description, but the unconditioned statistics do not
signal.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proofsketch}
Using Kraus operators and cyclicity of the partial trace,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \operatorname{Tr}_A
 \sum_i(K_i\otimes I)\rho(K_i^\dagger\otimes I)
 &=
 \operatorname{Tr}_A
 \left[\rho\left(\sum_iK_i^\dagger K_i\otimes I\right)\right]\\
 &=\operatorname{Tr}_A\rho.
 \end{aligned}
\]
\end{proofsketch}

\begin{corollary}[Public processing cannot manufacture distinguishability]
For every channel \(\Phi\), trace distance contracts:
\[
 \frac12\|\Phi(\rho)-\Phi(\sigma)\|_1
 \le
 \frac12\|\rho-\sigma\|_1.
\]
Thus a deterministic public process can preserve or lose operationally available
distinctions, but cannot create evidence distinguishing two inputs that was absent
before the process.
\end{corollary}


\begin{theorem}[No information without disturbance]
\label{thm:no-information-without-disturbance-ledger}
Let an isometric interaction leave two pure system states unchanged while exporting
an environment record:
\[
 V\lvert\psi\rangle
 =\lvert\psi\rangle\lvert e_\psi\rangle,
 \qquad
 V\lvert\phi\rangle
 =\lvert\phi\rangle\lvert e_\phi\rangle.
\]
Then
\[
 \langle\psi\mid\phi\rangle
 =\langle\psi\mid\phi\rangle
  \langle e_\psi\mid e_\phi\rangle.
\]
If the inputs are nonorthogonal and distinct, the environment states coincide up to
phase.  A record that distinguishes nonorthogonal alternatives therefore cannot be
created while leaving both alternatives undisturbed.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proofsketch}
An isometry preserves inner products.  If
\(\langle\psi\mid\phi\rangle\ne0\), division gives
\(\langle e_\psi\mid e_\phi\rangle=1\).  Hence no admissible environment
measurement distinguishes the two exported states.  This is the elementary
Hilbert-space instance of the information--disturbance consequences of purification
\cite{chiribella-purification}.
\end{proofsketch}

\begin{corollary}[No exact arbitrary-state cloning]
\label{cor:no-cloning-ledger}
If one isometry cloned both \(\lvert\psi\rangle\) and
\(\lvert\phi\rangle\), inner-product preservation would give
\[
 \langle\psi\mid\phi\rangle
 =\langle\psi\mid\phi\rangle^2.
\]
Thus a family is exactly clonable only when its distinct members are orthogonal.
There is no universal exact cloning process for quantum pure states.
\end{corollary}

\subsection{Classical records, erasure, and open-system evolution}

\begin{theorem}[No erasure without export]
\label{thm:no-erasure-without-export}
Suppose a channel resets two orthogonal pure inputs to the same pure system state:
\[
 \Phi(|0\rangle\!\langle0|)
 =
 \Phi(|1\rangle\!\langle1|)
 =|s\rangle\!\langle s|.
\]
In every isometric dilation \(V\) there are orthogonal environment states
\(|e_0\rangle,|e_1\rangle\) such that
\[
 V|0\rangle=|s\rangle\otimes|e_0\rangle,
 \qquad
 V|1\rangle=|s\rangle\otimes|e_1\rangle,
 \qquad
 \langle e_0|e_1\rangle=0.
\]
The distinguishing record has been exported, not destroyed.  A quantitative
Landauer cost requires an additional thermal/KMS state, Hamiltonian, and temperature
calibration; Ledger Invariance alone does not derive \(k_BT\ln2\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proofsketch}
A pure reduced output forces each dilated output to factor through \(|s\rangle\).
The isometry preserves the orthogonality of the two inputs, so
\(0=\langle0|1\rangle=\langle e_0|e_1\rangle\).
\end{proofsketch}


\begin{proposition}[Entanglement account of mixedness]
\label{prop:entanglement-account-mixedness}
For a pure bipartite dilation \(\lvert\Psi\rangle_{BE}\), the reduced states
\(\rho_B\) and \(\rho_E\) have the same nonzero spectrum.  Consequently
\[
 \boxed{S(\rho_B)=S(\rho_E).}
\]
The entropy of a reduced state is therefore an exact account of correlation with an
omitted interface in the pure global description, rather than evidence that the
complete process destroyed information.
\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem}[Conditional Landauer equality]
\label{thm:conditional-landauer-ledger}
Assume in addition a memory \(S\), a reservoir initially in the Gibbs state
\(\rho_R=e^{-\beta H_R}/Z\), no initial system--reservoir correlation, and a joint
unitary interaction.  Define the entropy decrease and reservoir heat by
\[
 \Delta S=S(\rho_S)-S(\rho'_S),
 \qquad
 \Delta Q=\operatorname{Tr}\!\left[H_R(\rho'_R-\rho_R)\right].
\]
Then
\[
 \boxed{
 \beta\Delta Q
 =\Delta S+I(S':R')+D(\rho'_R\Vert\rho_R)
 \ge\Delta S.}
\]
For erasure of a maximally mixed bit to one pure standard state,
\[
 \boxed{\Delta Q\ge k_BT\ln2.}
\]
The mutual-information and relative-entropy terms record the finite-reservoir and
correlation overhead beyond the ideal bound.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proofsketch}
The process completion supplies the unitary dilation and information conservation,
but not the Gibbs state, Hamiltonian, temperature, or energy calibration.  Under
those additional thermal premises, unitary entropy conservation and the identity
relating a Gibbs state's relative entropy to its energy change give the displayed
exact equality; positivity of mutual information and relative entropy gives the
inequality \cite{reeb-wolf-landauer}.
\end{proofsketch}

\begin{theorem}[Copyable public records are classical]
\label{thm:no-broadcasting-records}
In the complex completely positive completion, a set of density operators can be
broadcast by one physical channel exactly only when the operators commute
\cite{barnum-broadcasting}.  Consequently a family of repeatable, freely copyable
public records is represented by a commutative pointer algebra.  Noncommuting
quantum states may condition records, but cannot themselves be duplicated as
public facts.
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}[Why this fits Public Confluence]
Public Confluence asks compatible records to admit a common nondemolition
refinement.  The no-broadcasting theorem identifies the maximal freely repeatable
part of the complex quantum interface as classical.  Classicality is therefore not
a second substance inserted beside the quantum system; it is the commutative record
surface on which repeated public comparison is possible.
\end{remark}

\begin{theorem}[Markovian open dynamics has GKSL form]
\label{thm:gksl-conditional}
Let \(\{\Phi_t\}_{t\ge0}\) be a norm-continuous, time-homogeneous Markov semigroup of
finite-dimensional deterministic processes:
\[
 \Phi_{t+s}=\Phi_t\Phi_s,
 \qquad
 \Phi_0=\mathrm{id}.
\]
Since each \(\Phi_t\) is CPTP, its generator has the
Gorini--Kossakowski--Sudarshan--Lindblad form
\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal L(\rho)
 =-i[H,\rho]
 +\sum_j
 \left(
 L_j\rho L_j^\dagger
 -\frac12\{L_j^\dagger L_j,\rho\}
 \right).}
\]
Conversely every generator of this form produces a CPTP semigroup
\cite{gks,lindblad}.  Ledger Invariance derives the CPTP jurisdiction; the semigroup,
Markov, and time-homogeneity assumptions are additional dynamical hypotheses.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{A quantitative correlation consequence}

\begin{theorem}[Tsirelson bound from the completed ordinary quantum package]
\label{thm:tsirelson-closure}
Assume the conditional complex-kinematic theorem supplied by
Genesis-to-Jordan, the qubit-carrier lemma, and Primitive Public Irredundancy,
together with the Born rule and the process completion above.  For dichotomic local
observables \(A_0,A_1,B_0,B_1\) with square equal to the identity, the CHSH
operator
\[
 \mathcal C
 =A_0\otimes(B_0+B_1)
  +A_1\otimes(B_0-B_1)
\]
satisfies
\[
 \boxed{|\langle\mathcal C\rangle|\le2\sqrt2.}
\]
The bound is attained by a maximally entangled qubit pair and appropriate Pauli
measurements, so the package predicts the exact quantum correlation ceiling rather
than merely relativistic no-signalling \cite{tsirelson}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proofsketch}
Using \(A_i^2=B_j^2=I\),
\[
 \mathcal C^2
 =4I-[A_0,A_1]\otimes[B_0,B_1].
\]
Each dichotomic observable has operator norm one, so each commutator has norm at
most two.  Hence \(\|\mathcal C^2\|\le8\), therefore
\(\|\mathcal C\|\le2\sqrt2\), and every Born expectation is bounded by the
operator norm.
\end{proofsketch}


\subsection{Gauge recoding and anomaly cancellation}

\begin{principle}[Gauge transformations are presentation recodings]
A gauge transformation changes the internal representative of a field configuration
without changing the physical configuration.  Once the gauge API has been selected,
it is therefore a unitary lossless recoding rather than an additional physical
alternative.  Its full quantum realization must preserve every public amplitude and
record, not merely the classical equations.
\end{principle}

\begin{theorem}[Gauge-anomaly exclusion]
\label{thm:gauge-anomaly-exclusion-ledger}
A fundamental gauge symmetry is admissible only when its quantum realization is
non-anomalous:
\[
 Z[A^g]=Z[A],
 \qquad
 \Gamma[A^g]=\Gamma[A].
\]
For four-dimensional left-handed Weyl matter, all perturbative gauge and mixed
gauge--gravitational anomaly coefficients must vanish, including
\[
 \sum_f
 \operatorname{Tr}_{R_f}
 \bigl(T^a\{T^b,T^c\}\bigr)=0.
\]
A nontrivial fermion-measure Jacobian would make a supposed presentation recoding
change public amplitudes and is therefore incompatible with equivalence-invariance
and Isometric Ledger Invariance.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proofsketch}
Fujikawa's construction identifies a chiral anomaly with the regulated Jacobian of
the fermionic functional measure \cite{fujikawa-measure}.  When the transformation
is a gauge redundancy, a nontrivial Jacobian makes gauge-equivalent representatives
physically inequivalent, so the candidate quantum theory does not exactly realize
its own gauge API.  Under the usual perturbative gauge-theory hypotheses, one-loop
cancellation can be maintained to all orders by the Adler--Bardeen theorem
\cite{adler-bardeen-anomaly}.
\end{proofsketch}

\begin{proposition}[Standard Model anomaly checksum]
\label{prop:sm-anomaly-checksum-ledger}
For the sequentially sealed all-left-handed generation
\[
 Q:(\mathbf3,\mathbf2)_{1/6},\quad
 u^c:(\overline{\mathbf3},\mathbf1)_{-2/3},\quad
 d^c:(\overline{\mathbf3},\mathbf1)_{1/3},
\]
\[
 L:(\mathbf1,\mathbf2)_{-1/2},\quad
 e^c:(\mathbf1,\mathbf1)_1,\quad
 \nu^c:(\mathbf1,\mathbf1)_0,
\]
the local anomaly sums vanish generation by generation:
\[
 [SU(3)]^3:\quad 2-1-1=0,
\]
\[
 [SU(3)]^2U(1):\quad
 2\!\left(\frac16\right)-\frac23+\frac13=0,
\]
\[
 [SU(2)]^2U(1):\quad
 3\!\left(\frac16\right)-\frac12=0,
\]
\[
 [U(1)]^3:\quad
 6\!\left(\frac16\right)^3
 +3\!\left(-\frac23\right)^3
 +3\!\left(\frac13\right)^3
 +2\!\left(-\frac12\right)^3+1=0,
\]
\[
 [\mathrm{grav}]^2U(1):\quad
 6\!\left(\frac16\right)
 +3\!\left(-\frac23\right)
 +3\!\left(\frac13\right)
 +2\!\left(-\frac12\right)+1=0.
\]
The familiar global \(SU(2)\) anomaly also vanishes: one generation contains three
colored quark doublets and one lepton doublet, hence four left-handed doublets, an
even number \cite{witten-su2}.
\end{proposition}

\begin{remark}[Gauge anomalies versus physical anomalous currents]
The exclusion applies to transformations asserted to be gauge redundancies.  It does
not forbid an anomalous global current whose failure of conservation is itself a
public physical effect.  The axial anomaly, for example, need not be erased by this
principle unless the corresponding transformation is promoted to a gauge symmetry.
\end{remark}

\begin{corollary}[Ward identities as comparison equations]
In an anomaly-free gauge realization, calculations made in different gauges are
comparison maps of one public process.  Gauge-parameter independence of physical
records and the associated Ward or Slavnov--Taylor identities are therefore exact
public-consistency equations, not optional calculational conventions.
\end{corollary}

\subsection{Conditional gravitational information preservation}

\begin{proposition}[No fundamental black-hole information destruction]
