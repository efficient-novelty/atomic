## The ladder (strict order)

1. **R-T1 — Quotient first.** Apply the adopted equality and orbit
   machinery (frozen univalent equality, naturality transport, the
   family-versus-instance law) to the Stage-4 minimizer cone. If the four
   presentations quotient to **one** semantic package, the tie dissolves:
   uniqueness is restored at act level, F-BF1 clears, and T-BF1/T-BF3
   resume with no new law. Presentation-multiplicity is not a tie.
2. **R-T2 — Confluence second.** If k > 1 genuinely distinct minimizers
   survive the quotient, attempt the confluence theorem: prove their
   continuations are isomorphic (each choice discharges the same demand,
   emits the same successor obligation, and generates equivalent sealed
   futures under the certified machinery). If confluence holds, the tie
   needs no break: the laws determine the world **up to tie-equivalence**,
   the enacted history is one gauge slice, and every law-level statement
   (ledger, halt, debt chain) is tie-invariant. Gauge freedom is recorded
   as a property of the theory, not repaired.
3. **R-T3 — Adjudication last.** Only if distinct minimizers survive
   *and* confluence fails is there a genuine free choice in the law. That
   adjudication (constitutive choice recording the enacted winner as an
   arena-founding act, versus explicit non-determinism with the enacted
   world as one branch) is the user's, on grounds that must not cite any
   downstream score or the desire for a fifteen-step derivation.
   **Enumeration order is not an admissible option at this rung**: the
   sealed tie-break is presentation artifact by construction and is
   recorded as testimony only.

## The companion build (one module, both symptoms)

The same naturality/orbit transport that R-T1 needs is exactly what the
E-5 A3 generator lacks. One construction serves both stops:

- extend the certified orbit quotient to the A3 generator's inventory,
  with the J3 subset as the regression gate (the shared 64 must reproduce
  the certified 8 families exactly — F-Q2 pattern);
- quotient the remaining 25 non-J3 seeds honestly (families or named
  pending, never singletons-by-default);
- add the missing term-level typed demand outputs;
- then re-run the E-5 successor (semantic O(16), full F1 disposition,
  T-BF2 authorization) and T-BF1/T-BF3 (Stage-4 cone quotient, ladder
  rung R-T1).

## Falsifiers

- **F-RT1.** Any winner selected before rung R-T1 completes → invalid;
  the stop stands.
- **F-RT2.** The generator's J3 regression failing to reproduce the
  certified 8 families → the transport implementation, not the certified
  quotient, is at fault; fix the machinery, never the certificate.
- **F-RT3.** Any appearance of enumeration order, hash order, or any
  presentation-dependent order in a law-level selection → invalid on its
  face, at every rung.
- **F-RT4.** Grounds at rung R-T3 citing downstream scores, the
  fifteen-step target, or the deadlock history → adoption invalid
  (the standing F-S8-2/F-BF4 rule).
