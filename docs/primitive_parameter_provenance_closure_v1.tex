\section{Primitive-Parameter Provenance and Physical Closure}
\label{sec:parameter-provenance}

The structural theory determines admissible APIs more strongly than it determines
continuous physical coefficients.  The following axiom closes a specific part of
that gap without restoring value-based selection at guarded stages.  It constrains
the passage from a selected API to a determinate physical realization and therefore
belongs to the actualization/integration face of the Selective Law.

\begin{definition}[Public realization moduli]
Let \(\mathcal S\) be a selected physical API over realized public history \(H\).
Write \(\operatorname{ExactReal}_H(\mathcal S)\) for its exact internal
realizations.  Two realizations are publicly equivalent,
\(R\simeq_{\mathrm{pub}}R'\), when every admissible internal probe and every
compatible public-event extractor gives the same retained public records, up to
Public Confluence and the declared observer-code tolerance.  The public realization
moduli type is
\[
 \mathfrak M_H(\mathcal S)
 :=
 \operatorname{ExactReal}_H(\mathcal S)/{\simeq_{\mathrm{pub}}}.
\]
A \emph{public parameter continuum} is a positive-dimensional connected family in
\(\mathfrak M_H(\mathcal S)\); equivalently for the applications below, it is a
family \(R_\lambda\), \(\lambda\in I\subset\mathbb R^k\) with \(I\) containing a
nonempty open set, such that distinct parameter values are distinguished by some
admissible public record.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Endogenous provenance certificate]
A parameter value has an endogenous provenance certificate when at least one of the
following holds.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{P\arabic*},leftmargin=2.4em]
\item \emph{Quotient:} the apparent variation is removed by
      \(\simeq_{\mathrm{pub}}\), and hence is not a physical parameter.
\item \emph{Inheritance:} restriction to an earlier locked public seal selects a
      finite or contractible subfamily.
\item \emph{Universal or spectral determination:} a universal property, canonical
      normalization, quantization rule, or isolated spectrum selects a finite or
      contractible subfamily.
\item \emph{Dynamical determination:} parameter-independent sealed dynamics,
      together with already certified boundary or initial records, has a finite or
      contractible solution set for the parameter.
\item \emph{State-record determination:} the parameter is represented by a
      dynamical carrier inside the API and its value is actualized by an internal
      stable record satisfying Internal Record Actualization.
\end{enumerate}
A numeral occurring in the very transition law that is supposed to generate its
record is not a certificate for itself.  State-record determination is therefore
available for contingent states and dynamically promoted moduli, not for an
unrepresented bare coefficient of the law.
\end{definition}

\begin{axiom}[Primitive-Parameter Provenance]
\label{ax:parameter-provenance}
No completed physical seal contains a publicly inequivalent primitive continuum
without an endogenous provenance certificate.  Equivalently, every
positive-dimensional component of \(\mathfrak M_H(\mathcal S)\) must be removed by
public equivalence, collapsed to a finite or contractible family by inheritance,
universal structure, spectrum, or sealed dynamics, or represented as an actualized
state variable by an internal record carrier.  If none of these occurs, the API may
be formally realizable but is not yet a determinate public physical law.
\end{axiom}

\begin{remark}[No third primitive role and no guarded-stage minimization]
The axiom does not add a third primitive explanatory role.  It constrains how a
selected admissible API becomes an actual physical realization, so it lies on the
Selective-Law side together with free sealing and Internal Record Actualization.
Nor does it compare numerical values at a guarded stage.  It rejects an unresolved
physical modulus after selection; it does not rank total dischargers by the value of
their parameters.
\end{remark}

\begin{proposition}[Parameter-provenance trichotomy]
\label{prop:parameter-trichotomy}
Every continuous physical quantity is one of three kinds:
\[
 \text{presentation gauge},
 \qquad
 \text{law-derived quantity},
 \qquad
 \text{recorded state or historical datum}.
\]
In particular, an exact law constant cannot be an arbitrary real numeral.  It must
have a finite constructive specification, an isolated universal or dynamical
characterization, or a spectral certificate.  A historical continuum is admissible
only to the precision carried by its internal record.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proofsketch}
If the variation is not publicly distinguishable it is removed in the quotient.  If
it changes the law and remains publicly distinguishable, state-record certification
would be circular unless the parameter has first been promoted to a carrier governed
by parameter-independent dynamics; hence it must instead be inherited or derived.
If it changes only a state inside an already fixed law, Internal Record Actualization
can certify it.  These cases exhaust Axiom~\ref{ax:parameter-provenance}.
\end{proofsketch}

\begin{theorem}[Locked-extension rigidity]
\label{thm:locked-extension-rigidity}
Let \(\mathcal S_0\subset\mathcal S_1\), let \(R_0\) be the locked public
realization of \(\mathcal S_0\), and let \(R_\lambda\) be a connected family of
exact realizations of \(\mathcal S_1\).  Assume:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)},leftmargin=2.2em]
\item the restrictions \(R_\lambda|_{\mathcal S_0}\) are publicly inequivalent as
      \(\lambda\) varies;
\item \(\mathcal S_1\) introduces no internal transition record declaring a change
      of the old public interface; and
\item the equation
      \(R_\lambda|_{\mathcal S_0}\simeq_{\mathrm{pub}}R_0\) has a finite or
      contractible solution set \(K\).
\end{enumerate}
Then every admissible extension lies in \(K\).  If \(K=\{\lambda_0\}\), the
extension parameter is forced to be \(\lambda_0\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proofsketch}
Internal Record Actualization locks \(R_0\) into later admissibility.  A parameter
outside \(K\) silently overwrites that record.  Since the new API contains no record
of such a transition, the variation has neither an inheritance certificate nor a
state-record certificate and is excluded by
Axiom~\ref{ax:parameter-provenance}.  Values in \(K\) preserve the prior seal.
\end{proofsketch}

\begin{corollary}[Sequential hypercharge rigidity]
\label{cor:sequential-hypercharge-rigidity}
Suppose the charged six-slot matter--Higgs representation is sealed before the
right-handed-neutrino extension.  The simultaneous seven-slot equations admit
\[
 Y_\epsilon=Y+\epsilon(B-L).
\]
Because \(B-L\) acts nontrivially on the already sealed charged slots, distinct
\(\epsilon\) change their public charges.  No transition record authorizes such a
retuning.  Locked-extension rigidity therefore forces \(\epsilon=0\).  The
neutrino Yukawa equation, mixed gravitational anomaly, and cubic anomaly then reduce
respectively to \(Y_{\nu^c}\), \(Y_{\nu^c}\), and \(Y_{\nu^c}^3\), so
\[
 \boxed{Y_{\nu^c}=0},
 \qquad
 \boxed{q_\nu=0}.
\]
\end{corollary}

\begin{remark}[What remains of the sealing-order premise]
The provenance axiom rejects the simultaneous \(Y+\epsilon(B-L)\) family as an
uncompleted physical seal and derives non-retroactivity once a prior charged layer
exists.  One finite question remains: prove that the charged six-slot layer followed
by \(\nu^c\) is the unique admissible staging, up to public equivalence, among all
well-typed decompositions of the matter API.  Thus the former continuous
sealing-order ambiguity is reduced to a finite dependency-order census.
\end{remark}

\begin{theorem}[No bare action moduli]
\label{thm:no-bare-action-moduli}
Let a primitive action presentation contain linearly independent public operators
\(\mathcal O_a\) with coefficients \(\lambda_a\):
\[
 S_\lambda=S_0+\sum_{a=1}^r\lambda_a\mathcal O_a .
\]
Assume varying \(\lambda\) changes some admissible public record after quotienting
field redefinitions, units, total derivatives, and gauge.  Then the coefficient
vector may enter a completed physical law only if it has an inheritance, universal,
spectral, or dynamical certificate.  It may not be licensed merely by measuring the
world generated by \(S_\lambda\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proofsketch}
The coefficient space gives a public realization continuum.  A later measurement is
circular as a law certificate because the map from states to records already depends
on \(\lambda\).  Axiom~\ref{ax:parameter-provenance} therefore requires an
independent certificate.  If \(\mathcal O_a\) is not forced by the selected minimal
complete API and has no certificate, relative free sealing omits the operator; in the
displayed basis its primitive coefficient is zero.
\end{proofsketch}

\begin{corollary}[Dynamical promotion of couplings and Yukawas]
The gauge couplings, Higgs-potential coefficients, and Yukawa matrices cannot remain
bare real or complex input data.  Each must be one of:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\alph*)},leftmargin=2.2em]
\item a canonical trace or spectral normalization;
\item an isolated point of a sealed renormalization-group or boundary-value problem;
\item a quantized value; or
\item the settled value of an explicit dynamical carrier whose state is internally
      recorded.
\end{enumerate}
In particular, the current framework's Yukawa matrices are not parameter-closed
until the proposed flavor-settling dynamics produces a finite or contractible
realization quotient.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proposition}[One-loop gauge fixed-point attempt]
\label{prop:one-loop-fixed-point-attempt}
For the declared one-Higgs Standard Model matter content,
\[
 \beta_i(g_i)=\frac{b_i}{16\pi^2}g_i^3+\cdots,
 \qquad
 (b_1,b_2,b_3)=\left(\frac{41}{10},-\frac{19}{6},-7\right).
\]
At one loop the only common fixed point is Gaussian, \(g_1=g_2=g_3=0\).  The
positive \(b_1\) means a nonzero hypercharge coupling runs away from the Gaussian
point toward the ultraviolet.  Hence the simplest certificate --- an isolated
nonzero ultraviolet fixed point of the minimal one-loop matter theory --- fails.
Parameter closure requires additional fixed dynamics, a finite-scale spectral
boundary condition with a derived placement, or a promoted coupling carrier.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proofsketch}
Since every displayed \(b_i\) is nonzero, \(\beta_i=0\) implies \(g_i=0\) at this
order.  The integrated equation
\[
 \frac1{g_i^2(\mu)}
 =
 \frac1{g_i^2(\mu_0)}
 -\frac{b_i}{8\pi^2}\log\frac{\mu}{\mu_0}
\]
shows that \(b_1>0\) moves a nonzero \(g_1\) away from zero as \(\mu\) increases.
The proposition is a failed closure attempt, not a no-go against interacting fixed
points once further certified dynamics is added.
\end{proofsketch}

\begin{definition}[Cauchy-public completeness]
A local physical sector is Cauchy-publicly complete when every continuous datum that
can change a future public record is either determined by the active two-layer public
interface or is carried by an explicitly selected additional field or record.  No
future-distinguishing hidden jet coordinate is left outside the public state API.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Depth-two exclusion of nondegenerate higher-jet dynamics]
\label{thm:depth-two-cauchy}
Assume Cauchy-public completeness and identify the two-layer chronological interface
in the continuum limit with configuration data and its first normal comparison.
For a local Lagrangian sector that is nondegenerate in its highest time derivative,
fundamental equations are at most second order unless extra selected carriers record
the additional Cauchy data.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proofsketch}
A nondegenerate Lagrangian depending on derivatives through order \(N\) has
Euler--Lagrange equations of order \(2N\) and requires \(2N\) independent initial
jet data.  For \(N>1\), the data beyond configuration and first normal comparison
form a continuous family that changes future records.  If the API has no extra
carrier for those jets, this is a publicly inequivalent primitive continuum and is
excluded by Axiom~\ref{ax:parameter-provenance}.  Degenerate higher-derivative
presentations survive only when constraints derive the apparent extra jets or when
they are equivalent to a lower-order system with explicit additional carriers; they
do not furnish hidden unrecorded higher-order data.
\end{proofsketch}

\begin{corollary}[Partial closure of the Einstein--Hilbert premise set]
\label{cor:eh-partial-closure}
In a four-dimensional, local, metric-only, diffeomorphism-covariant,
divergence-free gravitational sector whose public state is Cauchy-complete and has
no extra gravitational carrier, Theorem~\ref{thm:depth-two-cauchy} supplies the
previously assumed second-order premise.  Lovelock uniqueness then gives
\[
 G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}=8\pi G\,T_{\mu\nu},
 \qquad
 S=\frac1{16\pi G}\int(R-2\Lambda)\sqrt{-g}\,d^4x .
\]
The provenance axiom does not by itself derive the metric-only premise or the
numerical values of \(G\) and \(\Lambda\).
\end{corollary}

\begin{proposition}[Primitive cosmological term]
\label{prop:primitive-lambda}
At the primitive API level, if no vacuum-energy carrier, boundary condition, or
spectral certificate demands the zero-derivative volume term, minimal completeness
and relative free sealing omit it.  In that restricted sense,
\[
 \Lambda_{\mathrm{primitive}}=0.
\]
An effective cosmological term generated by a certified quantum state, boundary
record, anomaly, or coarse-grained gravitational dynamics is not excluded; its value
must be derived from that certificate.  This proposition therefore removes an
unprovenanced bare dial but does not calculate the observed late-time acceleration.
\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem}[Canonical state on a transitive public center]
\label{thm:canonical-center}
Let
\[
 \mathcal Z=\bigoplus_{a=1}^k\mathbb C P_a
\]
be the center of a settled public record after all carrier ranks and within-sector
coordinates have been retained in separate records.  Suppose a group \(G\) of public
presentation equivalences acts transitively on the primitive central projectors and
no sealed clause or record distinguishes their labels.  Then the unique canonical
actualized state on \(\mathcal Z\) is
\[
 \boxed{\omega(P_a)=\frac1k\quad(1\le a\le k)}.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proofsketch}
The normalized states on \(\mathcal Z\) form a simplex, hence a public continuum.
An arbitrary point has no provenance.  Equivalence-invariance requires
\(\omega\circ g=\omega\) for every \(g\in G\).  Transitivity makes all
\(\omega(P_a)\) equal, and normalization fixes them to \(1/k\).  Thus the
provenance axiom and Constitutive canonicity select the unique fixed point rather than
postulating an equipartition value.
\end{proofsketch}

\begin{corollary}[Koide central-weight closure, conditional only on rank separation]
\label{cor:koide-provenance}
Assume the flavor record factorizes so that the ranks and internal coordinates of
\(U\) and \(W\) are recorded separately, while its residual radial center is
\[
 \mathcal Z=\mathbb C P_U\oplus\mathbb C P_W
\]
with the public exchange \(P_U\leftrightarrow P_W\).  Then
Theorem~\ref{thm:canonical-center} gives
\[
 \omega(P_U)=\omega(P_W)=\frac12.
\]
Under the state--sector-norm identification
\[
 \omega(P)=\frac{\|Pv\|^2}{\|v\|^2},
\]
one obtains
\[
 \|P_Uv\|^2=\|P_Wv\|^2,
 \qquad
 \boxed{Q_N=\frac2N},
 \qquad
 \boxed{Q_3=\frac23}.
\]
Thus Rank-Blind Central Actualization need not remain a separate numerical axiom if
the rank-separation and public-exchange lemma is proved.  A charged-lepton mass
prediction still requires a positive spectral mass seal, jurisdiction over Yukawa
settling, and selection between the two finite positive roots; the provenance axiom
does not choose a finite root pair.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proposition}[Primitive topological-phase exclusion]
\label{prop:theta-exclusion}
Suppose a selected gauge sector has integer topological charge \(Q\) and a proposed
primitive sector weighting
\[
 \chi_\theta(Q)=e^{i\theta Q},
 \qquad \theta\in\mathbb R/2\pi\mathbb Z.
\]
If distinct \(\theta\) are detected by admitted public probes, they form a public
continuum.  If the selected API contains no topological-phase carrier, no prior
orientation record, and no universal or dynamical equation fixing \(\theta\), that
continuum is inadmissible.  The relative free seal of the API without a character
generator uses the trivial character,
\[
 \boxed{\theta_{\mathrm{primitive}}=0}.
\]
If no admitted probe detects the weighting, it is removed instead by public
equivalence.
\end{proposition}

\begin{remark}[Strong-CP boundary]
Proposition~\ref{prop:theta-exclusion} removes an unprovenanced bare Yang--Mills
angle.  It is not yet a complete proof that the physical QCD combination
\(\bar\theta\) vanishes, because chiral phases of the quark mass seal can contribute
to that invariant combination.  Full closure requires a gauge-invariant phase or
determinant theorem for the settled quark mass operators and control of derived
radiative contributions.  A positive spectral mass seal is a natural candidate, but
that result is not presently established.
\end{remark}

\begin{proposition}[What the axiom does not select]
\label{prop:finite-choice-boundary}
Primitive-Parameter Provenance excludes unresolved positive-dimensional public
moduli.  It does not by itself choose among a finite set.  Consequently it does not,
without additional structure, determine:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)},leftmargin=2.2em]
\item the global gauge quotient
      \(G_0/\Gamma\) for
      \(\Gamma\in\{1,\mathbb Z_2,\mathbb Z_3,\mathbb Z_6\}\);
\item one versus two or more rigid Higgs multiplets;
\item three versus four or more rigid generations; or
\item the high versus low positive Koide root.
\end{enumerate}
Each is a discrete ambiguity and admits models with no unresolved continuous
parameter.  Complete-probe effectivity, No Unpaid Multiplicity, a derived orientation
obligation, and a spectral-ordering theorem remain separate candidate closures.
\end{proposition}

\begin{corollary}[Restriction on extended Higgs and flavor sectors]
Although the axiom does not prove \(N_H=1\) or \(N_{\mathrm{gen}}=3\), it excludes a
generic extra Higgs or flavor sector whose new mixing angles, phases, masses, and
couplings are inserted as bare parameters.  Any such extension must provide a
certificate fixing or dynamically recording every new public modulus.  Hence the
remaining multiplicity question is sharply finite: whether a rigid, fully certified
extra carrier is demanded by an independent public obligation.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proposition}[Extractor and clock consequences]
For a fixed observer, publicly inequivalent POVMs, tolerances, sufficient statistics,
or clock calibrations cannot be primitive dials.  They must be quotiented as
operationally equivalent, uniquely derived from the sealed observer dynamics, or
carried by an explicit calibration record.  Thus
Axiom~\ref{ax:parameter-provenance} does not universalize \(\mathcal Q_p\) or choose
seconds from the Genesis ledger; it upgrades both gaps into mandatory provenance
obligations.  The seven-cell thermal-repetition observer satisfies the requirement
model-wise because its dynamics derives the Hamming-weight statistic and optimal
error floor.
\end{proposition}
