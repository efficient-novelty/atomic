# GCap Quotient Decision Contract (GQ0)

Date: 2026-08-04
Status: DRAFT-NOT-FROZEN (formulation lane, plan binding decision 7). This
document mints no authority, decides no quotient, and may change freely until
its registered freeze gate: `~gcap` freezes only after adversarial review,
before JG3 executes and before any live registered-prefix raw carrier is
inspected (plan §6.4, §7.4 item 1).

Frozen inputs (immutable):

- the JG1 seven-role grammar, eight admission obligations, and receipt-source
  grammar (`docs/260801_jg1_pre_exposure_generative_capability_grammar.md`);
- the JG2a constructor grammar: one closed constructor schema per role, with
  pairwise-distinct finite interface shapes and role derived from shape
  (`docs/260801_jg2a_constructor_and_stage_surface.md`); and
- the TC-R/TC-A statements as frozen at their own gate; GQ0 consumes their
  transport and support-delta vocabulary, never their proofs-in-progress.

Justification discipline: every adjudication below must be argued from the
JG1 role grammar, the JG2a interface shapes, and support accounting. No
choice may be justified by which quotient makes `gamma` positive, matches
any historical stage count, or simplifies a desired downstream comparison.

## 1. Decision register

Each item is marked PROPOSED (with the argued default) or OPEN (a genuine
underdetermination registered for adversarial review). PROPOSED items are
not decided until the freeze.

### Q1. Equivalence generators for `~gcap` — PROPOSED

Two admitted raw capabilities are identified exactly when all of the
following hold; the relation is the least equivalence generated by these
clauses and no others:

1. **Same constructor shape.** Both realize the same JG2a constructor
   schema; role is derived from the verified shape, never from a tag.
2. **Same public typed interface up to transport.** Their finite public
   typed interfaces (JG1 admission obligation 3) are equal after rendering
   in one common transported naming frame (Q2), including input-sort
   sequence, output sort, and declared context.
3. **Same formal-action support class.** Their typed support (newly paid
   clause or prior live generative output, JG1 obligation 7) resolves to
   the same support class under the frozen shared comparator; capabilities
   whose support differs by a TC-witnessed formal-action key are distinct.
4. **Presentation closure.** They differ only by the invariances of Q3
   (aliasing, reordering, duplication, adopted presentation equivalence).

Nothing else generates identification. In particular, extensional
input/output agreement on any finite test set, byte-digest collision,
matching names, and equal `kappa` are all non-generators.

### Q2. Renaming/transport compatibility with TC-R — PROPOSED

The quotient commutes with transport. For every admissible renaming `h` in
the TC-R carrier:

```text
Quotient_gcap(Transport_h(RawGCap(H,A)))
  = Transport_h(Quotient_gcap(RawGCap(H,A)))
```

and the induced map on classes is a bijection. Literal identifier equality
is never a class criterion (lesson 16): comparison inside Q1 clause 2
happens only after both sides are rendered in one transported frame.
Formal-action keys transport structurally per TC-R's locus-commutation
lemma; the quotient may not erase them to force commutation. If the frozen
TC-R carrier changes, Q2 reopens; it cannot be patched in place.

### Q3. Presentation and duplication invariance — PROPOSED

The quotient is invariant under:

- opaque identifier permutation (via Q2);
- declaration and enumeration reordering;
- transparent aliases and definitional extensions;
- duplicated syntactic presentations of one capability; and
- adopted presentation equivalence of the sealed public grammar.

Duplicating one presentation may not enlarge any class count, the class
inventory, or (downstream) `gamma`. This mirrors the task-quotient
invariance list of plan §3.3 and the neutrality rules of §5.6.

### Q4. Empty-carrier behavior — PROPOSED

If JG2c issues a completely covered empty carrier (a valid outcome under
plan §7.3), the quotient of that carrier is the empty quotient, with zero
classes, complete coverage, and a valid verdict. An empty quotient is not
an error, not `Unknown`, and not evidence of any defect. Incomplete
coverage of a nonempty or empty carrier is a fail-closed abort before
quotienting begins; the quotient never repairs coverage.

### Q5. Coarseness rationale — PROPOSED

Why exactly the Q1 generators, argued without reference to desired `gamma`:

- The JG1 role grammar is a closed seven-member enum with no `Other`; the
  reusable-capability ontology it fixes distinguishes capabilities by what
  public operation they are (constructor shape), over what typed surface
  they operate (finite public interface), and what paid or live structure
  supports them (receipt-source grammar). Q1's three substantive clauses
  are exactly these three frozen axes; adding a finer axis would
  distinguish capabilities by structure JG1/JG2a never registered, and
  dropping one would identify capabilities the frozen grammars can tell
  apart.
- The JG2a shapes are pairwise distinct and role-deriving, so clause 1 is
  decidable from verified structure alone and admits no caller tag.
- Clause 3 preserves the TC lesson that formal-action keys witness
  genuinely new support (plan §6.3): a quotient coarse enough to merge them
  would erase exactly the action-generated structure `GCap` audits.
- Clause 4 removes only presentation freedom, which mints no capability by
  the neutrality discipline of §5.6.

### Q6. Class-representative discipline — PROPOSED

Classes are compared by canonical class data derived inside the quotient
compiler; no caller-selected representative, enumeration order, or digest
selects or splits a class. Representative independence must be proved in
the S1b3 style (independently recomputed, order-insensitive).

### Q7. Interface-equality granularity — OPEN

Whether Q1 clause 2 compares interfaces up to exact transported equality
only, or additionally up to the adopted definitional-extension equivalence
of the sealed grammar (two interfaces equal after unfolding transparent
definitions). Exact-only is finer and cheaper to decide; unfolding-closed
matches Q3's alias invariance more uniformly but needs a normalization
theorem for interfaces. Adjudication procedure: draft both relations over
the frozen JG2a sort vocabulary, exhibit a discriminating fixture pair,
and adopt at the freeze the relation under which Q3 invariance is provable;
if both are provable, adopt the coarser (unfolding-closed) with the
argument recorded, since the difference is pure presentation. The choice
may not be made by inspecting any live carrier.

### Q8. Support-class comparison depth — OPEN

Whether Q1 clause 3 compares support at the granularity of the frozen
shared comparator classes only, or also requires equal support
multiplicity (dependency/coverage repetition counts). The S1c2 edge-27
result shows companions add multiplicity but no new class; identifying
multiplicity-differing supports is therefore consistent with the frozen
class structure, but multiplicity may later carry cost information for KP.
Adjudication procedure: decide at the freeze from whether any frozen
theorem makes multiplicity class-relevant; default to class-only if none
does, and record the alternative as a rejected generator with rationale.
KP0 cost concerns may motivate a separate register but may not thicken
`~gcap`.

## 2. Anti-bias clause (freeze-before-observation)

The quotient may not be chosen, tuned, or revised:

- to make `gamma` positive, nonzero, or large at any history;
- to match, approach, or avoid any historical stage count, the length
  fifteen, or any decoded interpretation; or
- by inspecting any live registered-prefix raw carrier, any actual JG2c
  output over the registered prefix, or any per-history class count.

`~gcap` freezes before JG2c carrier inspection. Fixture carriers used to
test the quotient must be synthetic, target-neutral, and constructed from
the frozen grammars only. If a live carrier is observed before the freeze,
the draft is burned: the observation is recorded and any subsequent
quotient definition is a new experiment under the K3 fresh-evidence rule.
A post-freeze change to `~gcap` is a new version under plan §5.5 and
invalidates any `gamma` derived from the old version.

## 3. Generic mutant suite

The frozen quotient's audit must reject each mutant below at the quotient
layer, on fixtures earlier layers accept (lesson 13):

- **M1 (too coarse, mandatory).** A quotient that merges two capabilities
  with TC-witnessed distinct formal-action support (distinct formal keys
  from a nonempty action). The audit must detect the merged class as a
  violation of Q1 clause 3.
- **M2 (too fine, mandatory).** A quotient that separates two presentation
  variants of one capability (alias, reordering, or duplicated
  presentation per Q3). The audit must detect the split as a violation of
  Q3 invariance.
- **M3 (transport-broken).** A quotient that compares untransported
  identifiers, so that some renaming `h` changes the class count. Rejected
  by the Q2 commutation check.
- **M4 (caller-seeded).** A quotient that accepts a caller-supplied class
  list, representative choice, or expected class count. Rejected by Q6.
- **M5 (coverage-forgiving).** A quotient that silently drops uncovered
  carrier members instead of aborting. Rejected by Q4's fail-closed rule.

Resource exhaustion during quotienting or auditing returns `Unknown` for
the affected verdict; it never yields a partial quotient, a smaller class
inventory, or a mutant pass.

## 4. Interaction boundaries

The quotient decision consumes only:

- JG2c's complete raw carrier (`RawGCap(H,A)`), through its opaque
  authority; and
- the frozen JG1 and JG2a grammars, plus the frozen TC-R transport
  vocabulary named in Q2.

The following may not enter the quotient decision, its class criteria, or
its audit facts: any JG7 receipt or receipt-source value; any semantic SR2
result or `nu`; any task-extension value, `TaskExt` set, or dominance
verdict; any selective-profile outcome, gate result, or survivor set; any
`kappa` value; any R3 account, footprint back-edge, or copied raw/source/
owner ordinal; and any held-out or historical evidence. Provenance (JG7)
and extension (KW) are downstream consumers of the quotient, never inputs
to it.

## 5. Freeze gate

`~gcap` freezes exactly when all of the following hold, and not before:

1. every PROPOSED item above is confirmed or amended, and every OPEN item
   is adjudicated by its registered procedure, with rationale recorded;
2. adversarial review has run against this register, including an explicit
   search for a hidden gamma-positive or count-matching bias;
3. the mutant suite of §3 is implemented and discriminating on synthetic
   fixtures; and
4. no live registered-prefix raw carrier has been inspected (§2).

The freeze precedes JG3 execution (plan §7.4 item 1) and any live carrier
inspection. Until the freeze, JG3 is blocked and `gamma` remains `Unknown`.
