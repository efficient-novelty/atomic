# XF-2 — The Two-Layer Campaign Structure

**Date:** 2026-07-25. **Status:** frozen; versioned successor to the
XF-1 brief and Amendment 1, restructuring the campaign under
`docs/MISSION.md`. Nothing sealed is edited; the XF ledger continues,
append-only, with items re-tagged R or F below. The two layers share
one honesty floor and differ in exactly one discipline: what blindness
is for.

## The R-layer: reconstruction closure (the active writing-phase work)

**Goal:** lossless retrodictive closure — the two laws plus a priced
closure layer carrying the relations and parameters of known physics
*exactly*, per MISSION.md's standard: lossless abstraction, scored by
compression.

**Governing metric: the compression ledger under the losslessness
constraint.** Every R-item is a row:

| axiom / bridge | costs (postulated content) | buys (facts recovered exactly) | loss notes | ratio notes |

Two disciplines govern the rows. \emph{Losslessness}: a "buy" counts
only if the covered relation or parameter is recovered exactly; any
approximation, domain restriction, or fact quietly broken by a later
fit is a **loss note**, disclosed in its own column — an R-layer
regression discipline (a new axiom may never damage an earlier row's
recovery; that is a published regression, not a trade). \emph{Priced
tuning}: tuning is not forbidden, *undisclosed* tuning is; every fit
records what was adjusted, to what, and why. The
realization-quotient criterion (F-XF6) survives as a label, not a
gate: rows that meet it are marked parameter-predictive; rows that
don't are honest fits. More bits are acceptable where physics demands
them — the goal is the least description that loses nothing, not the
least description.

**R-layer falsifiers (inherited, re-scoped):** register eligibility
(F-XF1 — ledger quantities still need bridges, because a reconstruction
that decorates itself with its own bookkeeping constants is compressing
nothing); no silent promotion of a fit to a prediction (F-XF6's
original teeth); the compression ledger may never omit a row's cost
(the R-layer's version of F-XF4).

## The F-layer: forward predictions (thin, blind, registered)

**Goal:** the book's bets against the future — few, sharp,
parameter-free where possible, registered before comparison, burned
honestly if hit.

**Governing discipline: the full XF-1 constitution, verbatim.**
Prediction-blindness (F-XF2: derivation sealed before data; no
target-aware premises), burn semantics (F-XF3), all-predictions-report
(F-XF4), the hazard fence (no φ/Fibonacci physics without a derived
bridge; α out of reach), and the two-part status ladder
(registered → derived → corroborated/burned).

**Current F-items:**

- **F-A Grade-2 exactness** (full Möbius hierarchy, I_T = 0 for
  |T| ≥ 3, on its stated domain) and **F-A2 phase-support exactness**
  — the flagship vetoes; mechanization artifacts pending.
- **F-B1 q_ν = 0** (derived, certificate chain per Amendment 1).
- **F-B2 one-Higgs consequence bundle** (activates when R3 adopts —
  an R-layer axiom arming an F-layer veto is the intended coupling
  between layers).
- **F-B4 colorless charge integrality** (activates when R4 closes the
  global form).
- **F-θ θ̄_fund = 0** with EDM exposure (derivation obligation: the
  orientation-record count at register grade).
- **F-CB critical-boundary Higgs/top** — the one continuous-number
  bet: λ(M_Pl) = 0 exactly, with the present ~2–3 GeV tension against
  the measured Higgs mass stated *in the registration*, resolvable by
  future top-mass precision in either direction. Registered with its
  tension or not at all.
- **F-L Lovelock consequences** (no scalar/vector GW polarization, no
  vacuum dispersion — activates when R6 adopts).

**Coupling rule between layers.** R-adoptions may arm F-items (R3→F-B2,
R4→F-B4, R6→F-L); F-failures reflect back as R-costs (a burned F-item
raises the price of every R-axiom that implied it — the compression
ledger records the markup). The layers share artifacts but never share
discipline: an R-fit may not be cited as an F-corroboration, and an
F-registration may not consult an R-fit's tuned values.
