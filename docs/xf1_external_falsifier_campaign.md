**The campaign's constitution (three rules, before any physics).**

1. **Register eligibility (F-XF1).** A prediction may be built only
   from lawful-register content: the semantic ledger, κ and the demand
   chain, the width-2 window and d_obl = 2, the provenance ceiling,

   A prediction found resting on them is void, not weakened.
2. **Prediction-blindness (F-XF2).** Every prediction is registered
   with its derivation *before* comparison against data, and no step
   of any derivation may select a form, constant, or bridge premise
   with knowledge of the measured target. Retro-fitting is the
   103-temptation with laboratory data; the recorded order
   (derivation sealed, then comparison) is part of each artifact.
3. **Burn semantics (F-XF3).** A prediction killed by measurement
   stays killed: the observation is published verbatim, the veto is
   recorded against the theory, and any survival argument goes
   through a versioned adjudication whose grounds may not cite the
   desire to survive. All registered predictions report --- hits,
   misses, and pendings; suppressing a miss voids the campaign
   (F-XF4).
