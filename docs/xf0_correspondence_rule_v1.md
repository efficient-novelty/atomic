## 3. Unit of counting

`D(n)` counts **independent structural primitives**: structures (operations,
formers, fields, modalities, constructors) in the standard mathematical
presentation of the stratum's reading that are genuinely new relative to the
cumulative ladder below it. The granularity is fixed by the three calibration
anchors frozen in the campaign brief (Section 5): the count is of *minted
ideas*, never of field-theoretic component counts (helicities, local field
components, dimensions of solution spaces).

## 4. Counting discipline

Enumerate the defining data of the stratum's standard presentation (per cited
standard references — author, title, chapter/section required per row).
Assign each datum exactly one class:

- **(D) Derived carrier** — the underlying object is constructible from
  strata 1..n−1 content by constructions already available at that ladder
  point. Contributes 0.
- **(P) Property** — a mere property, propositional law, or coherence
  condition (associativity as a law, uniqueness clauses, naturality squares,
  completeness as a property). Contributes 0.
- **(C) Canonically determined** — existence *and* identity are implied by
  prior strata or by other data already counted, up to canonical isomorphism
  (e.g., a quantity computed by a formula from counted data). Contributes 0.
- **(T) Topological/discrete rigidity** — classification data with no local
  content: discrete invariants, clutching classes, choices that are pure
  gauge. Contributes 0.
- **(N) New independent structure** — everything else: a structural datum
  whose existence is not implied by prior or counted content. Contributes 1.
  Boundary clause: a datum unique up to canonical isomorphism *whenever it
  exists*, but whose existence is not implied, is class (N) — the existence
  is the minted content. A datum whose existence and identity are both
  implied is class (C).

`D(n)` = number of class-(N) data. The classes are exhaustive and each datum
gets exactly one; when in doubt between (N) and any zero class, the analyst
must state the doubt and resolve by the boundary clause, not by convenience.
