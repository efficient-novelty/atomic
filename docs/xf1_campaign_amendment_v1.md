## A. Constitutional addition: the realization-quotient criterion

A parameter is *predicted* only when all four hold: (1) the space of
physically inequivalent realizations of the sealed structure is
contractible or has a finite derived set of components; (2)
actualization selects one component without target data; (3) the
public observable extractor is fixed; (4) nuisance evolution from the
selected value to the measurement scale is fixed. This formalizes what
the hazard fence implied: discrete/topological outputs (kernels,
multiplicities, congruences, exact ranks) can qualify; couplings,
Yukawas, the Higgs potential parameters, cosmological coefficients,
and the Koide radial coordinate currently do not, and no structural
theorem may be silently promoted to a numerical prediction while its
realization fiber contains a continuum. Added as F-XF6.

## B. XF-A upgraded: the full grade-2 hierarchy, on its honest domain

1. **Strengthened claim.** The registered prediction is promoted from
   \(\kappa_3 = 0\) to the full grade-2 quantum-measure statement:
   for alternatives with amplitude vectors \(a_i\) and Born weight
   \(\mu(S) = |\sum_{i \in S} a_i|^2\), every Möbius/Sorkin
   coefficient \(I_T = \sum_{S \subseteq T} (-1)^{|T|-|S|} \mu(S)\)
   vanishes for \(|T| \ge 3\) — because each pair contribution
   carries the factor \((1-1)^{|T|-2} = 0\). Equivalently, the
   N-path intensity is exactly determined by empty-, one-, and
   two-path records. The derivation rests on conjugate self-pairing
   alone; the derivation-obligation artifact is updated to carry this
   proof and its linkage to the width-two structure, with the
   standing register caution unchanged.
2. **XF-A2 — Phase-support exactness (new registered prediction).**
   Fixed N-alternative instrument, all paths open, independent phase
   modulation \(\theta_i\): the phase-response Fourier support is
   contained in \(\{0\} \cup \{e_i - e_j : i \ne j\}\), and the
   pairwise-coefficient matrix is Hermitian positive semidefinite
   (Gram). Veto: any reproducible irreducible Fourier mode involving
   three or more independently modulated phases. Stronger than any
   normalized \(\kappa_3\), uses the whole data cube, avoids
   aperture-boundary ambiguity.
3. **Domain restriction (both A-items).** The veto condition is
   restated at theorem grade: a nonzero intrinsic grade-3 coefficient
   *in one fixed additive event algebra*, after closing the
   documented loopholes (detector nonlinearity, mask contextuality,
   multiparticle response, leakage, background). This is not a rescue
   clause; it is the mathematical domain of the derivation, stated
   before any experiment is scored. Apparent higher-order signals
   from nonlinear media or changed boundary-value problems are
   outside the theorem and are not vetoes.
4. **Caution recorded (discriminator gap).** Grade-2 exactness does
   not single out complex quantum theory: Euclidean-Jordan and
   broader grade-2 GPTs survive it. XF-A is a sharp veto whose *hit*
   is weak confirmation. A future discriminator item (central-phase /
   local-tomography) is named as XF-A3, unregistered until its
   derivation route exists.
5. **Born-exponent route replaced.** The tensor-word/primeness
   uniqueness argument is retired as vulnerable (it excludes
   \(|z|^p\) only within its chosen language, and historical
   depth-two does not constrain unary algebraic degree). The
   registered derivation program is: Jordan/Hilbert reconstruction +
   frame invariance + orthogonal additivity over public channels +
   continuity + normalization \(\Rightarrow\) \(f(s+t) = f(s)+f(t)\)
   via Pythagorean composition \(\Rightarrow\) \(W(\psi) = |\psi|^2\)
   \(\Rightarrow\) grade-2 hierarchy. The exponent two is selected as
   the unique continuous frame-invariant additive-under-orthogonality
   weight — never as an echo of engine counting rules.
