## The proposed law: `demand-parsimony-law-v1`

1. **Generative law (unchanged, A4):** nothing without demand — every
   step totally discharges the live obligation profile of its window.
2. **Selective law (threshold-free):** nothing beyond demand — among
   admissible dischargers, the minimal one, under the parsimony order:
   least κ, then least certified ν. Comparative only; no bar, no
   threshold, no distance-to-anything. This clause simultaneously
   discharges the reserved multiple-discharger sub-rule of the Option-B
   adoption — and it is adopted at the moment of maximal
   verdict-blindness, since the verified record contains no ties for it
   to break (all audited discharger counts are 1).
3. **Bootstrap clause:** stages whose window exports no demand (the
   pre-structural prefix; the stage-3 wrinkle stands as recorded) are
   *constitutive* arena-founding acts, registered as such — the
   generalization of R1's adopted treatment of Stage 1. The demand chain
   begins with the first exported obligation.
4. **Halt:** debt-freedom. The sequence ends where O = ∅ — Theorem 12,
   already engine-verified. On the open band the generative law alone
   forecloses acceptance: no demand, no discharge, no admissibility.
   A5 and the certification machinery remain as the *evidentiary*
   regime (what could ever be certified), now corroborating rather than
   carrying the halt.
5. **The bar's status:** Δₙ = Fₙ, Φₙ, Ωₙ, and Barₙ remain *descriptive*
   diagnostics of interface dynamics per Corollary 14's split — real,
   recorded in certificates, gating nothing. The Fibonacci ledger is
   physics of the interface, not legislation of the sequence.

**What this formulation makes lawlike, stated without euphemism:** the
halt ceases to be a valuation contest and becomes the law's own
statement — "runtime begins where debt ends." The theory's empirical
content relocates entirely to the demand structure: the claim *the chain
has exactly fifteen links* is verified by the O-ladder and refutable by
exactly one thing — Guard-Rail F1, a demanded-but-underdetermined
instance. E-5 is therefore the experimental frontier of the bar-free
theory, not an auxiliary check.
