\chapter[Formal Two-Law Schema]{\titlesplit{The Two Laws}{Formal Axioms and Schema}}\label{app:two-laws}

This appendix fixes the technical contract used by the later appendices, in
the form the law holds after the completed adjudication ledger and the
cone-level certification campaign. Two laws govern realized history: the
\emph{Constitutive Law} (nothing without demand) determines admissibility, and
the \emph{Selective Law} operates in two registers --- obligation gates the
guarded stages, certified value gates only the debt-free open band. Numerical
thresholds gate nothing anywhere; where the laws are silent among genuinely
distinct admissible continuations, the silence is real and history is a cone.
The previous formulation of this appendix, which encoded the enacted engine's
bar-clearing selection as law, is retained unedited as
\texttt{app\_a\_two\_laws\_formal\_axioms\_old.tex}; under the audit results
summarized below it has the status of testimony.

All definitions are relative to a fixed ambient CCHM-style cubical extension
calculus \(\mathfrak{C}\), equipped with cumulative universes, \(\Pi\)- and
\(\Sigma\)-types, finite sums, path types, univalence, Kan composition,
normalized public signatures, and sealed opaque extensions.

\paragraph{Register discipline (mandatory for this book).}
Every numerical value of an extension carries a register tag.
\emph{Semantic-family} quantities (Definition~\ref{def:semantic-register})
are act-local, transportable, and proof-bearing; they are the only values
admissible in law-level statements. \emph{Structural} quantities --- the
audited totals of the enacted engine, the cumulative bar trajectories, and
every quantity derived from them --- are sealed testimony and lawful
diagnostics, with no law-level authority. Presenting a structural total as a
semantic count invalidates the presenting artifact regardless of its other
content.

\section{State, Signatures, and Candidates}

\begin{definition}[History and public boundary]
A \emph{history} \(H_n\) is a finite sequence of sealed extensions
\[
H_n = (x_1,\dots,x_n)
\]
interpreted in \(\mathfrak{C}\), modulo definitional equality and univalent
equivalence of normalized public signatures. Its exported interface is the
normalized telescope
\(\partial H_n := \mathrm{NF}(\partial x_1,\dots,\partial x_n)\).
Write \(\mathcal{L}(H_n)\) for the finite audited basis of derivation schemas
generated by \(\partial H_n\) under the fixed closure operator
\(\mathrm{Cl}_{\mathfrak{C}}\). Equivalent schemas are counted once.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Candidate continuation]
A candidate continuation over \(H\) is a finite normalized extension telescope
\[
x = (\partial x,\; w_x,\; O_\bullet(x\mid H),\; b_x),
\]
where \(\partial x\) is the proposed public boundary, \(w_x\) its constructive
realization datum, \(O_\bullet(x\mid H)\) the normalized obligation complex
induced by sealing \(x\) against \(\partial H\), and
\(b_x : \partial H \to \partial(H,x)\) the boundary inclusion preserving the
sealed public interface.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Presentation equivalence]
Candidates \(x,y\) over \(H\) are presentation-equivalent, \(x \simeq_H y\),
when their sealed public signatures are connected by a univalent equivalence
over \(\partial H\) preserving normalized kernel clauses, computation clauses,
and the audited derivation-schema basis. All quantities below are invariant
under \(\simeq_H\). Presentation-multiplicity is not plurality: candidates are
counted at the level of acts, after this quotient and the natural-family
transport of Definition~\ref{def:schema-family-instance}.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Grammar, cone, sealing, integration]
A grammar \(G\) is a finite normalized constructor signature;
\(\mathrm{Cand}_G(H)\) is the raw expressible cone over \(H\). The free
sealing completion \(F_x(H)\) of a selected candidate is the least lawful
realization of its API --- the quotient of
\(\mathrm{Term}_{\mathfrak C}(H;\mathcal S_x)\) by the least congruence
containing the old judgmental equalities and the equations forced by
\(\mathcal S_x\), with no additional generator or equation. Integration seals
it: \(H^+ = I(H,x) := \mathrm{Seal}_{\mathfrak C}(F_x(H))\). Relative
free-sealing initiality (\(F_x(H)\) initial among lawful exact realizations of
the selected API over \(H\)) holds as in the previous formulation; the
universal property governs realization \emph{after} selection and is not a
selection principle.
\end{definition}

\section{The Constitutive Law}

The Constitutive Law has two faces: an admissibility predicate, and the
windowed demand structure whose total discharge is the sole acceptance
condition at guarded stages. The two faces are one law stated twice: because
coherence is relational, a law of admissible form is automatically a law of
what admitted form lacks --- every admission creates definite, checkable
incompletenesses, and the demand structure below is nothing but the
admissibility requirements read as a ledger of obligations.

\begin{definition}[Constitutive admissibility]
\(\mathrm{CL}_H(x)\) holds iff:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, label=\textbf{CL\arabic*.}]
\item \textbf{Witness-bearing realizability.}
\(\partial H,\partial x \vdash_{\mathfrak{C}} \mathrm{ok}\) and
\(\partial H \vdash_{\mathfrak{C}} w_x : \mathrm{Realize}(x\mid H)\).
\item \textbf{Equivalence invariance.} \(x\simeq_H y\) implies
\(\mathrm{CL}_H(x)\Leftrightarrow \mathrm{CL}_H(y)\) and
\(I(H,x)\simeq I(H,y)\).
\item \textbf{Bounded local satisfiability.} \(O_\bullet(x\mid H)\) has finite
normalized support and every primitive obligation is discharged inside the
constitutive depth bound \(d_{\mathrm{obl}}=2\)
(\hyperref[app:d2-proof]{Appendix \ref*{app:d2-proof}}).
\item \textbf{Canonicity under sealing.} Every closed exported construction in
\(\partial I(H,x)\) normalizes to a canonical public value, path, computation
rule, or opaque constant.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Demand window and obligation profile]
\label{def:demand-window}
The \emph{demand window} at stage \(n\) is the width-two pair
\((S_n,S_{n-1})\) of the two most recently sealed stages. The window width
\(2\) is the theory's single visible memory parameter; it reappears in the
quantitative debt recursion and in the forgetting horizon of
Section~\ref{sec:cone}. The \emph{obligation profile} live at stage \(n+1\) is
\[
O(n+1) \;=\; C(S_n,S_{n-1})\setminus D(B_n),
\]
the demands generated by the active window and not derivable in the current
library \(B_n\). An orbit-ledger certificate for \(O(n+1)\) must prove
\emph{extraction completeness} (every normalized demand of the window is
enumerated), \emph{derivability completeness} (every extracted orbit is
decided by a typed derivation or a live witness), and \emph{window locality
and expiration} (every demand live at \(n+1\) is generated by
\((S_n,S_{n-1})\), and every demand of an older window was discharged and
remains discharged under cumulative weakening).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Future holes: demands as typed hypotheses]
\label{def:future-holes}
A future-directed demand scheme is an \emph{open judgment}
\(\Gamma \vdash \mathrm{scheme}\), in which each hole is an ambient hypothesis
carrying a \emph{declared, \(B_n\)-formable motive}: the world knows the type
of what it owes before the thing exists. Motives are declared at scheme
registration --- enumerated, never inferred, never selected by outcome.
Actions of sealed operators on holes are the adopted transparent-former
closure and contextual-internality rules acting on hypotheses. A scheme is
\emph{discharged} by a sealed filler exactly when the hypothetical derivation
specializes at that filler and replays under the closed rules (instantiation
coherence). Holes and their fillings mint nothing: zero \(\kappa\), zero
\(\nu\), no anchors.

Ambient contexts are \emph{dependent}: \(\Gamma =
(p_1{:}M_1,\dots,p_k{:}M_k(p_1,\dots,p_{k-1}))\), with sequential typed
substitution checked left to right. A registration is \emph{authoritative}
only together with a total-specialization theorem --- every kernel-admissible
assignment of the declared context yields a kernel-accepted specialized body.
Totality is restored only by honest dependent re-declaration or published
narrowing of the declared motive, never by outcome-dependent filtering of
assignments. Arity is declared, not capped. A hypothesis may declare as
motive the sealed kernel type of a named prior clause, referenced by digest
and used whole (opaque dereference, zero charge). Interface slots of
chronological schemes map to context parameters by order-preserving identity,
or are realized by canonical expressions over a declared \emph{support
context} computed from the sealed instance's dependency structure ---
honest comprehension, never flattening --- with the acyclicity of the motive
graph proved.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Directive and quantitative debt]
The \emph{directive debt} at stage \(n+1\) is the obligation profile
\(O(n+1)\) itself: the typed anticipations the world has issued and not yet
discharged. The \emph{quantitative debt} \(\Delta_n\) is the
presentation-invariant integration-debt scalar of the enacted ledger; its
two-term recursion is the numerical shadow of the width-two window. A field is
\emph{debt-free} when \(O = \varnothing\).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Constitutive bootstrap]
A stage whose window exports no demand is a \emph{constitutive, arena-founding
act}: it is registered, not selected, and no selective clause applies to it.
The demand chain begins with the first exported obligation. (In the certified
Genesis trace the bootstrap comprises stages 1--3, with the recorded stage-3
wrinkle: a demand exported before any lane has jurisdiction.)
\end{definition}

\section{The Semantic Register}\label{sec:semantic-register}

\begin{definition}[Typed operational schema audit]
For a history \(H\), \(\mathsf{RawSch}_2(H)\) is the set of well-typed raw
derivation-schema judgements of the fixed depth-two closure fragment. Write
\(\approx_H\) for the least equivalence generated by judgmental equality and
transport along univalent equivalences of normalized public signatures,
preserving conclusions and all substitution and naturality squares. An
operational audit supplies a terminating typed normalizer
\(\mathrm{nf}_H\) with certificates of type preservation, soundness, and
completeness (\(\mathrm{nf}_H(D)=\mathrm{nf}_H(E) \Leftrightarrow D\approx_H
E\)); absent these certificates the audit fails closed.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Schema family and instance]
\label{def:schema-family-instance}
A semantic schema family over \(H\) is a typed normal schema varying naturally
in its declared parameters; \(\mathsf{Sch}_2(H)\) is the finite set of family
classes. For a family \(\sigma\) and well-typed substitution \(\theta\), the
term \(\sigma[\theta]\) is an \emph{instance}, not a second family; uniform
specializations never enter \(\mathsf{Sch}_2(H)\) directly. The boundary
inclusion induces typed weakening
\(\mathrm{wk}_x:\mathsf{Sch}_2(H)\to\mathsf{Sch}_2(I(H,x))\), and the marginal
families of \(x\) are
\(\operatorname{Marg}_2(H;x) :=
\mathsf{Sch}_2(I(H,x))\setminus\operatorname{im}(\mathrm{wk}_x)\).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[The semantic-family register]
\label{def:semantic-register}
The \emph{semantic-family value} of a candidate,
\[
\nu_H(x) \;=\; \bigl|\operatorname{Marg}_2(H;x)\bigr|,
\]
is lawful only under an \emph{act-local, anchored} audit:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, label=\textbf{SR\arabic*.}]
\item \textbf{Act-locality (transportability).} Every counted family is
derived from the candidate and its sealed prefix alone, through the adopted
family, orbit, and transport machinery. The derivation must replay
identically on any branch containing the same act over the same prefix.
Joins against archived history certificates are regression comparators,
never authority; a count whose derivation consumes a recorded total, or any
outcome of the enacted future, is void.
\item \textbf{Anchoring (provenance injection).} The audit carries an
equivalence-invariant injection of \(\operatorname{Marg}_2(H;x)\) into the
provenance-tag type
\(\bigl(\mathsf K_H(x)\times\mathcal R_2\bigr)\sqcup
\coprod_{o\in\mathsf{LiveOrb}(H)}\mathsf{Req}_H(o)\),
where \(\mathsf K_H(x)\) is the kernel-clause set,
\(\mathcal R_2=\{\mathsf{KernelHead},\mathsf{AdjointMate},
\mathsf{SupportAction},\mathsf{Coherence}\}\) is the four-role local code, and
the demand summand ranges over pre-existing live orbits --- together with a
dependent witness that every tag is an actual typed anchor (first irreducible
fresh source with proved role, or typed discharge of the named demand
output). String labels, cardinal slots, and package membership are not
anchors.
\item \textbf{Blindness.} The role grammar, orbit equivalence, and injection
are stage-generic and consult no bar, stage index, desired winner, accepted
trace, or survivor list; they commute with substitution, weakening, and
univalent equivalence. Without this certificate \(\nu_H(x)\) is undefined and
\(x\) cannot be valued.
\end{enumerate}
The kernel cost \(\kappa_H(x) = |\mathsf K_H(x)|\) counts irreducible public
clauses, charged once, at first irreducibility; definitional completion,
aliases, and forced projections are free; \(\kappa_H(x)=0\) marks a
theorem-readout, not a candidate.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Provenance accounting bound]
\label{prop:provenance-bound}
Every certified candidate satisfies
\[
\nu_H(x) \;\le\; 4\,\kappa_H(x) \;+\;
\sum_{o\in\mathsf{LiveOrb}(H)}|\mathsf{Req}_H(o)|.
\]
On a debt-free field the demand summand is empty, giving
\(\nu_H(x)\le 4\kappa_H(x)\) and \(\rho_H(x)\le 4\) for the efficiency ratio
of Definition~\ref{def:diagnostics}. This bound is the open band's
foreclosing wall.
\end{proposition}

\section{The Selective Law: Two Registers}

\begin{definition}[Guarded and open stages]
Stage \(n+1\) is \emph{guarded} when \(O(n+1)\neq\varnothing\) and \emph{open}
when \(O(n+1)=\varnothing\).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Selective Law, guarded register]
\label{def:guarded-register}
At a guarded stage, acceptance is \emph{total discharge with typed
provenance}: a candidate \(x\) is acceptable iff \(\mathrm{CL}_{H_n}(x)\),
\(x\) totally discharges \(O(n+1)\) (every live orbit's required outputs
realized by typed discharge proofs), and its audit is certified per
Definition~\ref{def:semantic-register}. \emph{Value never legislates at
guarded stages}: no bar, threshold, efficiency comparison, or parsimony order
may select among acceptable candidates, and no deterministic key ---
enumeration, hash, or normalized-presentation order --- may break a tie. If
exactly one acceptable candidate exists, it is selected; this uniqueness is
an empirical property of the trace, certified stage by stage, never an axiom.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Explicit nondeterminism; the tie ladder]
\label{def:tie-ladder}
If several acceptable candidates at a guarded stage are genuinely distinct
--- distinctness surviving the full quotient (presentation equivalence,
natural-family transport, univalent equivalence) --- the law is silent and
history is a \emph{cone}: each acceptable act founds a lawful branch, none is
privileged, and the enacted branch is identified indexically (testimony, not
law). The machinery ascends a strict ladder before this conclusion is
reached: \emph{quotient first} (presentation-multiplicity is not a tie),
\emph{confluence second} (equivalent futures make the choice gauge),
\emph{adjudication last} --- and an adjudication may found or index, but its
grounds may cite no downstream score, no desired history length, and no
enumeration artifact. Choice is thereby secured against \emph{value}, not
against \emph{proof}: a certified equivalence collapsing apparent
alternatives destroys no choice, because none existed.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Selective Law, open register]
\label{def:open-register}
On the open band (\(O=\varnothing\)), acceptance requires \emph{certified
clearing}: an acceptance must carry a complete certified audit whose valued
novelty justifies extension. An unrankable candidate --- one whose required
evidence is not constructible --- cannot be accepted. By
Proposition~\ref{prop:provenance-bound} every certified debt-free candidate
has \(\rho\le 4\); the open band forecloses.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Halt at debt-freedom; cone-level]
\label{thm:halt}
The Genesis sequence ends where debt ends. On every branch of the lawful
cone: the demand ladder runs one live obligation per stage from the first
exported demand to stage 15; the completed library is debt-free
(\(O(16)=\varnothing\), proved at full instance granularity, \(89/89\)
instances of the stage-16 window decided); and the falsifier F1 --- a
demanded-but-underdetermined instance, the one refutation channel of the
fifteen-link claim --- has been executed and excluded. The Constitutive Law
alone forecloses continuation (no demand, no admissibility); the open-band
envelope \(\rho\le4\) stands as independent corroborating wall, not premise.
These claims are \emph{cone-level}: theorems of the laws, not of the enacted
choice.
\end{theorem}

\section{The Cone}\label{sec:cone}

\begin{definition}[The Stage-4 cone]
The first guarded stage of the certified trace (stage 4, demand
\(\mathsf{former\_eliminator}\)) admits exactly four acceptable acts,
genuinely distinct under the full quotient: a \(2\times2\) of former
\(\{\Pi,\Sigma\}\) against application order. This is the only multiplicity
in the lawful landscape --- every other guarded stage, on every branch, has a
unique acceptable discharger. The lawful world-structure is therefore a
four-branch cone rooted at stage 4.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Certified cone structure]
\label{prop:cone-structure}
The following are certificate-backed. (i) The \emph{order axis} is structure:
typed transport obstructions exist between the two application orders, and
the semantic register separates them
(\(\nu = 2\) at order one, \(\nu = 3\) at order two --- the enacted act is
nonminimal, a diagnostic fact with no legal weight). (ii) The \emph{former
axis} is value-invisible (\(\Pi\) and \(\Sigma\) tie at each order) and
successor-scheme-invisible at the deepest key; whether it is act-level gauge
is the one open identity question (the unattempted act-equivalence
\(B_4^{\Pi}\simeq B_4^{\Sigma}\)). (iii) \emph{Healing of acts}: winners on
all four branches are byte-identical from stage 8 to the halt, and identical
modulo relabeling of stage-4 references at stages 5--7; per-stage
\((\kappa,\nu)\) ledgers agree from stage 5 onward. (iv) \emph{Permanent
trace}: cumulative quantities (\(\sum\nu\), all cumulative bar trajectories)
and at least one live demand's scheme presentation differ across branches
forever. The fork is real in value, silent in consequence, and remembered
only by unbounded-memory description.
\end{proposition}

\paragraph{Claim quantifiers.}
Every law-level claim in this book is either \emph{cone-level} (quantified
over all four branches: the demand ladder, the halt, \(O(16)=\varnothing\),
F1 exclusion, per-stage ledgers from stage 5) or \emph{branch-indexed}
(naming its branch: the stage-4 act and its value, cumulative sums, bar
trajectories, windowed tables, provenance identities). Silence is not a
disposition; unindexed cumulative claims are invalid.

inheritance, or combinatorial multiplier mints value without a distinct paid
