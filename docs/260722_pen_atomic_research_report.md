## 1. The program

The two-law theory proposes that a world-library is built by exactly
two principles: a **generative law** — nothing without demand; every
step totally discharges the live obligation profile of its window —
and a **selective law**, whose correct formulation is the central
revision this report describes. The Genesis sequence of fifteen steps,
constructed in a cubical type-theoretic kernel (`--safe --without-K`
discipline, Agda-mirrored where required) and enacted by a Rust engine,
is the theory's object: fifteen sealed acts running from a
pre-structural bootstrap through sphere and HIT strata (steps 5–8)
into bundle, connection, curvature, operator, Hilbert, and temporal
shells (9–15), halting at a debt-free field where a sixteenth step is
refused.

The method is as much a finding as the content. Every rule is adopted
through a versioned adjudication, signed verbatim, with falsifiers
registered before results exist; artifacts are create-new and
replay-certified with mutation batteries; sealed history is never
edited ("fix the machinery, never the history"); and no rule or motive
may be justified by a desired outcome — verdict-blindness is enforced
by falsifiers that void adoptions citing downstream scores. The
adjudication ledger stands at **twenty-three signed items**. Three
hypotheses (SH-1, EGP v1/v2) have been burned by their own
pre-registered criteria and were not retuned; forecasts were formally
retired from the record when one failed.

## 3. The two-law revision, as it now stands

The current best formulation — proposed, deliberately unsigned until
its prerequisite theorems replay — is the **demand-parsimony law**:

1. *Nothing without demand.* Every step totally discharges the live
   obligation profile of its window (unchanged).
2. *Nothing beyond demand.* Among admissible dischargers, the minimal
   one under the parsimony order: least κ, then least semantic-family
   ν. Comparative only — no threshold, no bar, no distance to
   anything.
3. *Constitutive bootstrap.* Stages whose window exports no demand are
   arena-founding acts, registered as such; the demand chain begins
   with the first exported obligation.
4. *Halt at debt-freedom.* The sequence ends where nothing further is
   owed. On the debt-free field the generative law alone forecloses
   continuation: no demand, no admissibility.
5. *The bar as physics, not law.* The Fibonacci ledger, Ω, and Bar_n
   are descriptive dynamics of the interface — real, recorded, gating
   nothing. Under WB-1's conjecture they are the shadow of the width-2
   demand window, with φ its fixed point.

To this the year's adjudications have added two structural amendments
the original formulation lacked. First, **the cone**: the laws
determine the world up to a four-fold choice at Stage 4; determinism
is recovered only if the branch-invariance program proves the cone

entirely if UC-1 proves the branching is gauge. Second, **the
register**: the ν the selective law reads must be act-local and

The revision's empirical content has relocated accordingly. The claim

## 4. Philosophical implications

**Constructive idealism, operationalized.** The program's working
metaphysics — reality as constructive, syntactic thinking — has
acquired precise correlates. The future-hole definition makes
anticipation a typed object: what does not yet exist is present as a
hypothesis with a declared type, and becoming is instantiation. The
halt theorem makes finitude internal: the world ends not by external
decree but by running out of obligation. And the register split makes
a sharp idealist claim about value: what an act is worth is what can
be proved of the act alone — worth is intrinsic, never conferred by
the archive that happens to record it.

**Authority migrates from happening to proof.** The program's
jurisprudence, read as a whole, is one long transfer: the sealed
enactment was demoted to testimony (the semantic audit governs); the
bar was demoted to diagnostic (debt governs); the archived totals were
demoted to structural testimony (act-local proof governs). Each
demotion was forced by a machine refusing to certify something, never
chosen for elegance. The residue is a theory in which *that something
happened* is never a ground — only *that it can be re-derived* is.

**One free moment, and its self-referential resolution.** The laws are
silent exactly once: four acts, identical in cost and value, genuinely
distinct, at the first commanded step. The adjudication of that
silence could not itself be lawful in the theory's sense — and the
choice made (explicit non-determinism over a founding decree) was made
on parsimony grounds: declining to add a legislator the laws do not
contain. The selective law — nothing beyond demand — was applied to
the theory itself. Whether that moment is real freedom (four worlds),
consequence-free freedom (four acts, one future), or no freedom at all
(one act, four presentations) is precisely the open experimental
question (BI / UC-1), and each answer is philosophically distinct:
branching structure, forgetful structure, or gauge.

**Honest limits.** Everything above is internal to a formal system:
certified consequences of frozen definitions. The theory has not yet
produced a parameter-free number that the measured world could veto,
and its stage names (curvature, Hilbert, temporal) are aspirations
attached to structures, not derivations tied to observation. By the
program's own deepest principle — only the falsifier speaks — the
claim of physical fundamentality has no standing until external
falsifiers exist. The internal discipline is the asset that makes that
next step meaningful.
