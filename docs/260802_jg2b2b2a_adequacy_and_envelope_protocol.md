## 1. Why this correction was necessary

The P0 audit correctly found that nine sort names and seven nominal arrows do
not determine an indexed calculus. The supplied depth-two sources sharpen that
finding. In the canonical cubical model, binary comparison is compatibility
along an exported path or equivalence. It is not merely equality of two kernel
normal forms. Higher structural coherence remains present in exact obligation
objects and becomes derived only when a checked open-box computation supplies
the missing face and filler from the public lower boundary.

The present `pen-kernel` cannot state that theorem. Its closed twelve-form term
grammar has no interval, face formulas, path types, path abstraction or
application, equivalences, univalence, `transp`, `hcomp`, or `hfill`. Its open
judgments are formation, typing, and normalization-based definitional equality.
The JG2b2b0 occurrence grammar and JG2b2b1 substitution substrate exhaustively
mirror that ordinary term grammar. They are exact ordinary-syntax anchors, not
a cubical adequacy bridge.

Accordingly, the former idea of a single `minimal-kernel-cwf-v1` realization
with a definitional-equality-only `ComparisonWitness` is rejected as a model
of the book's canonical cubical claim. It may still define a deliberately
restricted ordinary dependent-calculus profile, but it may not inherit the
depth-two theorem or be described as a CCHM realization.

```text
ordinary kernel declaration and later history-derived occurrence
```

Here `P` is an opaque, pre-exposure, verifier-minted indexed-ontology profile;

## 4. Frozen `EnvelopeProtocolV1<P>` definition

For verified complete-through-head history `H`, an internally derived exact
structural occurrence `o`, and constructor `c` from the closed ordered JG2a
vocabulary, a completed profile `P` defines

```text
Cand_P(H,o,c)
  = { nf_P(E) |
        Derive_(P,c)(H,E) and Anchor_(P,c)(H,E,o) }
      / approx^(env)_(P,c).
```

`Derive_(P,c)` is the constructor-specific checked realization judgment,
`Anchor_(P,c)` is the relation of section 4.5, `nf_P` is canonical
normalization, and `approx^(env)_(P,c)` is the frozen whole-envelope
presentation relation. Carrier-specific equality remains separate. The common
protocol does not infer these operations from Rust types. A carrier/action
substrate may be frozen before its constructor layer, but it is not a
completed or selectable `P` until every `Derive_(P,c)` and anchor selector is
closed and the profile transcript binds them.

Every completed profile's whole-envelope relation must preserve, in its
canonical quotient key, all normalized carrier/index/output and implementation
realizations, complete principal and nonprincipal field lineage, the separate
implementation/current-output/prior-support footprints, the full structural-
support projection and coverage data, the complete primitive-or-derived
disposition vector, every obligation subject, and the complete
`SubjectSyntaxV1` together with its byte-identical
`SubjectRootProjectionV1`. Subject literals are restricted to typed core,
carrier, occurrence, formal-action-argument, and pure support-function
projections. No subject literal may contain a full envelope, support wrapper,
quotient or final key, action trace, action image, pair transcript, or
disposition. A presentation witness must transport every retained projection.
Equality of carrier normal forms alone is never sufficient to identify
envelopes.

A profile may additionally define a canonical **action image** solely as the
codomain of its generic reindexing-law subjects. Such an image has the same
normalized quotient-key shape and selector lineage as an envelope, but is not
raw-code enumerated, need not satisfy `Derive_(P,c)`, and is never a member of
`Cand_P` or a source of a pair disposition. Its support projection is
recomputed from the final normalized action. Formal binder or subject-variable
dependencies introduced by the action remain formal support and may not be
relabeled as history occurrences or paid support. Every concrete
`Term::Global(g)` in a bounded action must check under `Sigma(H,o)` and resolve
to an exact current or older owner; ordinary V1 has no `ExternalGlobal`
fallback. The profile's `sel`, `nf`, and whole-envelope presentation relation
may extend to these non-authoritative action images only so that identity,
composition, and naturality propositions are well typed. This extension does
not turn a finite candidate grammar into a complete substitution universe.

The classifier disposition is determined only after complete finite
enumeration of `Cand_P(H,o,c)`:

```text
|Cand| = 0  -> CertifiedNoMatch
|Cand| = 1  -> UniquePositive
|Cand| > 1  -> TypedAmbiguity.
```

Malformed parents, an outside-fragment object, incomplete enumeration,
arithmetic or allocation failure, normalization failure, or resource
exhaustion yields no disposition. It is never converted to the zero-class
case.

### 4.4 Primitive and derived evidence

Every normalized public field receives exactly one of:

```text
Derived(ReplacementEvidence)
Primitive(CompleteNoReplacementCertificate).
```

`ReplacementEvidence` retains the lower public boundary, replacement
term/recipe, typing and boundary equations, semantic preservation, allowed-
dependency proof, substitution-stability subject/evidence, and public-
normalization compatibility.

`CompleteNoReplacementCertificate` is permitted only when `P` freezes a
finite decidable transparent-derivation grammar and proves the negative search
complete. Failure to find a replacement before a resource limit is not
primitive evidence. A profile without such a decision theorem returns outside
fragment or no disposition instead.

### 4.5 Anchor relation

`Anchor_(P,c)(H,E,o)` holds exactly when:

1. every footprint identity is freshly and exactly replayed from `H`;
2. `o` belongs to the current-birth/output footprint;
3. `Derive_(P,c)` checks the complete constructor realization using that
   footprint;
4. the profile-and-schema selector `sel_(P,c)` applied to `nf_P(E)` is `o`;
5. the selector is unique on that envelope, and the exact normalization,
   whole-envelope-presentation, admissible-renaming, and reindexing stability
   propositions are retained as formal theorem subjects.

Each constructor schema must designate one principal normalized
output-realization root *position* before any history is read. Checked replay
derives a provenance map from that position to exact current-birth occurrence
identities. `sel_(P,c)` is defined only when that provenance is a singleton;
zero or multiple origins makes the proposal a non-match, while incomplete
replay yields no disposition. A definition-closed profile must state the
selector totality/stability subjects exactly. Their universal proofs belong to
the later law-verified profile authority and are required before a factual
positive row may be admitted; A4-O definition closure alone does not prove
them. The common protocol does not choose the
lexicographically first node: that shortcut is presentation-sensitive for
bundled or multi-declaration implementations.

## 5. Comparison and depth policy

The following distinctions are frozen for every successor profile:

1. structural identity, kernel definitional equality, a public path or
   equivalence, and a comparison/naturality witness are distinct;
2. `ComparisonWitness` must retain its exact endpoints and the comparison
   route or path on which it depends;
3. a profile claiming the cubical lower bound must state a subject of the form

   ```text
   transp_Out(p, action_A(x))
     = action_B(transp_In(p,x)),
   ```

   or an exactly specified equivalent square;
4. only a proof that a map is an equivalence may be propositionally
   irrelevant where the selected calculus proves that fact; the equivalence,
   path, endpoints, and comparison remain data; and
5. exact higher coherence is retained by default. Arity-three-and-higher
   fields may be removed from a primitive public signature only with the full
   checked replacement evidence of section 4.4 and an applicable adequacy
   theorem.

Thus `depth two` supplies no license to truncate contexts, discard old opaque
exports, erase higher payload, or call unproved higher coherence derived.

## 6. Demand-side correction

The common profile requirements distinguish positive activation from present
derivability.

- `SealedPublicGrammar(Gamma)` is a finite normalized opaque export telescope
  plus its public operational fragment. It contains no complete history,
  stage labels, productivity profile, or held-out fact.
- `DemandScheme(Gamma,G)` is a finite normalized dependency-ordered family of
  typed ports, public anchors, parameters, and equation/coherence contracts.
- `LiveDemand(Gamma,G,S,a)` is the complete positive, deterministically
  specialized activation of `S` in `G`. It contains all dependency-ordered
  demanded entries. It does not assert absence or underivability.
- `Discharge(Gamma,G,S,L)` is one complete dependent section covering all
  entries of activation `L`, including shared dependencies and equation
  checks. Entry and entry-discharge projections may be auxiliary views only;
  neither is a `LiveDemand` or a total `Discharge`.

The factual derivability result is separate:

```text
Derivable(witness)
Underivable(complete negative certificate)
Unknown.
```

Positive activations and checked discharge witnesses reindex under the
profile's typed substitution action. Underivability is not required to reindex: a
specialization can make a previously underivable demand derivable. `Unknown`
is neither debt nor completion and prevents any complete ledger claim.
