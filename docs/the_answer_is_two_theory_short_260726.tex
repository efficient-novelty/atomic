\begin{abstract}
The theory states that every determinate, history-bearing world requires exactly two primitive explanatory roles. The Constitutive Law determines admissible coherent structure; the revised Selective Law governs selection, exact realization, and entry into shared public history. Its formal face is two-register acceptance: at guarded stages (nonempty windowed obligation profile), acceptance is total discharge of the live demands with typed receipts, and no value quantity legislates; on the debt-free open band, acceptance requires certified earned novelty, which a provenance ceiling (\(\nu\le4\kappa\)) forecloses. The Principle of Efficient Novelty survives as the law's evidentiary standard --- novelty counts only when anchored to paid clauses or discharged demands --- and as the open band's foreclosing wall, never as a guarded-stage selector; where genuinely distinct total payments coexist, the law's silence is certified rather than broken, and history is lawfully a cone. Its realization face is free sealing: once an API is selected, the new layer is its least term-model completion, containing no unforced generators or equations and initial among exact realizations of that selected API. This relative initiality must not be confused with the false stronger claim that PEN always selects the initial completion of a weaker requirement fixed in advance. Its physical face is Internal Record Actualization plus Public Confluence: a formal seal becomes a public event only through a stable decodable internal record, and compatible public records must admit a joint nondemolition refinement; finite-capacity failure is ``common seal'' or ``no common seal,'' never contradictory sealed facts on one overlap. The Two Laws do not determine a universal public-event extractor \(\mathcal Q_p\), tolerance, metric clock calibration, or resource entropies. They do, however, support a complete model-level bridge: in an explicit seven-cell thermal repetition observer, the dynamics uniquely forces Hamming weight as the minimal sufficient statistic for the retained event claim, the optimal no-abstention seal has error \(521/15625\), all public coarse-grainings glue by exact nondemolition refinement, and the measured second-Renyi resources are \(n_p=4.887399\) bits and \(n_g=3.894753\) bits, giving \(K_{\rm cap}=3.307335\) bits. This closes the immediate finite-observer construction problem without converting an observer-specific dynamics into a universal law. The exact obligation-depth package gives \(d_{\mathrm{obl}}=2\), \(d_\mu\le2\), a two-layer chronological window, affine Fibonacci scaling (descriptive interface arithmetic, gating nothing), and the certified fifteen-step Genesis Sequence ending in a temporal-cohesive shell: a demand chain with exactly one live obligation per stage from 4 through 15, a machine-certified unique total discharger at every guarded stage except Step 4 --- where four genuinely distinct payments survive the full quotient, making lawful history a four-branch cone whose futures heal (per-stage ledgers identical from Step 5, structures byte-identical from Step 8, only cumulative diagnostics permanently branch-indexed) --- and a certified debt-free halt: the Step-16 obligation ledger is empty at full instance granularity on every branch, the demanded-but-underdetermined falsifier is executed and excluded on every branch, and the halt is therefore a theorem of the laws rather than of the enacted choice. Value bookkeeping is register-disciplined: the enacted engine's structural totals are sealed testimony, and all law-level counts are act-local, proof-bearing semantic-family values. A reparameterization no-go shows that the structural ledger does not calibrate this runtime to seconds or redshift. A second underdetermination result is equally important: constructive admissibility, composition, Genesis availability, free sealing, and Public Confluence do not uniquely select complex Born quantum theory. Classical simplices, real and quaternionic quantum fragments, and generalized probabilistic models survive appropriate subsets of these requirements. The desired endpoint is therefore a reconstruction theorem deriving, rather than postulating, spectral dagger/filter completeness, a primitive central monoidal phase, free environment completion, and public-interface descent; these would respectively target homogeneous self-dual/Jordan structure, complex phase-balanced composition, purification, and gravity-compatible tomography. The framework is consequently strongest as a meta-kinematic and architectural theory: modern physics can be represented naturally inside it, but necessity and sufficiency for the unique modern kinematic framework remain the central open theorem.

\section{Formal Setting and Primitive Roles}

\begin{definition}[History and continuation]
A realized history is a cumulative state \(H\). A candidate continuation at \(H\) is an object \(x\) equipped with an intended update map
\[
I(H,x)=H'.
\]
The admissible cone \(\A(H)\) is the class of continuations coherent with both the Constitutive Law and the already realized history.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Admissible cone as a dependent fiber]
Let \(\Hist\) be the class of realized histories and let \(\Cand(H)\) be the raw candidate family over \(H\). The constitutive predicate is
\[
\CL_H:\Cand(H)\to\mathsf{Prop},
\]
and the admissible cone is the dependent subfamily
\[
\A(H):=\sum_{x:\Cand(H)}\CL_H(x).
\]
The integration map is partial on raw candidates and total on admissible selected candidates:
\[
\I_H:\A(H)\to\Hist.
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Determinate world]
A determinate world is a sequence
\[
H_0 \xrightarrow{x_0} H_1 \xrightarrow{x_1} H_2 \to \cdots,
\qquad
H_{n+1}=I(H_n,x_n),
\]
where \(x_n\in\A(H_n)\) is selected and integrated at each stage.
\end{definition}

\begin{principle}[Two primitive roles]
The primitive architecture consists of:
\[
\begin{aligned}
\CL &: \text{what coherently counts as admissible},\\
\SLaw &: \text{what becomes actual and how it is integrated}.
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{principle}

\begin{theorem}[Two-role exhaustiveness]
Every primitive explanatory role in a determinate world is classified either under admissibility or under actualization and integration.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proofsketch}
If only admissibility is given, there is a class of possible structures but no selected branch. If only actualization is given, there is no coherent candidate domain over which actualization can operate. A proposed third primitive role either constrains whether \(x\in\A(H)\), hence belongs to \(\CL\), or constrains whether and how \(x\) is selected and integrated, hence belongs to \(\SLaw\). No further primitive explanatory type is generated.
\end{proofsketch}

\begin{proposition}[Primitive law factorization]
Any primitive law \(L\) on determinate histories factors extensionally as
\[
L_H=(C_H,S_H),
\]
where \(C_H\subseteq\Cand(H)\) is its admissibility component and
\[
S_H:C_H\rightharpoonup \Hist
\]
is its actualization/integration component.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proofsketch}
The observable effect of \(L\) at \(H\) is exhausted by which raw candidates remain live and which live candidate is sealed into the next history. The first datum is the characteristic predicate of \(C_H\). The second datum is the partial next-history map \(S_H\). Any additional clause in \(L\) either changes \(C_H\), changes \(S_H\), or is inert with respect to determinate history.
\end{proofsketch}

\section{Constitutive Law}

\begin{principle}[Constitutive Law]
An admissible candidate is a coherently realizable, equivalence-invariant, locally satisfiable, canonically executable structure.
\end{principle}

\begin{definition}[Constitutive burdens]
The four constitutive burdens are:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item coherent realizability: existence is witness-bearing;
\item equivalence-invariance: equivalent presentations are identical in being;
\item local satisfiability: coherence obligations are discharged at bounded depth;
\item canonicity: closed constructions have determinate realization.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Constitutive judgment]
For history \(H\) and raw candidate \(x:\Cand(H)\), the judgment
\[
H\vdash_{\CL} x\ \mathsf{adm}
\]
means that \(x\) carries:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\mathrm{CL1} & \text{a witness-bearing realization type } \Real_H(x),\\
\mathrm{CL2} & \text{transport along all structural equivalences in its public interface},\\
\mathrm{CL3} & \text{a finite local family of coherence obligations } O_H(x),\\
\mathrm{CL4} & \text{canonical evaluation of closed generated terms.}
\end{array}
\]
Thus \(\CL_H(x)\) is the conjunction of CL1--CL4.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Formal shadow of \(\CL\)]
The canonical formal shadow of the Constitutive Law is a CCHM-style cubical homotopy type theory with dependent types, univalence, Kan composition, and computational canonicity.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proofsketch}
Witness-bearing existence is represented by judgments \(\Gamma\vdash a:A\). Equivalence-invariance is represented by univalence,
\[
(A\simeq B)\simeq (A=_{\U}B).
\]
Bounded local satisfiability is represented by structural integration obligations over finite public interfaces. Canonicity with univalence requires computational path constructors and filler operations; cubical Kan operations supply the required execution rules.
\end{proofsketch}

\begin{proposition}[Constructivity, univalence, and cubical canonicity]
CL1 forces constructive witnesses; CL2 forces identity to respect equivalence; CL4 forces the chosen univalent calculus to compute closed canonical forms.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proofsketch}
A witness-free existence predicate would make \(H\vdash_{\CL}x\ \mathsf{adm}\) insensitive to realization and would not supply data for integration. A presentation-sensitive identity predicate would distinguish equivalent public structures, violating CL2. Book-style univalence without computational path and filler rules supplies equivalence-invariance but not the canonical execution demanded by CL4. CCHM-style cubical structure supplies paths, transport, composition, and normalization in one formal shadow.
\end{proofsketch}

\section{Selective Law and PEN}

\begin{definition}[Selective burdens]
The revised Selective Law carries ten burdens: localizability, commitment, admissibility, valuation, critical sufficiency, constructive primeness, exact realization, coherent integration, internal recordability, and public confluence.
\end{definition}

\begin{principle}[Selective Law]
The Selective Law operates in two registers. At guarded stages --- whenever the live window exports unmet obligations --- the accepted continuation is the admissible total discharger of those obligations, with typed provenance; value quantities are audited but select nothing, and where genuinely distinct total dischargers coexist after the canonical quotient, the law's silence is certified and history is lawfully a cone. The selected API is realized by its free sealing completion and folded into further history without unforced generators or equations.
\end{principle}

\begin{principle}[Strengthened physical Selective Law]
The Selective Law has three interfaces that must not be conflated. Two-register acceptance fixes which admissible API is sealed (obligation at guarded stages, certified earning on the open band, with PEN as the audit standard). Free sealing realizes the chosen API. A formally integrated continuation becomes a \emph{public physical event for an internal context \(p\)} only when it is actualized as a stable, decodable record inside that context. Compatible public records on an overlap must admit a joint nondemolition refinement; incompatible records yield no common seal, not two contradictory sealed facts. Selection without such a record is structural evolution, not yet a public event for \(p\).
\end{principle}

This strengthening does not add a third primitive role. Free realization, record formation, and public confluence constrain how a selected admissible continuation becomes determinate history and therefore belong to the actualization/integration side of \(\SLaw\). They do, however, expose structure that the value audit alone does not contain.

\begin{definition}[Definitional derivability and irreducible clause]

\]
for a canonical value \(v_c\), with all required computation behavior inherited from the existing eliminator fragment. A clause \(c\) is constructively irreducible over \(H\) when every attempted realization of \(c\) contains a new neutral head or stuck eliminator relative to \(\rightsquigarrow_H\), equivalently when orienting the intended behavior requires adding a new operational, equation, or API clause.
\end{definition}

\mathrm{Unity} & \text{the clauses form one structural bundle},\\
\mathrm{Min} & \text{no proper structural sub-bundle is complete and independently acceptable.}
\end{array}
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Irreducible integration cost]

\[
\kappa_H(x):=
\left|\mathrm{Irr}_H(\mathcal{S}_x)\right|,
\]
where \(\mathrm{Irr}_H(\mathcal{S}_x)\) is the set of constructively irreducible atomic clauses in a minimal complete specification for \(x\). Definitionally derivable clauses contribute \(0\). Nonzero admissible candidates satisfy \(\kappa_H(x)>0\).

\end{definition}

The sealing latency of \(x\) at \(H\) is
\[
\Delta_H(x):=|\mathcal{G}_x|.
\]
It counts the normalized atomic coherence witnesses required to integrate \(x\) against the active historical interface. Thus \(\kappa_H(x)\) charges irreducible specification clauses, while \(\Delta_H(x)\) charges the interface debt incurred by sealing those clauses into public history.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Generative gain]

\left|

\langle \Schemas(\B_H)\rangle_H
\right|,
\]
where \(\B_H[x]\) is the library obtained by adding the specification of \(x\), and \(\langle \Schemas(\B_H)\rangle_H\) is the closure of inherited schemas under definitional derivability in the old library.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Components of generative gain]

\[

\begin{theorem}[Depth-two theorem package]

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]

\item \(d_\mu\le 2\);

\item under uniform payload size \(c\), the presentation-invariant sealing cost satisfies
\[

\end{enumerate}

Thus the exact obligation depth is two and the public chronological window is two.
\end{proofsketch}
