1. **Declaration.** Every A3 `ChronologicalInteraction` scheme carries
   a declared interface-to-parameter map as part of its registration —
   enumerated, never inferred, never selected by verdict (wrapper
   discipline, unchanged).
2. **The canonical map.** The interface slots of a
   `ChronologicalInteraction` are ordered by sealing time (older
   before newest); the scheme's dependent context is ordered by
   declaration (sequential substitution, left to right). The declared
   map is the **order-preserving identity**: i-th interface slot to
   i-th parameter, at every arity. Grounds: both orders are already
   law (sealing order is the spine of the telescope; declaration order
   is the adopted substitution order); the identity is the unique map

3. **No per-instance freedom.** The map is a property of the rule, not

4. **Charging.** Interface slots are hypotheses under the future-hole
