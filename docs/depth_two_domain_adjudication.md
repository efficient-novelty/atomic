## Rule: `depth-two-semantic-domain-v1`

1. **The domain.** The depth-two semantic domain over a history \(H\)
   is \(\mathsf{NfSch}_2(H)\): the typed normal schema judgments of
   the fixed depth-two closure fragment of the ambient calculus,
   defined by the frozen kernel typing relation, the terminating typed
   normalizer, and the adopted equality (judgmental equality plus
   univalent transport of normalized public signatures, preserving
   conclusions and all substitution and naturality squares), quotiented
   by the adopted natural-family/instance law. The domain is defined
   by *what the kernel accepts and the normalizer identifies* — no
   generator inventory, seed list, enumeration order, or issuer output
   appears anywhere in its definition.
2. **Independence discipline.** The domain's implementation may share
   the kernel, the normalizer, and the equality procedure with the
   rest of the engine — those are adopted, generator-free machinery.
   It may not import, consult, or be seeded by any generator
   enumeration, A3 inventory, or bridge row set. Membership in the
   domain is decided by typing and normalization alone.
3. **Completeness restated (noncircular).** Depth-two
   generator-completeness (BC1's target) is the theorem that the
   generator set is *surjective onto* \(\mathsf{NfSch}_2(H)\): every
   domain inhabitant is reached by some generator derivation, with the
   family quotient respected in both directions. The depth-one
   restriction theorem is the same statement relativized to the
   depth-one subfragment, and must re-derive the sealed depth-one
   campaign as a corollary, not by citation.
4. **Boundedness obligation.** The definition is operative only with a
   replayable proof that \(\mathsf{NfSch}_2(H)\) is finitely
   enumerable up to the adopted equality for the sealed histories in
   scope — decidability of membership and finiteness of the quotient
   are theorem obligations, not assumptions. If the fragment is not
   finitely presentable by the frozen machinery, that is a named gap
   and a lawful stop, not a license to re-introduce generators as a
   proxy.
5. **Zero charge.** The domain is description, not construction:
   defining, enumerating, or normalizing domain inhabitants mints no
   \(\kappa\), no \(\nu\), and no anchors.

**Grounds (all dated, none outcome-shaped).** (i) The typed
operational schema audit and the family/instance law (adopted): the
domain is exactly what those rules already quantify over — this
adjudication names their range as a first-class object. (ii) The
frozen equality and normalizer (adopted, replay-bound): the domain's
deciding machinery exists and is generator-free. (iii) The register
jurisprudence: authority that certifies itself is not authority (the
provenance-circularity finding); a completeness theorem needs a target
its subject did not manufacture. (iv) The BC1 stop as occasion, never
ground.

## Falsifiers

  enumeration, seed inventory, or bridge row set reaches the domain's
  membership decision → the domain implementation is void; the stop
  stands.
- **F-D2-2 (regression).** T-D2-1's regression failing on the 61
  families or 89 rows → machinery fault; fix the machinery, never the
  definition, and never the certified corpus.
- **F-D2-3 (proxy re-entry).** Clause-4 failure answered by any
  generator-derived surrogate domain → invalid; a named gap is the
  only lawful outcome.
- **F-D2-4 (key shopping).** Any key-schema choice in T-D2-3 made or
  revised with knowledge of join outcomes → invalid (F-BC2-lineage of
  the 103-temptation ban).
- **F-D2-5 (scope).** This adjudication opens no gate: M-3 v1 remains
  authoritative, `m4_authorized` remains false, and only a rerun
